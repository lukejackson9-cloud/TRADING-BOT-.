## Catalyst
Skyworks rose +9.79% on 2026-09-10 on news that its ~$22B merger with
Qorvo is progressing toward close: Skyworks has now cleared US antitrust
review (the FTC allowed its timing agreement to expire without further
action), and "all required foreign investment approvals" have reportedly
been obtained, leaving China's SAMR and South Korea's KFTC as the last
pending competition-authority reviews. Management has reiterated hoping
to close before year-end 2026. This is a real, dated, company-specific
event (regulatory-approval progress on a previously-announced,
market-moving deal), not a rumor.

Two caveats on "how resolved" this actually is:
1. China's SAMR review is reported elsewhere (thecapitolforum.com) as
   likely heading into a more extensive Phase 3 review — i.e., the
   single biggest remaining regulatory hurdle is not yet cleared, only
   narrowed. The +9.79% pop reflects removal of the US leg of
   uncertainty, not full deal certainty.
2. Skyworks' own Q3 FY26 earnings (reported ~2026-07-28, well before this
   move) were solid on their own (revenue/EPS beat, Q4 guide of
   $1.01-1.06B revenue) but that print is over a month stale relative to
   this session — it's supportive background, not this week's driver.

## Risks
- **Classic "sell the news" / already-fired-reaction risk (lessons.md
  #1)**: the +9.79% pop already happened on 2026-09-10; researching and
  potentially acting on it today (09-11) means entering after the
  reaction, the exact pattern this project's own evidence says tends to
  be a worse entry, not a better one.
- **Deal is not actually closed**: the real remaining risk (China SAMR,
  reportedly heading toward a deeper Phase 3 review, plus South Korea's
  KFTC) is still open. A stumble or extended timeline in China could
  reverse a meaningful chunk of this move — this is a regulatory-approval
  re-rate, not a fundamental re-rate, and regulatory outcomes are
  binary/event-risk in nature.
- M&A-arb-style moves like this are sensitive to deal-specific news flow
  (leaks, regulator statements) more than to broader technicals — a
  genuinely different risk profile than a typical momentum/catalyst
  trade this project is set up to evaluate.
- No new company-specific operating news (no fresh earnings/guidance
  print) — the fundamental Q3 beat is over a month old and not this
  session's driver, so this is a single-issue (deal progress) story, not
  a broad-based improvement in the business.
