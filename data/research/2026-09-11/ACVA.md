# ACVA — 2026-09-11

## Catalyst
Confirmed, real, dated, company-specific catalyst: Copart (CPRT) has
signed a definitive agreement to acquire ACV Auctions for **$10.50/share
in cash** (~$1.9B implied equity value), announced 2026-09-10/09-11.
This is corroborated by Copart's own SEC Form 8-K exhibit, Bloomberg
(which had reported the deal as "near agreement" the prior day), and
multiple financial-media write-ups (Yahoo Finance, dealershipguy.com),
all describing the same price and structure. The offer represents a
~45% premium to ACV's unaffected 08-10-2026 close and ~41% premium to
its 30-day VWAP through 09-09. Mechanically it's a tender offer
(Copart's subsidiary will tender for shares, conditioned on majority
tender + HSR antitrust clearance) followed by a back-end merger for any
untendered shares at the same $10.50 cash price. This is about as solid
a "real explanation" as a +44% one-day mover gets — no ambiguity here
about whether the move is justified.

## Step 0 (journal/lessons check)
grep of trade_ledger.md for "ACVA"/"ACV Auctions" returned no prior
entries. No directly relevant lessons.md pattern for M&A-arb specifically,
but lesson #1 (buying right after a big catalyst-driven gap is often a
worse entry, not better, because the reaction has already fired) is
relevant in spirit: once a definitive cash acquisition price is public,
the stock re-rates toward that fixed number almost immediately, and the
move described here (+44.2%) is very plausibly already most or all of
the way to fair value under the deal terms rather than a move with
further momentum left to capture.

## Sentiment
Unambiguously positive on the announcement (a confirmed buyout at a
large premium), but this not a "sentiment" story in the usual
momentum/catalyst sense — it's binary deal-completion economics. Once a
cash deal is announced, the market typically prices the target close to
(but slightly under) the offer price, with the residual gap reflecting
time-to-close and deal-break risk, not any further re-rating.

## Risks
- **Upside is structurally capped**: shares should trade at or below
  $10.50 (minus a discount for deal risk/time value) from here — this is
  not a stock with open-ended short-term upside the way an organic
  catalyst (earnings beat, guidance raise) can be.
- **Binary deal-break risk**: consummation depends on majority tender and
  HSR antitrust clearance — either could slip or fail, which would send
  the stock back toward its pre-deal level, not just stall the gain.
  This risk plays out over weeks-to-months, not this project's 2-week
  short-term horizon, and doesn't fit a stop-loss/take-profit momentum
  framework at all.
- This does not fit the project's momentum + news-catalyst Strategy
  (CLAUDE.md): there's no continuation trade here, just an arbitrage
  spread with regulatory/timing risk — a fundamentally different trade
  type than what this pipeline is built to evaluate or size.
- Q2 2026 results (reported prior to the deal) actually missed on EPS
  (-$0.05 vs -$0.03 est.), underscoring that the move is 100% deal-driven,
  not fundamentals-driven.
