# COO — 2026-09-11

## Catalyst
Confirmed, dated, company-specific: Cooper Companies (CooperVision +
CooperSurgical) reported fiscal Q3 2026 results on 2026-09-09/09-10,
which drove the -14.67% move on 09-10. This is a real, documented
earnings/strategic-review catalyst, but it is bearish, not bullish:
- Non-GAAP EPS of $1.15 (up 4% YoY) beat the ~$1.11 consensus — the
  headline beat, but not what moved the stock.
- **Revenue missed**: $1.066-1.07B reported vs. ~$1.10B consensus,
  driven specifically by CooperVision — management explicitly cited
  "proactive U.S. channel inventory reductions" at CooperVision as the
  driver, and said the drag will continue into next quarter (i.e. this
  is not a one-quarter blip per the company's own words).
- **Full-year guidance cut**: adjusted EPS guidance lowered to
  $4.51-$4.55 from a prior $4.58-$4.66.
- **Strategic review concluded against a sale**: the board completed its
  strategic review of CooperSurgical (the fertility/women's-health unit)
  and voted to retain it rather than sell — a meaningful chunk of the
  investor base was reportedly positioned for a divestiture, so this
  compounded the disappointment.
- One non-recurring positive noted but not enough to offset the above: a
  $307M discrete tax benefit from a favorable HMRC (UK tax authority)
  ruling, plus record quarterly free cash flow of $273M.

Step 0 check: no prior data/journal/trade_ledger.md entry for COO. No

this is a real, company-confirmed forward headwind, not just a bad print
in the rearview.

## Sentiment
Bearish. This is a straightforward "beat on EPS, missed and guided down
on the metric that matters (revenue/guidance), lost the M&A catalyst
investors wanted" setup — there is no credible bull case in what was
found. TradingView's technical consensus agrees: **SELL** (3 buy / 16
sell / 7 neutral).
