# AXTI — 2026-09-17

## Catalyst
AXT Inc (NASDAQ: AXTI), confirmed real operating company — a compound
semiconductor wafer-substrate maker (indium phosphide, gallium arsenide,
germanium; also purified gallium). Prior context checked per Step 0:
lessons.md #3 and HANDOFF.md both document that AXTI moved as part of a
correlated semiconductor/AI-infrastructure sector-wide rally on
2026-09-08 (alongside KLAC, ALAB, NBIS, TSEM, FORM, UCTT, TTMI, SMTC,
BE) with no single discrete AXTI-specific news event that day. Today's
+11.44% move to $64.30 on 09-16 has a genuinely different, company-
specific driver: AXT is being added to the S&P SmallCap 600 index
(replacing Matthews International), effective before the open on
09/21/2026 — a real, dated, company-specific event (index inclusion
forces passive-fund buying). This is layered on an existing bullish
narrative (indium phosphide demand tied to AI data-center optics/
components, stock up 600%+ YTD per Yahoo Finance) and a slate of
upcoming investor conferences (Needham Semiconductor & SemiCap, B. Riley
Consumer & TMT, Morgan Stanley Asia Best Corporate Day) that management
scheduled around the same window.

## Sentiment
Bullish, and unlike 09-08, today's driver is index-mechanical
(inclusion-driven passive buying) plus a genuinely company-specific
secular demand story (indium phosphide for AI data centers), not another
undifferentiated sector-wide pop. Financials look healthy: $47.6M
quarterly revenue, 32.2% gross margin, $10.4M operating income, $412M
cash, minimal debt, 4.8 current ratio.

## Risks
- Stock is up 600%+ YTD — a large amount of the AI/data-center optimism
  may already be priced in; index-inclusion pops are also frequently
  transient (they front-run the effective date and often fade once
  passive buying is complete on 09/21).
- Small-cap, historically volatile name; today's ~11% one-day move on
  index news alone (not fundamentals) is a "sell the news"-style setup
  the project has flagged before (lessons.md #1) once the 09/21
  inclusion date passes and forced buying is done.
- Underlying compound-semiconductor demand can be as cyclical/sector-
  correlated as the 09-08 move already demonstrated — a genuinely
  AXTI-specific catalyst today doesn't rule out this being part of a
  broader semiconductor upswing too.
