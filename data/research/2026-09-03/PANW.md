# PANW — 2026-09-03

## Catalyst
PANW reported fiscal Q4/FY2026 earnings on 2026-09-01 (after close) and beat on revenue
($3.41B, +34% YoY, vs. $3.35B consensus) with above-consensus FY2027 guidance and record
RPO ($14.2B). Despite the beat, the stock fell 5.2% on 09-01 and another ~8-9% on 09-02
(close $328.18, down $33.91), a two-day drop of ~13.2% — the steepest back-to-back move
since Feb 2024. The sell-off is being attributed to gross margin compression (74.8%, down
100bps YoY) and management commentary that cloud hosting/memory/storage costs will grow
faster than revenue in FY2027, on top of a rich valuation (~72x P/FCF for 17% underlying
growth) that left no room for anything less than a flawless print. Notably, several sell-side
firms (DA Davidson, Jefferies, Scotiabank) raised price targets ($420-$450) even as the
stock fell — a real disconnect between headline sell-side sentiment and price action.

## Sentiment
Mixed/bearish near-term price action vs. bullish underlying analyst sentiment. The move
looks like a "sell the news"/margin-scare reaction on a stock that had already run up into
earnings, not a thesis-breaking event — but it's a real, large move on heavy volume
(~13.2M), not noise.

## Risks
- By the time this research is being read (09-03), the catalyst (earnings) has already
  fired and the reaction has already played out over two full sessions — most of the
  information edge is gone; chasing here is chasing a move, not front-running one.
- Margin-compression concerns are structural (rising AI/cloud infra costs), not one-time —
  could keep multiple compressed rather than mean-revert quickly.
- High valuation means further disappointment has little cushion.
- Analyst price-target raises could reflect stale sell-side optimism rather than new
  information, and don't reliably predict near-term price direction.
