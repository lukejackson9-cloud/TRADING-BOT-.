inverse, single-stock-linked ETF (Tidal Investments/Defiance) that targets

options rather than holding PLTR directly. It is not an operating company.

product moving roughly -15% to -16% against a +7.9% move in the underlying is
consistent with how the product is designed to behave (with some variance
from compounding/reset mechanics), so this is not a distinct catalyst on
PLTZ itself — it is entirely derivative of PLTR's move.
