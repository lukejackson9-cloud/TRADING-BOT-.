# METC — 2026-09-03

## Catalyst
METC is **Ramaco Resources, Inc.** — confirmed: a real operating
metallurgical (steelmaking) coal producer in West Virginia/Virginia, also
developing a rare-earth/critical-minerals project (Brook Mine, Wyoming) as a
secondary growth thesis. Not an obscure or synthetic ticker.

Today's ~-12.8% to -14.3% decline is corroborated by a same-day article
("Ramaco Resources (NASDAQ:METC) Shares Down 12.8% – What's Next?",
published 2026-09-03). The move looks like continued fallout from a string of
negative sell-side revisions following soft Q2 2026 results (reported Aug 4:
$15.4M net loss, Class A diluted EPS -$0.26, Adjusted EBITDA only $5.7M):
Baird cut its price target sharply (from $25 to $13) and then downgraded the
stock outright to Neutral from Outperform; Morgan Stanley cut its target from
$13 to $11 with an Equal Weight rating (Aug 18). This is happening despite a
broader met-coal price backdrop that's actually been firming (Australian HCC
index above $252/t, prices at an 11-week high per general commodity data) —
which suggests the pressure on METC is more company-specific (execution,
margins, Brook Mine cost/scrutiny) than a sector-wide coal selloff. Notably,
not all analysts are bearish: B. Riley raised its target to $24 (from $22)
and kept a Buy. Ramaco's rare-earth claims at Brook Mine have also drawn
public scrutiny from geologists and short-sellers, which is a standing
overhang on the bull case's speculative upside.

## Sentiment
Mixed-to-bearish near-term (post-earnings de-rating, momentum still down
today), with a real bull counter-case (B. Riley Buy, firming met-coal prices,
Brook Mine optionality) that keeps this from being a clean "avoid."

## Risks
- Earnings quality: net loss and weak EBITDA in Q2 despite decent commodity
  prices raises questions about cost control/execution.
- Brook Mine rare-earth thesis (the main long-term re-rating catalyst bulls
  cite) is contested by outside geologists and short-sellers — real
  headline/whipsaw risk either direction.
- Analyst target spread is unusually wide ($11 to $24+), signaling genuine
  disagreement, not a settled read.
- Coal-sector macro exposure (steel demand, China import policy) even though
  today's move looks idiosyncratic rather than macro-driven.
