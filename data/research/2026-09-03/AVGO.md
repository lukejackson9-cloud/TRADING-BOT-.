# AVGO — 2026-09-03

## Catalyst
Broadcom reported fiscal Q3 2026 results after the bell (adjusted EPS $3.32,
revenue $29.59B, both ahead of consensus; AI semiconductor revenue up 221%
YoY to $16.7B; record free cash flow $13.7B). Despite the beat, fiscal Q4
guidance of ~$34.8B came in below the ~$35.03B Wall Street was modeling,
and that guidance miss triggered the sell-off that started in after-hours
trading Wednesday and continued into today's session. Coverage also
attributes part of the move to a sector-wide reaction: Marvell Technology's
Q4 results (also released this week) drew a bearish reaction in the
connectivity-chip space, and traders appear to be broadly trimming
high-flying AI/semi names — Schwab data cited "clients were net sellers of
Broadcom in July," consistent with profit-taking on a stock that has run up
significantly. So this is a real, dated, company-specific catalyst
(guidance miss on an earnings beat) compounded by sector rotation/profit
taking — not pure unexplained noise.

Note: quoted magnitude of the move varies by source and time of day
(-3.43% to -5.7% intraday vs. the -2.7% figure in the screener) — the
screener snapshot and later-session/intraday web reporting don't fully
agree on size, though direction (down) is consistent across all sources.

## Sentiment
Mixed-to-bearish near-term, despite strong fundamentals. The earnings
print itself (EPS/revenue beat, AI revenue growth, FCF) is bullish
long-term evidence of continued AI infrastructure demand. But the
market's reaction is clearly bearish in the short term: guidance
disappointment + profit-taking after a large prior run-up + spillover
weakness from Marvell's report. No analyst downgrades were found in the
search results — this reads more as "priced-to-perfection stock undershoots
sky-high guidance expectations" than as a fundamental thesis break.

## Risks
- This is a LONG-ONLY bot: the catalyst found is a negative one (guidance
  miss + sector de-rating). There is no bullish catalyst to trade on the
  long side today; a long entry here would be trying to catch a falling
  knife on already-negative news, not trading a documented positive
  catalyst.
- Post-earnings AI/semi stocks can be volatile for several sessions
  (whipsaw risk) as the market digests guidance vs. beat.
- Mega-cap, highly liquid — a ~3-6% single-day move on an earnings
  reaction is a real, explainable catalyst (not just noise), but that
  same liquidity means the move is already fully reflected in price by
  the time a retail advisory bot could act on it.
- Broader AI-capex-sensitivity risk: any read-through from Marvell/Broadcom
  guidance to a slowing AI capex narrative could pressure the whole group
  further.
