## Bear case (from independent agent)
Summary: This is already a "sell the news" stock — a genuine beat-and-raise
got sold as hard as 9.5% after-hours, a strong signal the market found
something structurally wrong under the hood despite the headline numbers.
The two things that spooked the market are real and company-acknowledged,
not sentiment noise: management itself flagged sequential margin
moderation into Q4, and orders grew 42% YoY vs. revenue's 34% — CEO Neri
directly attributed the gap to DDR5/DDR4/NAND/wafer shortages he does not
expect to ease "anytime soon," some persisting through 2028. This
reframes "record backlog" as evidence HPE can't fulfill demand it already
has, not proof of unstoppable demand — and it's not new: Morgan Stanley
downgraded HPE to Equalweight back in November 2025 on this exact
memory-supercycle thesis, and Sept 2's print is confirming that thesis
playing out, not resolving it. Valuation has already re-rated hard: up
~120-149% YTD, forward P/E expanded from ~13.8x in mid-August to ~20x now
vs. a ~16x sector average.
Self-assessment: strong, well-sourced — "the margin-moderation guidance and
supply-constrained backlog are company-acknowledged facts... not analyst
speculation." Flagged the insider-selling angle itself as weak padding
(trades were 4-6 months old, at half today's price) rather than overselling it.
Key sources: [AH selloff detail — Investing.com](https://uk.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/hpe-stock-selloff-what-owners-and-nonowners-should-do-next-93CH-4857581), [Neri on supply constraints through 2028 — Digitimes](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20260903VL205/demand-hpe-revenue-cost-growth.html), [Nov 2025 Morgan Stanley downgrade — StreetInsider](https://www.streetinsider.com/General+News/Morgan+Stanley+slashes+ratings+on+Dell,+HP+and+HPE+amid+memory+spike/25621687.html), [Forward P/E expansion — GuruFocus](https://www.gurufocus.com/term/forward-pe-ratio/HPE)
