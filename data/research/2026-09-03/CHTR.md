# CHTR — 2026-09-03

## Catalyst
CHTR rose ~8.7% on 2026-09-02 (range $144.83-$150.49, ~2.9M volume) after CFO Jessica
Fischer's resignation was announced on 2026-08-28 (effective 2026-10-15) and had initially
pushed the stock down (~1.8% pre-market slide on the news). The 09-02 pop is best read as a
relief rally: management reassured investors the departure was amicable and unrelated to any
operational or financial-reporting disagreement, reaffirmed financial guidance, and named a
long-tenured internal successor (Kevin Howard, current EVP/Chief Accounting Officer, who
previously served as interim CFO in 2010) — removing uncertainty rather than adding new
positive information. This sits against a backdrop of the pending $34.5B Cox Communications
merger (FCC/DOJ approved, expected to close mid-2026) and a rough operating picture: CHTR
lost 120,000 broadband subscribers in Q1 2026 (more than double the prior-year pace) and the
stock fell 25.5% in a single day after that Q1 print. Mobile lines are a bright spot (+17%
YoY to 12.1M).

## Sentiment
Mixed-to-cautious. Consensus analyst rating is "Hold" with a $150 price target — essentially
at spot after the 09-02 move, meaning the pop was mostly a relief bounce, not sell-side
upgrading its view. Analysts cite high leverage and ongoing subscriber pressure holding the
name back despite strong cash flow.

## Risks
- The specific move is a bounce-back from an overreaction to a disclosed executive departure,
  not a new fundamental positive — that kind of move often round-trips once the news cycle
  fades.
- Broadband subscriber losses are a real, ongoing structural problem (cord-cutting/fixed
  wireless competition), and CHTR already delivered a much larger negative surprise (-25.5%)
  on this exact theme earlier in 2026 — the trend that caused that crash hasn't reversed.
  Q3 subscriber trends (next earnings) are the real test.
- Integration risk and leverage from the pending $34.5B Cox acquisition.
- CFO transition itself, even framed as amicable, adds near-term execution/reporting risk
  during a major integration.
- Analyst price target (~$150) offers little upside from the post-pop price — not a
  favorable risk/reward for a fresh long here.
