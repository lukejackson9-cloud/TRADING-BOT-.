retire ~$1B of convertible notes (cutting debt ~30%), which is a genuine
capital-structure de-risking move, but again not a same-day catalyst.

## Sentiment
Mixed. Bullish on price action (+10.8-10.98%) and a real multi-year strategic
pivot story (Bitcoin miner → AI/HPC infrastructure, using power assets as the
scarce resource). But one source explicitly frames the AI pivot as leaving
"the market divided and buzzing" — i.e., not a consensus bull case — and
today's specific move is attributed to BTC beta, not the AI story.

## Risks
- Direct Bitcoin-price beta: MARA holds a large BTC treasury and its
  mining/AI-power business is capital- and power-intensive; a BTC pullback
  would likely hit the stock disproportionately.
- Conflicting BTC price figures across sources today is itself a signal to
  double check the news feed before treating this as confirmed instead of
  trusting a single AI-generated summary.
- The unverified "White House crypto summit" claim should not be repeated to
  the user as confirmed fact.
- AI/HPC pivot is unproven — analysts are described as "divided," and the
  Texas land deal's power capacity doesn't materialize until Oct 2027 at the
  earliest, so near-term financial read-through is limited.
- Company sold a meaningful chunk of its BTC treasury (15,133 BTC) in 2026,
  which cuts both ways: less BTC upside exposure, but also less of a "pure
  BTC proxy" than MSTR if BTC keeps rallying.
