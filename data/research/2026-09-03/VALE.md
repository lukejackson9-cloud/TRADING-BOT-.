identifiable drivers behind Vale's recent weeks are: (1) iron ore prices
easing to the $90-98/tonne range on softer China steel demand; (2) Vale
raising its 2026 C1 cash cost guidance (US$22.50-23.50/tonne) on a stronger

340-360Mt, which one Sept 1-3 iron-ore-market recap frames as
supply-discipline that some investors read as *supportive* for future

"mega-cap noise/rotation, not a real distinct catalyst" pattern to be
skeptical of.
