Mine (Mali), Masbate (Philippines), Otjikoto (Namibia), and the newer Goose
Mine (Canada). As flagged going in, this looks like the same sector-beta
move already documented for CDE on 2026-09-02: gold priced around

State of Mali on 2026-08-07 (a genuine positive for a near-term production

held Q2 production below plan. None of these are dated to today or
yesterday. No BTG-specific news was found that explains today's move beyond
the gold price.

## Sentiment
Bullish on price action, but read-through is macro/sector beta rather than

## Risks
- Pure commodity-beta exposure: a reversal in the Iran de-escalation
  narrative or a hawkish Fed re-repricing unwinds this as fast as it built
  — same risk flagged for CDE.
- Operational/safety overhang: a fatal accident at Masbate (2026-08-28) is
  a live regulatory and reputational risk, not yet resolved.
- Goose Mine has already underperformed plan this year due to the April
  crushing-circuit fire; consolidated 2026 guidance sits in an already-cut
  820,000–920,000 oz range.
- Jurisdictional/political risk: Mali (Fekola) carries elevated
  sovereign/permitting risk relative to a developed-market miner, even with
  the Menankoto permit now in hand.
- Ex-dividend date 2026-09-10 for a small ($0.02) dividend — not a real
  catalyst either way, just a note for anyone tracking total return.
