proceeds; and there has been notable insider buying, including the CEO

company also did a 1-for-7 reverse split effective June 1, 2026, which
thins the float and can amplify percentage moves on modest dollar volume.
Given no same-day headline was found, today's specific spike looks more
consistent with continued momentum in a low-float microcap turnaround
story than a fresh, dated catalyst.

## Sentiment
Mixed-to-bullish. The fundamental trend (profitability turnaround, insider
buying, capital raise completed rather than pending) is genuinely
constructive. But absent a same-day news item, the specific size of
today's move can't be pinned to anything beyond momentum/thin float, which

## Risks
- No dated, same-day catalyst found — the move may be largely float-driven
  momentum rather than new information, which raises whipsaw/reversal risk.
- Very small, thinly traded insurance microcap; a 1-for-7 reverse split
  earlier this year confirms a small, easily-moved share count.
- Insurance is a cyclical, weather/claims-exposed business; a single good
  quarter (improved combined ratio) is not yet a multi-quarter trend.
- P&C insurers carry tail risk from catastrophic loss events that can
  reverse an improving combined ratio quickly.
