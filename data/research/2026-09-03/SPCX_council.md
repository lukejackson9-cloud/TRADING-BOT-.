## Bear case (from independent agent)
Summary: Today's pop is buying the news, not ahead of it — Oppenheimer's
target raise came out today and has already been consumed in one session,
against a $117-$222 target spread where Oppenheimer sits at the high end,
not consensus. Valuation leaves no room for error: ~$2.03T market cap on
~$36-39B 2026 revenue estimates is a 51-78x P/S, reportedly 3x Broadcom's
multiple, with Morgan Stanley not modeling FCF-positive until 2035. Most
decisive: Starship Flight 14 is a maiden orbital attempt PLUS a novel
landing-catch maneuver on one flight, against a rough recent Starship test
record (explosions in March, June, and November 2025). The Sept 9 lockup
tranche (319M shares) falls before Flight 14 even happens, and one report
explicitly attributes part of today's rally to "position adjustment ahead
of upcoming insider lockup expiration" — meaning some of today's buying may
itself be pre-unlock positioning, not a durable re-rating. Plus governance
risk: Musk controls ~84% of voting power on ~39% economic ownership.
Self-assessment: strong, not manufactured — "points 1, 3, and 4 in
particular are dated, specific, and land inside the exact trade horizon."
Key sources: [SPCX opened +5.12% on Oppenheimer note — TradingKey](https://www.tradingkey.com/news/market-movers/262149133-market-movers-spcx-20260903), [Valuation vs. Broadcom — Morningstar](https://www.morningstar.com/stocks/spacex-rally-continues-expanding-sky-high-valuations), [FAA approves landing-catch on Flight 14 — Tesla Oracle](https://www.teslaoracle.com/2026/09/03/the-faa-approves-landing-catch-of-starship-in-flight-14/), [Lockup schedule detail — Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/analysis/spacex-lockup-countdown-when-shares-may-become-safer-to-buy-200682574)
