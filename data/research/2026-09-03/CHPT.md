# CHPT — 2026-09-03

## Catalyst
ChargePoint Holdings (real, NYSE-listed EV-charging network operator) reported
Q2 FY2027 results Wednesday after the close: revenue of $116.1M, up 17.7%
YoY and above the $105.2M consensus; adjusted EPS of -$0.35 vs. -$0.85
expected; adjusted EBITDA loss narrowed to $4.75M from -$22.1M a year ago;
record 38% gross margin (35% ex tariff refunds); and management said the
quarter had zero cash burn. That beat-and-narrower-loss combination is the
specific, findable cause of today's +74-75% move — this is not a rumor or a
sympathy move, it's a real earnings reaction in a stock that was down 22%
YTD and 6% over the prior month coming in.

## Sentiment
Mixed-to-cautiously-positive. The earnings print itself is genuinely good
and the market's reaction is a legitimate repricing, not noise. But the
move is extreme (74%+ in a single session) and short interest is high
(~16.2% of the 24.4M share float per Fintel), so a real fundamental beat
and a short squeeze are almost certainly compounding each other. Analyst
sentiment has not caught up: consensus rating is still "Hold" with an
average price target around $9.67 (some sources ~$8.94-$9), which is at or
just above today's ~$9.05 print — Wall Street isn't calling this a
new-trend buy yet, just recalibrating off a very low bar.

## Risks
- The move already happened: by the time this research was written the
  stock was already up ~75% on the day. Chasing a name after a vertical
  one-day spike driven partly by a short squeeze carries high mean-reversion
  risk — a lot of the "not-yet-priced-in" edge is gone.
- Balance sheet is still fragile: ~$96M cash on hand and a historical
  run-rate burn around $20M/quarter (even though this specific quarter was
  breakeven on cash) — one good quarter doesn't erase the multi-year cash
  burn pattern.
- Real dilution overhang: ~3.4M shares issuable via outstanding warrants,
  and any future capital raise (still plausible given thin cash) would
  dilute holders further.
- High short interest cuts both ways — it fueled today's pop but also means
  volatility (in either direction) will stay elevated over the next 1-2
  weeks.
- Small float / low share count (24.4M shares out) makes the stock prone to
  outsized, hard-to-hold swings.
