# GIII — 2026-09-03

## Catalyst
G-III Apparel reported Q2 FY2026 earnings on Wednesday 2026-09-02 (the session
the -11.5% move happened). EPS actually beat consensus ($0.26 vs $0.23 est.)
and the company raised full-year EPS guidance, but revenue of $554.1M missed
and was down ~9.6-9.7% YoY, and next-quarter revenue guidance ($870M) came in
soft. Multiple sources describe this as a top-line miss + weak forward
guidance overshadowing the EPS beat. There's also a structural overhang
flagged independently by sell-side (Piper Sandler, UBS, in earlier notes) around
the expiration of PVH-licensed brands (Calvin Klein / Tommy Hilfiger), which
they call a "clear medium-term headwind to EBITDA" — this predates the
9/2 print but is consistent with why guidance disappointed.

This is a real, sourced, earnings-day catalyst — not a vague "price moved" tag.

## Sentiment
Mixed-to-bearish. The EPS beat and raised full-year outlook are genuine
positives, but the market reaction (net -11.5% on the day) shows investors
weighting the revenue miss, soft guidance, and license-expiration headwind
much more heavily. No source found post-print upgrades or price-target
increases from 9/2-9/3; the more recent analyst commentary on file (Piper
Sandler $16 PT, UBS $26 PT, both Neutral) predates this print and was already
cautious on margin headwinds ahead of it.

## Risks
- The catalyst has already fired and the stock has already made its big move
  — this is chasing a post-earnings drop, not getting ahead of one. Post-
  earnings moves can continue (drift) or mean-revert; no confirmation either
  way was found in the 48-72h search window.
- Structural headwind (loss of PVH-licensed brands: Calvin Klein, Tommy
  Hilfiger) is a multi-quarter drag on EBITDA, not a one-day story — this is
  not a clean short-term bounce setup.
- Revenue down ~9.7% YoY is a real deceleration, not noise.
- Apparel retail is macro/consumer-spending sensitive and promotional-
  cadence sensitive heading into a holiday-adjacent stretch.
