## Risks
- SRPT has a genuinely rough safety history: two Elevidys patient deaths in 2026 (March and
  June, both non-ambulatory patients, linked to acute liver injury/failure), leading the FDA
  to add a boxed warning and narrow the approved population to ambulatory patients 4+. A
  third 2025 death was deemed unrelated. This is real, recent, company-specific tail risk —
  any negative update on the paused non-ambulatory dosing study or new adverse event could
  reverse sentiment sharply and fast, which matters a lot given the position-sizing/stop-loss
  rules this desk uses.
- No confirmed, dated near-term catalyst within the 2-week trading horizon this bot targets —
  the actual data readouts cited as the reason for optimism are H2-2026 (undated) or later.
- Analyst price targets are currently below spot, a bearish tell against chasing the pop.
- Thin-ish market cap (~$2.46B) and biotech name — prone to outsized headline-driven
  whipsaws in either direction.
