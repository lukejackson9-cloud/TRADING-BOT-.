it to the user as high-conviction."

## Bear case (from independent agent)
Summary: The Berenberg initiation was NOT ASTS-specific — it was a 4-stock
sector-wide coverage launch (Rocket Lab, ASTS, Planet Labs, HawkEye 360), a
generic "space economy" thesis, not a change in ASTS's own execution. The
$619K insider buy is a rounding error against ~$280.6M of net insider
selling in the trailing 90 days (Mikitani $270.9M, American Tower $159.6M).
The business took a real hit, not a sentiment one: commercial launch pushed
to early 2027 after a second Blue Origin-linked setback, funded partly by a
$1.15B dilutive convertible note that itself cut the stock 13-17% in July.
Q1 revenue missed consensus by a wide margin ($14.7M vs. ~$38.4M est.).
Self-assessment: strong, not manufactured — "the honest open question is
whether [this] is already reflected in the post-pop price."
Key sources: [Yahoo Finance — sector-wide Berenberg launch](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/berenberg-launches-space-coverage-initiates-082127939.html), [MarketBeat — 90-day insider selling](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/ast-spacemobile-nasdaqasts-major-shareholder-sells-15449980000-in-stock-2026-04-16/), [Motley Fool — launch delay to 2027](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/articles/ast-spacemobile-delays-launch-2027-231838039.html), [Barchart — $1B convertible, Q1 revenue miss](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3348793/a-1-billion-reason-to-sell-ast-spacemobile-stock-here)
