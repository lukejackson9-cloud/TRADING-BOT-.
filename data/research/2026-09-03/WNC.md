## Risks
- Volume ~1.04M is right at this project's >1M liquidity floor — thin enough
  that a single large order or headline can produce outsized, hard-to-exit
  moves. Flagging explicitly per screen criteria.
- No dated, ticker-specific catalyst for the actual 9/2 move was found — the
  move looks like it rode a broad cyclical/risk-on rally (falling VIX, falling
  oil) more than anything Wabash-specific. Trading it as a Wabash story risks
  misattributing a market-wide move to company fundamentals.
- Company is still GAAP-unprofitable; the bull case (DA Davidson) is a 2027
  demand-recovery thesis, i.e. explicitly not a near-term (days-to-2-week)
  story.
- Trailer/transportation-equipment demand is cyclical and freight-market
  exposed; a reversal in oil prices or renewed geopolitical risk (the same
  factors credited for today's rally) would remove the tailwind just as
  quickly.
