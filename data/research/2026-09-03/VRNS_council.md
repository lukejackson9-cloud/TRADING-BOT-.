conviction momentum entry."

Summary: This is the SECOND unconfirmed takeover rumor on this exact ticker
in under three months — Bloomberg reported preliminary PE interest from
Blackstone, Thoma Bravo, and Vista on 2026-06-23 (stock popped as much as
23% intraday), and roughly ten weeks later that process produced no deal,
only a new rumored suitor. Sell-side reaction to the September pop is
lukewarm, not confirmatory: Citi raised its target to just $45 — BELOW the
$46.76 close — while keeping a Neutral rating. An independent overvaluation

fundamental cracks sit underneath: Q2 billings fell 1.5% YoY, gross margin
compressed 4.2 points. Most seriously: an October 2025 disclosure of a

and is now the subject of an active securities fraud class action
(Molchanov v. Varonis Systems, S.D.N.Y.) alleging management misrepresented
renewal health.
Self-assessment: strong, not manufactured — built on a documented repeat
pattern, a lukewarm sell-side reaction to the very rumor that moved the
price, and an active fraud lawsuit tied to a real disclosure failure.
