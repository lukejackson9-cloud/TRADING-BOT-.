# ALNY — 2026-09-03

## Catalyst
Alnylam presented new Phase 3 HELIOS-B data for AMVUTTRA (vutrisiran) at the
European Society of Cardiology (ESC) Congress 2026, plus updated
zilebesiran hypertension data. The headline number: a 36% reduction in
all-cause mortality at 42 months, with management saying AMVUTTRA met
10 of 10 endpoints in HELIOS-B, and a prespecified subgroup analysis
(overall + monotherapy populations) showing 28.2%/32.8% reductions in
mortality and recurrent CV events through 36 months. This is a specific,
dated, substantive clinical readout — not just "stock went up."
In direct response, several sell-side desks moved same-window:
TD Cowen reiterated Buy, raised PT to $482; Canaccord Genuity reiterated
Buy, PT $429; Raymond James upgraded to Strong Buy, PT $420; Bernstein
maintained Outperform, PT $376; BMO reiterated Outperform citing ATTR
franchise strength. Stock moved from ~$246 (9/1 close context) up ~5.8-8.65%
into this cluster of upgrades/reiterations.

## Sentiment
Net bullish but not unanimous. Multiple large PT raises/reiterations
(TD Cowen, Canaccord, Raymond James, Bernstein, BMO) within the same news
window as the ESC data is a real, non-trivial positive signal. Counterpoint:
Jefferies kept a Hold with a $230 price target — well below spot — arguing
the data puts pressure on the TTR silencer franchise long-term (questioning
whether silencers are still needed once patients are stabilized on other
drugs). So sentiment is bullish-leaning but genuinely split among analysts,
not a clean consensus.

## Risks
- Q2 2026 EPS ($1.84) missed estimates ($2.00) by 8% — the underlying
  business isn't flawless even as the pipeline story improves.
- Single-drug/franchise concentration risk: much of the bull case rides on
  AMVUTTRA/TTR silencing; Jefferies' bear thesis (stabilizer drugs reducing
  need for silencers over time) is a real structural risk, not noise.
- Biotech binary/headline risk generally — sentiment can reverse sharply on
  any competitive readout (e.g., BridgeBio, Pfizer TTR-stabilizer data).
- The move is already 1-2 days old and has been picked up by mainstream
  financial media (Yahoo Finance, Investing.com, StreetInsider) with
  specific published analyst price targets — the most obvious "read the
  press release, buy" money is very likely already made.
- Next earnings 10/29/2026 is outside this bot's ~2-week horizon but a
  confirmed forward risk/catalyst window worth flagging.
