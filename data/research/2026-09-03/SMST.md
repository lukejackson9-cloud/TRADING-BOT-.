# SMST — 2026-09-03

## Catalyst
SMST is not an operating company. It is the "Defiance Daily Target 2X Short
MSTR ETF" (issued by Defiance ETFs, trading on Nasdaq), a leveraged inverse
single-stock ETF that uses swaps/derivatives to deliver roughly -2x the
daily price move of MicroStrategy Incorporated (NYSE: MSTR). Today's -35.1%
move is simply ~2x an implied strong up-day in MSTR (itself a Bitcoin-proxy
stock), not company-specific news about SMST — there is no underlying
business, earnings, or catalyst to research here.

## Risks
- Leveraged (2x) inverse single-stock ETF: explicitly excluded by this
  bot's mandate, which is long-only equities and does not trade
  leveraged/inverse products.
- Daily-reset leverage products suffer compounding/decay over any holding
  period longer than a single session, and this bot's horizon
  (intraday-to-2-weeks) would compound that decay.
- Expense ratio 1.31%; thinly traded relative to a normal equity; price is
  entirely a function of MSTR's daily move, which is itself a function of
  Bitcoin's price and MicroStrategy's leveraged BTC treasury — two layers
  removed from anything this bot can research as a "catalyst."
