lessons point in opposite directions here — stating which applies, as
3b requires, rather than defaulting:**

  catalyst" is the error that cost CLS and CCC.L.

  has run ~18% in two sessions and would be bought Monday at the top of
  that run, with no dated event inside the 2-week window to carry it.
- **Which applies**: the revision trend is real and is recorded as a
  positive in its own right (per 3b). But the trend was equally real on
  09-10, before an 18% two-session move — and this project's horizon is
  ~2 weeks with a -4% stop, which an extended name can trip on noise
  alone. The revision trend supports the COMPANY; it does not make THIS
  ENTRY, at this price, after this run, a good one.
- Note this project PASS'd BAND on 09-10 for "no confirmed dated
  catalyst" and it then rose 7.5% — logged as a candidate lesson #9
