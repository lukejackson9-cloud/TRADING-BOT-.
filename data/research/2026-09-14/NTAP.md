research pass, and the stock is already up roughly +23% from its pre-
earnings level by 09-11. Chasing the 09-11 print specifically means buying
into a multi-day post-earnings drift that has already re-rated the stock
across five analyst desks, not catching the reaction itself. Per lessons.md
#5, the specific 09-11 move leans on analyst target updates reacting to
stale news, which is weaker evidence than the original earnings print
itself. No new dated company-specific event on 09-11 beyond a routine
