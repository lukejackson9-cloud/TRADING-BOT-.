+14.18% on 2026-09-11. There are two stacked, real, dated catalysts
here, not one: (1) Q2 FY2026 earnings, reported 2026-09-09 — revenue of

(from $10.00 to $16.50), with Jefferies and Truist also having raised
targets (to $16-18) in the same window — the analyst re-rating is the

decline to an "algorithm dislocation" with its largest ad partner
hurting IL MAKIAGE customer-acquisition costs, while flagging
SpoiledChild (+35%+ growth, ~$350M run-rate) and METHODIQ as genuine
bright spots.
