earnings." The task's confirmed +7.71% move is dated 09-11, one day after
the print — consistent with an initial cautious reaction giving way to a
next-day rally as the market reassessed the comp-sales strength, rather
than same-day earnings euphoria.

## Sentiment
Genuinely mixed. The headline beat-and-raise is real, but multiple sources
flag that a meaningful part of the EPS beat ($0.23/share) came from tariff
refunds rather than core retail operations — an earnings-quality concern,
not just noise.

## Risks
- Earnings-quality flag: a real chunk of the beat is a one-time tariff
  refund, not organic margin improvement — weakens the "real edge" case
  even though the top-line comp-sales trend (5 straight quarters of
  growth) is genuinely positive.
- Per lessons.md #1, entering on 09-11 means buying one full session after
  the catalyst already fired and after an initial negative reaction
  reversed — a "buying the recovery" setup rather than catching the
  catalyst fresh.
- Per lessons.md #2, exact-magnitude/direction claims from WebSearch
  disagree (a same-day ~5% drop vs. a next-day ~6.7%/7.71% rally) and
  should not be taken as fully resolved without OHLCV verification if this
  were to advance further.
- Retail/consumer-discretionary sector exposure to tariff and consumer-
  spending macro risk generally.
