# ATEC — 2026-09-14

## Catalyst
Alphatec Holdings (spine-focused medtech) jumped +19.68% on 2026-09-11
after CEO Patrick Miles' Form 4 disclosure of an open-market purchase of
115,000 shares (~$1.01M, executed 2026-09-10 at a weighted-average price
of $8.81) hit the tape — a real, dated, company-specific event, not a
rumor. It landed on top of two other recent, verifiable positives: (1)
Alphatec's Wells Fargo Healthcare Conference fireside chat on 2026-09-09,
where management reaffirmed FY2026 guidance (~$882M revenue, $140M
EBITDA, $20M FCF) and framed procedure volumes/surgeon adoption/new
product launches as ahead of the broader spine market; (2) the most
recent quarter's print — revenue $213.5M, +15% YoY, positive free cash
flow. The insider buy is the specific trigger for the size of the
2026-09-11 pop; the guidance reaffirmation and Q2 strength are the
underlying support for why the market reacted as strongly as it did.

## Sentiment
Mixed-to-improving. TradingView's aggregated TA consensus reads BUY
(15 buy / 3 sell / 8 neutral indicators) as of 2026-09-14, consistent
with the post-pop technical picture. But context cuts the other way:
ATEC is still down -41.1% over the trailing 12 months (to 2026-08-28)
and the insider purchase was executed near a 52-week low ($6.82),
meaning the CEO was buying into a beaten-down stock, not a strong one —
a genuine conviction signal, but also a reminder how far the name had
fallen before this bounce.

## Risks
- By 2026-09-14 the reaction is 3 trading days old — per this project's
  own "sell the news"/overextension lesson (lessons.md #1), buying after
  a gap has already fired tends to be a worse entry than getting in
  ahead of it. There is no fresh, undigested catalyst as of today.
  This is a positive, real catalyst on a name that already ran on it —
  not a still-open long entry.
- Single insider (even the CEO) buying $1M of stock is a real signal but
  a modest dollar amount relative to the pop it produced; a large
  reaction to a comparatively small purchase can mean thin liquidity/
  short-covering amplified the move as much as the news itself.
- Small/mid-cap medtech name with a well-documented rough YTD — spine
  market execution risk and historical volatility remain unresolved
  regardless of this week's move.
