# SIG — 2026-09-10

## Catalyst
Real, dated, company-specific: Signet Jewelers beat Q2 earnings on
2026-09-09 (adjusted EPS $2.19 vs. $1.74 est., same-store sales +2.2%)
and raised FY guidance (adjusted EPS $10.45-$12.15, up from $9.20-$11.00).
Stock surged +23.96% same day. At least five analysts raised price
targets afterward.

Step 0 check: no prior trade_ledger.md entry for SIG.

## Sentiment
The underlying print is genuinely strong — a real beat-and-raise with
sell-side follow-through (multiple target hikes). But today (2026-09-10),
the stock is pulling back ~5.7% on profit-taking, one day after the pop.

## Risks
- **This is exactly the "reaction already fired" pattern from lessons.md
  #1** — the catalyst-driven move already happened yesterday; entering
  today means buying into a name that already made its big move and is
  now correcting, not catching the catalyst ahead of the reaction.
- Today's pullback could read as either a normal cool-off after an
  overextended pop (bearish for a fresh entry) or a discount re-entry
  point after an overreaction (bullish) — the search didn't surface
  anything that resolves which read is right, and guessing isn't research.
- No forward-looking catalyst identified inside the 1-2 week window beyond
  the raised guidance itself, which is now public and priced.
