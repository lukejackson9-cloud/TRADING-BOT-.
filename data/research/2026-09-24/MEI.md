nearly 3 months out) rather than to new fundamental improvement — the
actual news underneath (revenue growth but a widening net loss) is mixed,
not clearly positive. Per trade_ledger.md, MEI already has one prior

## Risks
- Classic "buy the calendar-date announcement" reaction — the news itself
  creates no near-term value, it's a promise of clarity 3 months out.
  Per lessons.md #1, entries right after a catalyst-driven pop tend to be
  a worse entry than a better one; today's price already reflects the
  announcement.
- Underlying fundamentals are still soft (net loss widened YoY despite
  revenue growth) — the investor-day optimism could unwind quickly if the
  next quarterly print disappoints again, which is exactly MEI's own
  recent pattern (see 09-03 ledger entry).
- Auto/industrial components exposure carries macro-cyclicality risk not
  addressed by an investor-day date.
- Nothing here rules out this being a short-covering/sentiment pop that
  fades before Dec 17 — 3 months is a long time for "buy now on a promise
  of a future presentation" to hold up.
