## Identity check
GRAL = GRAIL, Inc. (NASDAQ), a genomics/oncology company developing the
Galleri multi-cancer early detection (MCED) blood test. Not to be
confused with GRML (Greenland Mines Ltd) — a completely different
company that happens to look similar at a glance; confirmed distinct via
CNBC/Yahoo/SEC sources.

## Catalyst
Real, dated, and exactly the kind of "resolve the pre-catalyst" case
screen.md's earnings-lookahead logic is designed to catch same-session.
On 2026-09-23 the FDA's Molecular and Clinical Genetics Panel voted on
GRAIL's Galleri PMA application: 10-0 (or per one source, unanimous) in
favor of safety, 6-4 in favor of effectiveness, and 7-2 (one abstention)
that benefits outweigh risks for adults 50+. This is genuinely dated
company-specific regulatory news, not a bare price move — the panel's
own briefing documents and vote are the primary source.

Important caveat baked into the same event: the FDA's own panel noted
Galleri should NOT replace guideline-recommended single-cancer screening,
and the final FDA approval decision is still pending ("in the coming
months" per multiple sources) — this is a positive interim step, not an
approval.

## Risks
- **Priced well past every analyst's target already**: as of 09-21 (pre-
  vote), 11 analysts covering GRAL averaged a $79 price target — the
  stock is now ~27%+ above that average and above even the single
  highest-conviction post-vote target (RBC's $84, Canaccord's $80). This
  is a real catalyst that the market has already run well past
  fundamentals-implied value, the same "extended entry" pattern flagged
  repeatedly in this project (VICR, SECZ, OMER) — buying now means buying
  above where the people who cover this stock for a living think it's
  worth, even after accounting for the good news.
- **Not yet approved**: this is an advisory panel recommendation, not an
  FDA approval. The panel is non-binding (though the FDA usually follows
  it), and the final decision, labeling, insurer reimbursement, and
  post-approval study requirements are all still unresolved — real
  execution risk sits between here and actual commercial impact.
- **The panel's own reservation**: Galleri isn't meant to replace
  guideline-recommended single-cancer screening — a nuance the market's
  enthusiasm may be glossing over.
- **Elevated valuation flagged independently**: GuruFocus specifically
  called out GRAL's price-to-sales ratio as signaling "high growth
  expectations" even before this run, on top of the move itself.
- Move is still in progress (intraday as of today) — entering mid-surge
  on a name already above every covering analyst's target is a high-risk
  chase, not an early catch, despite catching the news same-session as
  designed.
