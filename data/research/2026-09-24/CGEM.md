biotech (T-cell engager pipeline — oncology + autoimmune). Two things are
true and in tension, and sources conflict on how they relate to the actual
-22.15% 09-22→09-23 move:

  selloff — Moderna -11%, ImmunityBio and Sarepta -8%, explicitly reported
  as sector-wide with "no company-specific catalyst" for the peers. CGEM's
  -22.15% is roughly 2-3x the size of that sector move, so a sector wobble
  alone doesn't fully account for it.
- **A CGEM-specific negative item exists but its date doesn't cleanly line
  up with the move being researched**: Cullinan announced (press release
  dated 2026-09-24, i.e. dated to today, one day AFTER the 09-23 close

  engager in lupus/rheumatoid arthritis/Sjögren's) will now read out as a
  consolidated update in December rather than the originally-flagged Q3
  window. Some secondary sources (MarketBeat, GuruFocus) attribute a ~7%
  same-day drop to this delay and reference "zipalertinib safety worries"
  lingering, but a positive item is ALSO in the record for CGEM this
  month: BTIG raised its price target to $40 (from $39, Buy) on 2026-09-14
  after positive Phase 3 REZILIENT3 data (zipalertinib, EGFR exon20 NSCLC)
  — the opposite signal, and also not same-day.
- Net: WebSearch sources here contradict each other on timing and
  magnitude for this exact session (a pattern lessons.md #2 already
  documents) — the size gap between CGEM's move and its biotech peers'
  moves is real and unexplained by anything cleanly dated to 09-23
