## Catalyst
Real, dated, resolved: Costco reported Q4 FY2026 results on 2026-09-24.
EPS beat ($6.75 actual vs. $6.54 estimated). Revenue figures are reported
inconsistently across sources — one source (FMP's earnings-calendar data)
shows $93.87B actual vs. $94.97B estimated (a miss), while another
(StockTitan) reports $95.72B, up 11.1% YoY, ABOVE the same estimate.
Per lessons.md #2 (verify facts when sources disagree), this is flagged
as unresolved rather than asserted either way — the EPS beat itself
(confirmed consistently across both sources) is the more reliable single
fact. A one-time $0.15/share benefit from IEEPA tariff refunds is also
disclosed, meaning the underlying beat is smaller than the headline
number in isolation suggests.

## Sentiment
Muted, not a strong reaction either way. Shares popped only about +1%
immediately after the release — well inside the ~3.53% move the options
market had priced in ahead of the print, meaning the actual reaction
UNDERSHOT what was expected. This is a large, heavily-covered, low-beta
mega-cap where an in-line-to-slightly-better print was already largely
anticipated.

## Risks
- Revenue figure discrepancy between sources is unresolved — don't treat
  either number as confirmed without a primary-source cross-check if this
  becomes relevant later.
- A modest ~1% pop on a well-telegraphed print is not the kind of dated,
  under-the-radar catalyst this project's short-term (intraday-to-2-week)
  strategy is built around — the market's own options pricing shows this
  was already well anticipated, not a surprise.
- The tariff-refund benefit means the "beat" is partly non-recurring, a
  detail a headline EPS number alone would miss.
