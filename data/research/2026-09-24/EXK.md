# EXK — 2026-09-24

(Identity confirmed: Endeavour Silver Corp — silver mining company
operating primarily in Mexico, NYSE/TSX: EXK.)

## Catalyst
The -11.89% move on 2026-09-23 ($10.51 → $9.26) is a mix of sector-wide
and company-specific negative drivers, both real and dated:
- **Sector-wide**: Spot silver itself fell sharply that session — from
  ~$67.04 the prior day toward an intraday low near $64.42 (roughly
  -2.8% to -3.7% depending on the snapshot), driven by a stronger US
  dollar and renewed Fed-tightening expectations. Peer silver miners
  moved together the same day: Pan American Silver (PAAS) -5.39%, Coeur
  Mining (CDE) -5.2% — confirming this was a real sector-wide
  precious-metals selloff, not an EXK-specific move alone.
- **Company-specific, on top of the sector move**: EXK's decline
  (-11.89%/-12.8%) is more than double its silver-mining peers' moves the
  same day. The specific driver is a real, dated operational event: a
  mechanical failure (mill head/trunnion) took the primary ball mill
  offline at EXK's Guanacevi mine in Mexico, cutting processing capacity,
  with the company itself guiding to an ~3-week repair window. This is a
  genuine negative production-disruption catalyst, not just sector beta.

## Sentiment
Bearish, and this is a negative catalyst on both counts (macro + company-
specific). Not fully one-sided: National Bank maintained a Buy rating
with a CAD 25.00 target on 09-22 (the day before this drop), and
Endeavour's own Q2 2026 results were strong (+150% YoY revenue, +36%
silver-equivalent production) — but neither of those offsets a real
multi-week production outage or a falling underlying commodity price.

## Risks
- This is a negative catalyst (falling silver price + a mine-processing
  outage) — per the long-only mandate, not actionable as a buy regardless
  of how the fundamentals argument reads.
- Sector-wide correlation: any bounce thesis here would really be a bet
  on silver prices broadly recovering, not an EXK-specific bounce case —
  see lessons.md #3 (sector-wide moves aren't independent opportunities).
- The ~3-week mill repair is a real, quantifiable near-term production
  hit that will show up in Q3 output numbers — a concrete negative
  overhang with a known (if approximate) resolution date, worth
  rechecking once the mill is back online.
- Balance sheet is reasonably solid (~$236.6M cash vs. $244.1M long-term
  debt, current ratio ~2.0) — this isn't a distressed-company situation,
  just an operational/macro setback.
