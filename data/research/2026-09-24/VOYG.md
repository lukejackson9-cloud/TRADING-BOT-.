# VOYG — 2026-09-24

## Catalyst
Confirmed: Voyager Technologies, Inc. (NYSE: VOYG), a defense/space technology company, announced on 2026-09-22 its intent to offer $350 million in convertible senior notes due 2032 (private placement to qualified institutional buyers), with an option for initial purchasers to buy an additional $52.5 million within 13 days. On 2026-09-23/24 the offering was priced: 0% coupon, initial conversion price ~$40.82/share (a 30% premium to the prior close of $31.40). Proceeds are earmarked partly for capped-call transactions (designed to reduce future dilution) and general corporate purposes; settlement is scheduled for 2026-09-28. This is a real, dated, company-specific event, not a rumor.

## Sentiment
Bearish on the news — a convertible-notes offering of this size (~$350M against a company priced around $31/share pre-announcement) reads to the market as dilution risk and often as a signal management sees the current share price as a good level to raise capital against. Per the desk's own tracking, the stock fell roughly 5-7% in reaction across the 09-23/09-24 window on dilution concerns. GuruFocus's own coverage explicitly frames this as "amid challenging fundamentals" — not a clean vote of confidence.

## Risks
- Straightforward dilution risk: even though it's a 0%-coupon convert with capped calls to blunt some of the eventual share dilution, the market's initial read is negative, and converts of this size are a real overhang until conversion terms are resolved or the notes are retired.
- This project is long-only (no shorting) — a negative, dilution-driven catalyst is not directly actionable here.
- No offsetting positive company-specific news found alongside the raise (e.g. no contract win or guidance raise disclosed in the same window) that would argue for a "raise to fund growth, that's bullish" read instead of a defensive-capital read.
