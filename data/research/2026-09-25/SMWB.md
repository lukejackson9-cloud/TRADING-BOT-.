# SMWB — 2026-09-25

## Catalyst
Similarweb (NYSE: SMWB, web-analytics/data company — identity confirmed,
not a name collision) fell -12.24% on 2026-09-24 ($8.58 → $7.53) on a
real, dated, company-specific catalyst: the announcement that founder/CEO
Or Offer will step down, with Michael Akkerman succeeding him as CEO
effective 2026-11-02. Compounding the leadership-change news, the
departing CEO disclosed two separate insider stock sales in the days just
before the drop — 37,500 shares on 9/18 (~$309,375) and 32,200 shares on
9/21 (~$270,480), both per SEC filings. This sits on top of an otherwise
strong recent fundamental backdrop: a record Q2 2026 print reported
earlier this month (revenue +9% YoY to $77.2M, first positive GAAP
operating profit, three new enterprise contracts worth $60M+, AI revenue
now 13% of total).

## Sentiment
Bearish reaction to a leadership-transition announcement, sharpened by
optics of the outgoing founder-CEO selling stock right before his own
departure was announced. Underlying analyst sentiment is still
constructive on paper — 5 analysts rate it Buy, 0 Sell, average 12-month
target ~$10.83 (vs. the post-drop ~$7.53 close, i.e. real implied
upside) — but that consensus predates the succession news and hasn't
been re-tested against it yet.

## Risks
- Founder/CEO transitions are genuine execution-continuity risk,
  especially at a company whose entire brand and go-to-market has been
  identified with its founder; the market's -12% reaction is a real
  signal, not obviously an overreaction to fade.
- Insider selling immediately before a leadership-change announcement
  is a red flag on timing/optics, even without evidence of wrongdoing.
- No dated near-term (1-2 week) catalyst that would resolve the
  uncertainty in this project's trading window — the new CEO doesn't
  start until 11/2, over a month out.
- Small/mid-cap name (thinner liquidity than a large-cap) — moves can be
  choppier than the headline analyst targets suggest.
