## Step 0: prior context
No prior entry for TWST in trade_ledger.md — first appearance. lessons.md

## Catalyst
Twist Bioscience (synthetic DNA/gene-synthesis + antibody discovery
platform) rose +16.1% on 09-24 ($158.50 -> $184.03) on a genuine,
same-day, company-specific catalyst: Twist announced a strategic deal to
supply antibody characterization data and services to **Eli Lilly's
AI/ML drug-discovery platform, TuneLab**, feeding lab data into Lilly's
models. This is dated 09-24-2026 itself (not a stale, weeks-old item),
confirmed across multiple independent outlets (StocksToTrade,
TimothySykes, TradingKey). Same-day, Leerink raised its price target on
TWST from $120 to $160 with an Outperform rating, explicitly tying the
raise to the AI drug-discovery opportunity — a real, dated, same-day
analyst action reinforcing (not standing alone as) the company-news
catalyst, so this doesn't fall into lessons.md #5's weaker
"analyst-action-only" category.

Important context: TWST was already in a strong multi-week uptrend
before today — from $124.72 on 09-04 to $158.50 by 09-23 (six straight
green days, +24% per Trefis as of 09-18), so today's +16% is a fresh
catalyst adding to an already-extended run, not the first leg of a move.
The company has also disclosed that pilot work on AI-designed
mini-binder proteins for Anthropic is expected to be reflected in its
FY2027 orders outlook — a second, related AI-platform data point in the
same vein as the Lilly news, adding some credibility that this is a
genuine emerging revenue theme (AI drug-discovery data supply) rather
than a one-off headline.

## Sentiment
Bullish, and better-grounded than the other three tickers researched
today: real company-specific news (a named partner, Eli Lilly, and a
named platform, TuneLab), dated to the move itself, plus a same-day
analyst price-target increase citing that same news, in a large-cap-ish,
liquid biotech (~$184/share, not a thin-float microcap).

## Risks
- **Extended-chart / already-running risk (lessons.md #1)**: the stock is
  up roughly +47% over three weeks into today's news, not starting from a
  flat base — a "buy the dip" entry here would be well above where the
  move started, and momentum names giving back a stretched run is a
  repeatedly observed pattern in this project's own history (DOCU, CHPT,
  MSTR, NX, TARS). This is the single biggest reason for caution despite
  the catalyst being genuinely fresh and real.
  - This is a deal for services (data supply), not disclosed with specific
  contract dollar figures found via WebSearch — real revenue impact size
  is unconfirmed, so magnitude of the fundamental change is uncertain
  relative to the size of the stock move.
- Company's own quote characterizes the Anthropic-related mini-binder
  work as only "probably sort of" already baked into FY2027 guidance —
  a hedge, not a firm confirmed guidance raise.
- Next earnings not until ~Nov 13, 2026 (per company's historical
  cadence) — that's outside this project's ~2-week horizon, but a
  reminder there's no near-term print to further confirm or reset the
  thesis before then.
- Biotech/genomics-tools names carry general sector sentiment risk
  (funding environment, NIH/biotech-budget headlines) independent of
  company-specific news.
