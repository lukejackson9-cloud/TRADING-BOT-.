# ACAD — 2026-09-25

## Catalyst
ACADIA Pharmaceuticals (NASDAQ: ACAD) fell from $25.43 to $22.18 (-12.78%)
on 2026-09-24 after announcing topline Phase 2 RADIANT study results for
remlifanserin in Alzheimer's disease psychosis (ADP) that narrowly MISSED
the primary endpoint: the 60mg once-daily dose showed a change of -12.6 vs.
-10.4 for placebo on the SAPS-H+D scale at week 6, p=0.0603 — just above
the 0.05 significance threshold. The key secondary endpoint (CGI-S-ADP
severity scale) did hit nominal significance (p=0.0077). This is a real,
dated, company-specific catalyst, clearly confirmed and consistent across
multiple sources including the company's own press release.

## Sentiment
Bearish reaction on the day, but not a full risk-off verdict from the
sell-side: Needham cut its price target to $35 from $40 while KEEPING a
Buy rating; Citizens cut to $33 while keeping "Market Outperform." CEO
Catherine Owen Adams framed the data as "highly Phase III enabling."
Acadia plans to drop the weaker 30mg arm and continue Phase 3 with 60mg,
with full efficacy/safety data due at the CTAD conference November
16-19, 2026 — nearly two months out, well beyond this project's ~2-week
trading horizon.

## Risks
- This is a negative binary-trial-readout catalyst, not a setup for a new
  long entry — the reaction already happened (price already fell) and this
  project does not short (Hard Risk Rules: no shorting, T212 equity API is
  long-only), so there is no directional trade this catalyst supports.
- No near-term (within-horizon) positive catalyst: the next real data point
  (CTAD) is ~8 weeks away, outside the ~2-week trading window this project
  uses.
- Genuine binary/regulatory uncertainty remains on the primary endpoint
  miss — analysts kept Buy ratings but meaningfully cut targets, signaling
  real (not just cosmetic) reduced confidence.
- Nothing in this research contradicts the trial result itself (multiple
  independent sources, including Acadia's own release, agree on the
  numbers), so this isn't a WebSearch-reliability case per lessons.md #2 —
  the facts are solid, the trade opportunity just isn't there.
