## Risks
- **Material dilution overhang tied directly to the very event the
  market is reportedly anticipating**: per Surrozen's 2025 PIPE financing
  (SEC 424B5, March 2025), the second tranche is contingent specifically
  on public announcement of FDA clearance of the SZN-8141 IND on or
  before **October 31, 2026** — upon that trigger, the company must issue
  ~6.0M shares of common stock, pre-funded warrants for up to 2.5M more
  shares, and Series E warrants for up to 4.2M additional shares. This
  means the exact catalyst bulls are reportedly pricing in (FDA
  clearance) simultaneously triggers a large, contractually-fixed
  dilution event — a structural "the catalyst is also the dilution
  trigger" setup, not a clean re-rating.
- No new dated news found for 09-23/09-24 specifically — high risk this
  is an unexplained speculative spike that can reverse as fast as it
  built, especially in a small/thin-float name (+108% in one session).
- SZN-8141 itself is still pre-clinical-stage regulatory (IND submission,
  not clearance yet) — actual data readout not expected until 2H 2027
  per the company's own timeline; a long way from a value-confirming
  event.
- Company has a history of dilutive financing (steep FY2025 net loss
  driven by financing charges per its 10-K).
