## Risks
- **Sell-the-news / already-extended entry (lessons.md #1)**: the reaction
  to both catalysts has already fully fired by the time this research is
  being done — the stock closed the session AT a fresh 52-week high after
  an 11%+ one-day pop. Per this project's own repeatedly-evidenced pattern
  (backtest gap-confirmation test, AEHR/CHPT/MSTR/NX/TARS discretionary
  cases), buying right after a big catalyst-driven gap tends to be a worse
  entry, not a better one. This is the single biggest risk here — a real,
  strong catalyst but likely already priced in for a 2-week horizon.
  (Lessons.md's counter-example on AEHR is noted too: a live, ongoing
  narrative CAN still run further — this cuts both ways, not a clean
  reason to dismiss the setup outright, but not a reason to ignore the
  extension either.)
- Preliminary FY2028 guidance is management's own forward-looking framing,
  18+ months out — not yet-realized results; guidance beats/raises can
  still disappoint on execution.
- S&P 500 inclusion is a one-time mechanical flow event (passive fund
  buying), not a recurring tailwind — once absorbed, that specific support
  is gone.
- No confirmation yet on whether this triggers a broader
  data-storage/infrastructure sector move (would raise a lessons.md #3
  correlation concern) — WebSearch results here were Everpure-specific,
  no evidence of sector-wide co-movement found in this pass, but not
  exhaustively checked either.
