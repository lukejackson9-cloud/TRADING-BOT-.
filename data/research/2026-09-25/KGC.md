# KGC — 2026-09-25

## Identity check
KGC = Kinross Gold Corporation, a real senior gold miner. Confirmed.

## Catalyst
Real, dated, and COMPANY-SPECIFIC — this is NOT simply gold-price sector
beta (gold itself was only down ~0.3% same day per spot-price data). On
2026-09-23, Kinross issued an operational update cutting 2026-2027
attributable production guidance to 1.84-1.86M gold-equivalent ounces
annually — 2-3% below the low end of prior guidance, ~8% below the prior
midpoint — while also raising all-in-sustaining-cost guidance to
$1,850-$1,900/oz. Multiple analysts cut price targets in response. Stock
fell -12.7% intraday, -11.6% close-to-close ($27.62 -> $24.42) on 09-24.

## Sentiment
Bearish, real, and company-specific — a genuine guidance cut, not a
sector-wide drag as the broader gold/silver weakness that day might
suggest at first glance.

## Risks
This is a negative catalyst on a long-only project — not actionable
regardless of how genuine the news is. Worth noting precisely because it
would have been easy to misattribute this move to the broader
gold-price/dollar-strength weakness reported the same day (lessons.md
#3's discipline applied correctly here: checked and ruled out the
sector-wide explanation before accepting the company-specific one).
