fell -36.6% on 09-24 ($11.45 -> $7.26), the single biggest mover in
today's pool. This has a clean, dated, company-specific catalyst: the

posted a Q4 net loss attributable to common shareholders of $199K
(-$0.02/share) despite the top-line beat — a classic "growth without
profitability" miss that punishes the stock even on a revenue beat.
Separately, effective 09-16-2026 TRT moved its listing from NYSE American
to the Nasdaq Global Market — a housekeeping event, not the driver of
this specific move.
