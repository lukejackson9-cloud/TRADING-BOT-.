# COHR — 2026-09-15

## Catalyst
COHR fell -12.73% on 09-14/09-15, part of the optical/semiconductor
decline cluster. Two overlapping, non-company-specific drivers were
confirmed:
1. The same weekend AI-safety essay/warnings (Amodei, Altman) that drove
   the cybersecurity rally on the other side of the trade also drove
   money OUT of AI-buildout-exposed hardware, including optical
   components.
2. Direct sympathy from Corning's (GLW) 09-11 SEC filing for a $2B ATM
   equity program: a 24/7 Wall St piece explicitly groups the reaction —
   "Corning Tumbles 12%... Coherent Sinks 11%, Lumentum Drops 9%,
   Fabrinet Slides 6%" — and a Yahoo/24/7WallSt companion piece explains
   why COHR tracks GLW specifically: the two companies monetize
   different components of the same AI data-center optical links
   (Corning supplies the passive layer — fiber, cable, connectors;
   Coherent supplies active devices — lasers, photodiodes, 800G/1.6T
   transceivers). GLW's dilutive news reads through to COHR as a peer/
   supply-chain read-across, not as new information about COHR itself.

No COHR-specific negative news was found. The only COHR-specific item
surfaced was a routine CFO share sale (Sherri Luther, 3,000 shares,
09-04) — immaterial in size and timing, not a plausible driver of a
09-14 move ten days later.

## Sentiment
Bearish, but this reads as pure sympathy/correlation on two fronts at
once (macro AI-safety rotation + Corning-specific dilution read-across)
with zero independent COHR catalyst underneath either. This is the
weakest case of the four tickers reviewed today for treating the move as
company-specific — closest to the XP/STNE/PAGS and RUN/CSIQ pattern in
lessons.md #3, except here there are two stacked correlated drivers
instead of one.

## Risks
- No independent catalyst to hang either a bull or bear thesis on; this
  project is long-only, so a sympathy-driven decline with no
  company-specific news is not actionable regardless.
- If GLW's ATM-driven weakness or the broader AI-safety rotation
  reverses, COHR could bounce on the same sympathy basis it fell on —
  but that would still not be a company-specific catalyst if it happens.
- Real underlying business fundamentals (AI datacenter transceiver
  demand) are reported as fine per background chatter; the move is
  sentiment/flow-driven, not fundamentals-driven, which cuts against
  reading too much into either direction.
