# YSS — 2026-09-15

## Catalyst
On 2026-09-10, York Space Systems, Inc. (YSS, NYSE) — confirmed correct
company name and ticker; not "Yotta," which is an unrelated
data-center/AI company sometimes confused with this ticker — announced
it was selected as prime contractor to build the initial phase of
Tomorrow.io's "DeepSky" next-generation weather-monitoring satellite
constellation, a contract reported at $187M. Confirmed via York's own
BusinessWire release and independent trade press (Via Satellite). This
is real, dated, and company-specific.

## Sentiment — has the reaction already played out? (5-day check)
The catalyst is real, but it has been completely swamped by a much
larger, ongoing structural collapse in the stock — this is close to a
textbook "one positive item can't overcome the negative fundamental
picture" case (lessons.md #5), compounded by an active legal-risk red
flag (lessons.md #6 pattern, same shape as VRNS's active securities
fraud lawsuit):
- YSS IPO'd in January 2026 at $34.00/share and has since fallen ~73%.
- In mid-August 2026 the company cut FY26 revenue guidance by roughly
  32% (from a prior $545-595M range down to $375-405M), citing
  government contract delays and supply-chain issues.
- That guidance cut triggered a wave of analyst downgrades: JPMorgan
  Overweight -> Neutral with target cut from $38 to $17; Goldman Sachs
  also slashed its target roughly in half; at least six analysts
  lowered targets ~54% on average since 08-14.
- A CFO departure was also reported around the same period.
- On 2026-09-14 (one day before this note) a securities class-action
  lawsuit was announced (Levi Korsinsky), alleging York made materially
  false or misleading statements about the functionality/readiness of
  its satellite software and the maturity of its "modular" platform —
  an active, unresolved legal overhang, not a resolved or historical
  matter.
- Price action confirms the catalyst did essentially nothing: the stock
  hit an all-time low of $8.38 on 2026-09-02 (BEFORE this catalyst),
  the mean-reversion paper-trade signal tagged with this catalyst
  entered at $7.88 on 2026-09-11 (i.e. the stock kept falling even
  after the 09-10 contract news), and as of today (2026-09-15) it
  trades at $8.63, down -2.15% just today, down -8.52% over the past
  week and -43.35% over the past month.
- TradingView aggregated TA consensus: SELL (6 buy / 12 sell / 6
  neutral) — agrees with the deteriorating picture, not the catalyst.

## Risks
- Active, unresolved securities class-action lawsuit alleging
  misrepresentation of core product readiness — a direct threat to the
  investment thesis, not a side issue.
- Guidance cut of ~32% and a CFO departure both post-date and dwarf this
  contract win in financial materiality.
- Stock in a severe, still-active downtrend (-77% over the past year);
  a single $187M contract (against FY26 revenue guidance of
  $375-405M) is real but not large enough to reverse that trajectory on
  its own, and the market has evidently not treated it as such.
- Thin credibility bar: after a "modular platform" misrepresentation
  allegation, further contract-win headlines warrant extra scrutiny
  rather than being taken at face value.
