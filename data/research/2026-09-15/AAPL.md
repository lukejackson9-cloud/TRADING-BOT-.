# AAPL — 2026-09-15

## Catalyst
On 2026-09-09 ("Surprise and Shine" event) Apple unveiled its first foldable
iPhone, the "Duo," starting at $1,999, alongside the iPhone 18 Pro. Morgan
Stanley estimated ~$14B in December-quarter revenue contribution from the
Duo (7-8M units H2-2026, up to 20M over the first product cycle).
Catalyst-tagged on 2026-09-11 for the paper-trading pass off a +1.75%
close / +3.6% intraday move that day.

**4 days later, this has already fully played out, not something still
building.** Price trajectory: ~$316 pre-event → $326.57 close 9/10
(+3.56%) → ~$335.49 intraday 9/11 (the tagged day) → $333.08 close 9/14.
That's flat-to-slightly-down over the last 3 sessions after the initial
pop — the reaction happened over 9/9-9/11 and has since stalled/
consolidated near the highs rather than continuing to run. Morningstar
did nudge its fair-value estimate up ($285→$290) to reflect the Duo, but
that's a valuation-model update, not a new dated trigger.

The one thing that COULD be a genuine forward catalyst — actual preorder
demand data — hasn't happened yet: Ming-Chi Kuo's supply-chain read (as
of 9/13) suggests preorders may not even open until Q4 2026, an estimate,
not a confirmed date. Per screen.md's rule on pre-catalyst tickers, an
unconfirmed future date is not a documented catalyst to trade on today.

## Sentiment
Mixed-to-positive but calm. TradingView aggregate rating: BUY (15 buy /
9 neutral / 2 sell across ~26 indicators) — consistent with a stock
holding recent gains, not screaming momentum. Some cautionary commentary
exists in parallel (a Yahoo piece specifically arguing the foldable
"won't send the stock soaring," an earlier April 2026 report of foldable
delays, ongoing supply-chain-shortage chatter) — sentiment is constructive
but not one-sided.

## Risks
- Classic "sell the news" pattern (lessons.md #1): the +3.6%
  catalyst-day pop is 4 days old and has already stalled near the highs
  rather than extending — buying now is not buying ahead of the reaction,
  it's buying after it.
- Production/supply constraints repeatedly flagged (only a few hundred
  units/day possible at actual launch per some reports vs. Apple's usual
  tens-of-millions cadence) — a real risk to the $14B estimate actually
  materializing on the assumed timeline.
- AAPL is a $4.85T mega-cap; a single product catalyst, even a large one,
  has limited ability to move the stock meaningfully within this
  project's ~2-week horizon — most of the "story" gets priced in within
  days, consistent with what's already been observed here.
- No fresh, CONFIRMED dated event in the next 1-2 weeks (preorder date
  still unconfirmed).
