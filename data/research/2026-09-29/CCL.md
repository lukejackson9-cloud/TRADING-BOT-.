# CCL — 2026-09-29

## Step 0: prior context
Pre-catalyst watch entry since 2026-09-24 (earnings originally flagged for
09-29). Earnings printed today as scheduled — per research.md's rule, the
print has now happened, so this moves off the automatic WATCH cap and
gets evaluated on the actual reaction like any other ticker.

## Catalyst
Real, dated, resolved, and genuinely strong: Carnival reported Q3 FY2026
results today (09-29) — non-GAAP EPS of $1.43 (beat $1.35 estimate by
$0.08), record revenue of $8.43-8.44B, an all-time-high net income of
$1.9B, despite absorbing $131M of adverse fuel/currency impact. Management
raised full-year adjusted EPS guidance to $2.24, citing booking trends
outpacing capacity growth. Record Q3 customer deposits (+~7% YoY on flat
capacity), and 2027 booked occupancy/pricing are both at record levels —
this is forward-looking strength, not just a backward-looking beat.

## Sentiment
Strongly bullish and broadly confirmed by sell-side: stock surged
+11-13% intraday, closing around $25.11 (+13.43%). Multiple analysts
raised price targets same-day, most clustering well ABOVE the post-pop
price: Stifel $34→$38, Barclays $33→$37, Melius $30→$36, JPMorgan
$34→$39 — all comfortably above today's ~$25 close, a meaningfully
different picture from several recent council reviews (TWST, KOD) where
even the freshest targets barely cleared the post-pop price. Two firms
went the other way (TD Cowen $34→$32 on fuel-cost concerns, Goldman
Sachs $35→$30 on yield/demand-weakness concerns) — real, dated dissent,
not just noise, though even Goldman's reduced target is still above the
close.

## Risks
- **Reaction already fired same-day (lessons.md #1)**: a +13% single-
  session pop on the earnings print itself means entry now is buying
  after the move, not ahead of it — the standard caution that has
  downgraded every CANDIDATE this project has evaluated to date applies
  here too.
- Real, dated bearish dissent exists alongside the bullish reaction (TD
  Cowen and Goldman both cut targets same-day on fuel-cost/yield
  concerns) — the "record quarter" framing isn't unanimous even among
  bulls-leaning coverage.
- Cruise lines carry real macro exposure (fuel costs, discretionary-
  spending sensitivity, geopolitical/travel-disruption risk) independent
  of this quarter's results.
- Stock is still down >25% YTD per pre-earnings coverage — today's pop is
  a partial recovery within a weak year, not a name making fresh all-time
  highs; some of the "cheap relative to target" framing may reflect that
  depressed starting point as much as newly bullish conviction.
