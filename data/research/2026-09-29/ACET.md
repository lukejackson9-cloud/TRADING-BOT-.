# ACET — 2026-09-29

## Identity check
ACET = Adicet Bio, Inc., a clinical-stage cell-therapy biotech. Confirmed
real company.

## Catalyst
Real, dated, and genuinely positive: on 2026-09-28 Adicet reported Phase 1
data for prula-cel in 22 systemic lupus erythematosus patients (16 with
lupus nephritis) at a webcast. At 12 months, 50% of evaluable lupus
nephritis patients achieved complete renal response and 54% reached DORIS
remission, with most remaining off immunosuppressants. Company plans a
pivotal lupus nephritis trial starting late 2026 with stated FDA
alignment. Stock fell today (09-29, -29.60% close-to-close per Massive),
the OPPOSITE direction from what this genuinely positive-sounding data
would suggest.

## Sentiment
Confusing/mixed: the clinical data itself reads positively (renal
response, remission rates, favorable safety, a stated pivotal-trial
pathway with FDA alignment), yet the stock cratered nearly 30% the
following session. Wall Street Zen raised its rating from Sell to Hold on
09-26 (2-3 days before the data, a small positive signal that didn't
predict today's reaction). This combination — good-sounding data,
negative price reaction — suggests the market saw something in the full
data set (magnitude of effect vs. expectations, durability, a small
sample size of 22 patients, or a "sell the news"/priced-in dynamic from a
run-up into the readout) that isn't captured in the top-line PR framing.

## Risks
- **Unexplained divergence between the reported data and the price
  reaction** is itself the central risk here — entering on the assumption
  the PR framing is the full picture, when the market's own reaction
  suggests otherwise, would be a mistake. This needs a closer, more
  skeptical read of the actual dataset (control-arm comparison if any,
  effect-size context, durability data) before treating "50% complete
  renal response" as clearly good news.
- Small sample (22 patients, 16 with lupus nephritis) — Phase 1 data at
  this size carries wide confidence intervals.
- Pivotal trial start is "late 2026," a plan not yet underway.
