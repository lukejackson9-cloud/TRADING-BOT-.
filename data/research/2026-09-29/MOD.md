# MOD — 2026-09-29

## Catalyst
Modine Manufacturing (thermal management systems; identity confirmed) fell
-11.63% on 2026-09-28. This is a **mechanical/structural corporate-action
effect, not a fresh operating-business catalyst**: 2026-09-28 was the
confirmed record date for Modine's previously-announced Reverse Morris
Trust spin-off of its Performance Technologies business (via "Platinum
SpinCo Inc.") and its immediate merger into Gentherm. Each Modine
shareholder of record gets one SpinCo share per Modine share held;
distribution is expected 2026-10-01, with the SpinCo/Gentherm merger
completing the same day, plus a related ~$1.90/share special dividend on
the Gentherm side. A stock mechanically re-rating down around the record
date of a spin-off (the market pricing out the value of the business unit
that is about to be distributed to holders separately) is expected
distribution mechanics, not new bad news about Modine's remaining
business.
