# NU — 2026-09-29

## Catalyst
Nu Holdings (Brazilian/LatAm digital bank, Nubank) fell 10.01% on
2026-09-28 ($13.59 → $12.23) on reports that it is in early-stage talks
to acquire UK digital bank Monzo for roughly £8-10B (~$10.6-13.3B) — a
real, dated, company-specific event (confirmed across multiple
independent outlets: TipRanks, Investing.com, Motley Fool, FX Leaders,
TradingKey), not a rumor recycled from earlier in the year (no prior
Monzo-Nu chatter found in this project's history or in current search
results). No deal has been agreed; reporting is consistent that talks
are still early. Nu lost ~$6.6B of market value in the session — a
disproportionate reaction relative to the (still unconfirmed) deal size,
reflecting investor concern about price and financing more than the
deal being confirmed. Nu also posted a record Q2 2026 (~$5.9B revenue,
~$1.1B net income, 33% ROE) — underlying fundamentals are not in
question, this is entirely an M&A-uncertainty catalyst.

## Sentiment
Mixed, leaning bearish on the immediate reaction but with a real bull
counter-case. Investors sold off on legitimate concerns: the reported
price tag (£8-10B) is roughly double Monzo's 2024 private valuation
(~£4.5B), a deal could be financed with cash + Nu stock (real dilution
risk for existing holders), and it's unclear the return would justify
the price. Needham (Kyle Peterson) pushed back hard against the
sell-off, keeping a Buy rating and $19 target (would imply ~55% upside
from the post-drop price) and calling the reaction overdone — but this
is a single analyst's contrarian take, not confirmed fundamental
news reversing the fear (lessons.md #5: weigh a lone analyst call below
company-reported facts). The deal itself, if it proceeds on the
reported terms, is a real structural risk (large, dilutive, unproven
strategic fit); if it doesn't proceed or is renegotiated smaller, the
sell-off could prove overdone.

## Risks
- Core catalyst is an unconfirmed, early-stage rumor, not a signed deal
  — genuinely binary and unresolved, similar in shape (though not in
  recycled-narrative history) to the VRNS Proofpoint-talks case that was
  downgraded at council for exactly this kind of unresolved M&A
  overhang (lessons.md #6 is about repeat rumors specifically, which
  this isn't, but the "unconfirmed talks = binary risk" logic still
  applies).
- Real dilution risk if the deal proceeds as reported (cash + stock).
- No dated resolution point inside a 2-week window — Nu's own Investor
  Day isn't until 2026-12-08, well outside this project's holding
  horizon.
- Sources give slightly different day-over-day move percentages
  (10.01% vs. 8.87% on TradingKey) — direction and rough magnitude are
  consistent, exact figure isn't (lessons.md #2).
