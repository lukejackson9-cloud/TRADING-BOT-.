## Fact-check notes
Independently verified the bear case's four most load-bearing claims
before weighing this review, since they materially affect the read and
the bull case's framing did not surface most of them:
1. **Prior 2025 deal voided — CONFIRMED.** ORSNA's board formally voided
   a preliminary agreement signed 2025-09-26 and ordered new talks
   2026-06-10 (Aviacionline, Boletín Oficial, Businesswire). This is a
   genuine instability precedent for the same concession, not routine
   multi-stage process, as the bear case argued.
2. **Q2 2026 EPS miss ($0.32 vs. $0.51 Zacks consensus, -37.26% surprise)
   — CONFIRMED**, revenue beat also confirmed. The bull case's
   "fundamentals were already solid going in" section cited revenue
   growth and leverage but never mentioned this EPS miss — a material,
   verified omission that weakens (does not invalidate) the bull case's
   fundamentals framing.
3. **Concession term discrepancy — PARTIALLY CONFIRMED, and this is the
   single most important correction.** The official administrative
   decision sets a FIRM, unconditional end date of February 2049.
   Extension to 2056 is CONDITIONAL: Aeropuertos Argentina must prove
   financing for a $600M Ezeiza investment by June 30, 2027, and reach
   75% completion on related works before 2031, with ORSNA determining
   compliance. Both the bull case and CAAP's own Businesswire headline
   describe "extended to 2056" without foregrounding this conditionality
   — the bull case's central catalyst framing overstates a firm 18-year
   extension that is actually a firm 12-year extension (2037→2049) plus
   a conditional further 7 years contingent on a 2027 milestone not yet
   met. (2066, appearing in one pre-announcement report, is not in the
   final decision at all — a reporting artifact, not a live term.)
4. **JPMorgan target cut ($33→$32, 9/18) and Zacks downgrade to Strong
   Sell — CONFIRMED** (Zacks date corrected to 9/23, not 9/21 as
   loosely stated). Both real, both bearish, both dated in the ten days
   immediately before this catalyst — sell-side sentiment was
   deteriorating into the news, not building toward it.

Net effect of fact-checking: every bear claim checked out, and the one
correction (2066 isn't real) doesn't help the bull case — it replaces a
seemingly bigger number with confirmation that even "2056" itself is
conditional, not the firm win the bull case and company press release
framing implied.
