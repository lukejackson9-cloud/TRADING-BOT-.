## Identity check
VEEE = Twin Vee PowerCats, Co. — a real, small operating boat
manufacturer (catamarans, AquaSport V-hulls) founded 1996, IPO'd 2021.
However, it is currently mid-transaction on a reverse merger with USFM
Corporation (announced July 2026): Twin Vee's actual boat
business is being carved out into a separate PRIVATE company (existing
public shareholders get only a non-transferable Contingent Value Right
to future distributions from it, not equity), while the public entity
merges into USFM and Twin Vee holders end up with just ~10% of the
combined company. A previously approved 1-for-37 reverse stock split
and a rename to "Twin Vee Bahama Co." are also part of this process.
This is a company in the middle of restructuring itself into something
almost entirely different from what "VEEE" has historically represented.
