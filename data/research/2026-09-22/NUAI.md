## Identity check
NUAI = New Era Energy & Digital, Inc. (formerly New Era Helium, Inc.,
Nasdaq: NEHC, renamed/re-ticker'd August 2025). Real, established
Nasdaq-listed company, headquartered in Midland, TX, with legacy
Permian Basin helium/natural gas assets, now pivoting into AI
data-center infrastructure. Genuine operating company, not a bare
shell — but note it needed a Nasdaq-compliance rescue as recently as
October 2025, i.e. it was a distressed small-cap before this pivot
narrative took hold.

## Catalyst
The pivot centers on the Texas Critical Data Centers (TCDC) campus
(up to ~1–1.4 GW capacity) in Ector County, TX. September 2026 coverage
cites a 20-year Vistra power deal tied to this Texas AI data-center
buildout, plus general "clean-tech expansion" news, as the driver
behind the run from the low $4s into the $6–7 range (closing $7.65,
+30.55%, around 09-21). Leadership was also overhauled in July 2026
with hires from Microsoft/AWS/TikTok infrastructure backgrounds, which
the market appears to be reading as execution credibility layered on
top of the pivot story.

## Sentiment
Seeking Alpha frames NUAI as a "Speculative Buy" on the AI power
bottleneck theme, explicitly contingent on securing anchor tenants,
financing, and moving from concept to construction — none confirmed
complete in this search pass. Balance sheet reportedly carries ~$84.8M
cash, giving some runway despite "steep losses." No bearish coverage
surfaced.

## Risks
- Pre-revenue-pivot, development-stage infrastructure story — the
  Speculative Buy thesis itself is conditional on milestones not yet
  achieved (anchor tenants, financing, construction start).
- The stock had already run over "weeks" (low $4s to upper $6s/$7s)
  before this report date — per lessons.md #1, part of the AI-pivot
  re-rating may already be priced in, even if the Vistra deal itself is
  a genuine incremental catalyst.
- Company's recent history (helium/oil-and-gas junior needing a
  Nasdaq-compliance rescue as recently as Oct 2025) is a real
  distress precedent, not just an unrelated old story.
- Execution and dilution risk explicitly flagged by outside coverage.
- Could not confirm from this search pass that the Vistra deal itself
  (vs. general momentum) falls precisely within the 09-18→09-21 window
  — treat the exact dating of the incremental catalyst as unconfirmed.
