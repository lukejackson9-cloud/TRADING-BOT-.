## Catalyst
Real and dated, but explicitly UNRESOLVED as of today. The FDA's
Molecular and Clinical Genetics Panel is scheduled to meet on
2026-09-23 — tomorrow — to review, discuss, and vote (non-binding
recommendation to the FDA) on GRAIL's premarket approval application for
Galleri. Today's +33.68% move (close $107.97, up from the high-$70s) was
driven by the FDA's own briefing documents, released ahead of the
meeting, which reportedly did not flag major accuracy or safety issues —
read by the market as a positive signal ahead of the vote itself. The
actual panel vote, and any FDA action following it, has NOT happened
yet.

## Sentiment
Bullish, but this is pre-event positioning on a still-binary outcome,
not a reaction to a resolved result. The briefing docs are a genuine,
positive data point (no major accuracy/safety flags), but the panel
could still vote unfavorably, request more data, or raise a concern not
evident from the briefing materials alone tomorrow. Standalone metrics
cited in reporting (99.6% specificity, PPV up to 77%, "moderate"
sensitivity) are real but is not the same as a completed favorable
recommendation.
