## Identity check
GRML = Greenland Mines Ltd. This is NOT a stable, established mining
operator — it is the former **Klotho Neurosciences, Inc.**, a cell/gene
therapy biotech (ALS/Alzheimer's programs), which completed a reverse
merger with Greenland Mines Corp in March 2026 and rebranded/re-ticker'd
to GRML effective 2026-03-12. So this identity is six months old. Deal
was structured entirely in convertible preferred stock; former Greenland
Mines holders ended up with ~93% of the fully diluted shares. This is
exactly the "shell rebrand into a hot theme" pattern the project treats
with extreme scrutiny.

## Catalyst
Two things compounding: (1) a **geopolitical, non-company-specific**
catalyst — President Trump unveiled a "permanent" US security agreement
with Denmark over Greenland, which moved GRML, CRML, and GLND together
overnight/premarket; (2) company-specific PR: a completed $35M Sarfartoq
acquisition, a new 262 sq km exploration license application, and an
independent Initial Assessment claiming a high-case pre-tax NPV up to
$2.05B / 118.6% IRR — but that same study explicitly confirms **no
reserves yet**, with permitting, financing, and execution all still
outstanding hurdles. Price went from $2.85 close (09-18) to intraday
$11.68 before closing $9.42 (09-21), a >230% move.

## Risks
- Thin-float pump signature: ~218M shares volume against a company that
  was a small biotech shell six months ago — implies an extremely thin
  float being violently traded, matching the project's BIAF precedent
  (see screen.md).
- The dominant catalyst (Greenland security deal) is sector-wide/
  geopolitical, not GRML-specific — it moved at least 3 tickers together
  (GRML, CRML, GLND) per lessons.md #3.
- The company's own resource study admits no reserves; NPV/IRR figures
  use "favourable sensitivities."
- Six-month-old identity via reverse merger from an unrelated biotech —
  no operating track record as a mining company.
- Extreme intraday range ($7.33–$11.68 on 09-21 alone) signals violent,
  low-quality price discovery, not an orderly re-rating.
