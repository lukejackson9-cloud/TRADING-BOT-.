## Catalyst
Real, dated, and company-specific — but it is the tail end of a
long-running, already fully public merger process, not a fresh surprise.
On 2026-09-21, Paramount Skydance reached a settlement with a coalition
of 12 state attorneys general who had sued to block Paramount's
acquisition of WBD, agreeing to behavioral concessions (maintaining
theatrical releases, $1.5B in U.S. production spend commitments, CNN
editorial-independence protections) in exchange for not having to
divest WBD's cable networks. This removes the last major outstanding
legal obstacle to a deal that: WBD shareholders already approved
(04-23-26), the DOJ already cleared without conditions (06-12-26), and
the FCC already signed off on (Middle East sovereign-fund equity
backing). The deal is now expected to close between late September and
December 2026, with one source citing an anticipated ~10-01 close.
Sources disagree on the exact deal value ($81B in one Wikipedia
reference vs. $110-111B in others) — treated as an unresolved precision
issue per lessons.md #2, not adjudicated here since it isn't decision-
relevant to the verdict below.

## Risks
- **Capped upside, not a momentum setup**: this project's strategy
  targets +8% profit-take / -4% stop over up to a 5-day-to-2-week
  window. A stock trading ~99.4% of its all-cash-or-cash+stock deal
  price has essentially no room to deliver an +8% move from here without
  the deal terms themselves changing — this is structurally a merger-arb
  situation, not a catalyst-momentum one, regardless of how "real" the
  catalyst is.
- Deal still hasn't formally closed — a low-probability but nonzero risk
