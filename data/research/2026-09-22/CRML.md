## Identity check
CRML = Critical Metals Corp — a real, established (not a fresh
shell/rebrand) Nasdaq-listed rare-earth/critical-minerals exploration
company. Holds the Tanbreez rare-earth exploration permit in Greenland
(the only such license in the region) with a 15-year offtake agreement
with REalloys, and is separately pursuing an all-share acquisition of
European Lithium (Australian scheme meetings underway, targeted
completion ~November 2026). Materially cleaner identity than GRML —
this is a genuine, years-old operating exploration company, not a
recent reverse-merger shell.

## Catalyst
+38.6% on 09-21 to a three-month high, driven by: (1) the same Trump
administration/Denmark Greenland security-agreement news that also
moved GRML and GLND — a geopolitical, sector-wide catalyst, not
CRML-specific; and (2) a new study outlining a proposed Romanian rare-
earth refinery targeting ~$1.8–2.2B annual revenue against ~$1.85B
build cost — still at the study/proposal stage, not a funded, committed
project.

## Sentiment
Bullish momentum commentary (Gurufocus, Timothy Sykes, StocksToTrade)
framing the Greenland deal as legitimizing Tanbreez's strategic
importance. No bearish counter-coverage surfaced in this pass.

## Risks
- Company remains unprofitable and cash-flow negative (pre-revenue
  exploration stage); P/S is the only meaningful valuation metric cited,
  P/E doesn't apply.
- The dominant driver of the move is a geopolitical/sector-wide
  catalyst that moved multiple tickers (GRML, CRML, GLND) together —
  per lessons.md #3, if this and GRML were both to reach council this
  should be flagged as one correlated Greenland/rare-earth bet, not two
  independent ideas.
- The Romanian refinery is a study/proposal, not committed or funded —
  meaningful execution and financing risk before any revenue.
- 58.4M shares volume is high relative to a small exploration company's
  likely float — some momentum/pump character present, though less
  extreme than GRML given the real, longer-standing operating identity
  and named assets/offtake partner.
