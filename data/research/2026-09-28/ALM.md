# ALM — 2026-09-28

## Catalyst
Confirmed identity: Almonty Industries Inc. (NASDAQ: ALM), a Canadian
tungsten mining company with the Sangdong mine in South Korea and Los
Santos in Spain — matches the ticker description given (not to be
confused with ALMU/Aeluma, a different ticker already logged separately
in config/watchlist.txt on 2026-09-21). ALM rose +10.65% on 2026-09-25
($12.40 → $13.72). The proximate, same-day trigger is a real, dated
catalyst: Stifel initiated coverage on 2026-09-25 with a Buy rating and a
$25 price target, framing Almonty as positioned to become the leading
Western tungsten producer by 2028 against a backdrop of a ~775% tungsten
price surge since early 2025 (China export restrictions). This sits on
top of several other real, dated, company-specific developments in the
prior two weeks: a Rwanda government joint venture securing a tungsten
concession/processing license (2026-09-14), a multi-year take-or-pay
offtake agreement with Sandvik subsidiary Wolfram Bergbau und Hütten AG
covering the Los Santos (Spain) site (2026-09-17, ≥1,720 tonnes WO3, a
conditional $3.0M upfront payment), Sangdong's processing/crushing
facilities clearing for commercial operation, and a board-authorized
buyback of up to 14.4M shares / $300M over 36 months.

## Sentiment
Bullish, driven primarily by a single fresh sell-side initiation
(Stifel) layered on genuinely real operational progress. Per lessons.md
#5, a single analyst action — even a real one — is weaker evidence on
its own than company fundamentals; here it's reinforced by actual
operational milestones (Sangdong ramp, signed offtake deal, JV), which
is a stronger combination than a bare upgrade alone.

## Risks
- **Valuation already extreme**: GuruFocus pegs the stock at ~170% above
  its own GF Value ($13.99 vs. $5.18) even before accounting for the
  9/25 pop to $13.72 — an aggressive re-rating already priced well ahead
  of current fundamentals, resting on a 2028 production target.
- The Rwanda JV (9/14) and Los Santos offtake (9/17) are each more than a
  week stale relative to today's move — they're real supporting context,
  not the actual trigger for the 9/25 price action, which appears to be
  the same-day Stifel initiation. Don't conflate "real news exists" with
  "today's specific move has a fresh trigger" — only the Stifel call is
  actually dated to 9/25.
- Sector/macro-driven: the tungsten price surge itself is a macro/
  geopolitical (China export-control) story affecting the whole tungsten
  supply chain, not unique to Almonty, even though today's specific
  catalyst (Stifel initiation) is company-specific. No same-day move in
  other tungsten names was confirmed via search, so this doesn't clearly
  fail the sector-cluster test (lessons.md #3), but the underlying macro
  tailwind is shared, not proprietary to ALM.
- Small/micro-cap mining name with execution risk on a mine still
  ramping to commercial production — the bull case depends on Sangdong
  actually hitting its 2027 output-doubling target.
- No insider-buying confirmation found; buyback authorization is a
  board decision, not a demonstrated insider-conviction signal.
