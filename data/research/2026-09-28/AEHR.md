initiated coverage that day with an Outperform rating and a $120 price

company-reported number (earnings, a signed contract, guidance) — per
lessons.md #5, weigh that lower than it might otherwise seem.

## Sentiment
Mixed-to-bullish but genuinely split, not uniformly bullish:
- Bullish: Oppenheimer Outperform/$120 (9/25, fresh), Jefferies Buy/$175
  (earlier, still the highest target out there).
- Bearish/cautionary: Zacks cut AEHR from "Strong Buy" to "Hold" on
  9/14 — a downgrade landing in the same recent window as Oppenheimer's
  upgrade, i.e. analysts are not converging on one view.
- GuruFocus flags GF Value at $20.62 against a $104.39 price — a roughly
  5x disconnect on that valuation model. Not treated as decisive on its
  own (GF Value is one model among many) but a real data point that the
  stock is priced for a lot of the AI-demand story to come true.

## Risks
- **Heavy, one-directional insider selling**: CEO Gayn Erickson sold
  40,000 shares (~$5.23M) on 8/12; insiders sold ~$13.8M worth over the
  trailing 3 months; no offsetting insider buying found. Persistent
  insider selling into strength is a real caution on a stock already
  this extended.
- **Imminent earnings (today, 9/28)**: buying now means buying directly
  ahead of an unresolved binary print — the opposite of a clean,
  already-resolved catalyst. A good or bad number could dominate
  whatever the 9/25 analyst-initiation move implied, in either
  direction.
- **Single-analyst-initiation catalyst**: per lessons.md #5, a new
  coverage initiation is a real, checkable fact but weaker evidence than
  a company's own reported results — and it arrives split against a
  same-month Zacks downgrade, not a clean consensus shift.
- **Valuation extension**: the stock is already far above both its
  pre-09-04 level and standard valuation-model fair value, on top of a
  history (09-04→09-15) of sharp moves in both directions.
- **History cuts both ways here**: the 09-04 "stale/sell-the-news" call
  was wrong once already on this exact ticker (lessons.md #1
  counter-example) — so this note explicitly does NOT lean on "already
  ran, must fade" as its main reasoning. The case for caution here rests
  instead on the insider-selling pattern, the imminent binary earnings
  event, and the single-analyst nature of the 9/25 catalyst specifically,
  not on assuming history repeats the 09-04 fade thesis.
