## Risks
- **Sell-the-news / already-fired reaction (lessons.md #1)**: the +7.65%
  move IS the earnings reaction — it already happened same-day. Multiple
  analysts trimming targets even while staying bullish suggests some of
  this was already priced in ahead of the print, and entering now means
  buying after the pop rather than ahead of it. This is the same pattern
  that downgraded CHPT, MSTR, NX, and TARS at council.
- **Sector-wide valuation compression risk**: the price-target cuts are
  explicitly tied to "sector multiple pressure" on AI-optics/semis as a
  group, not CRDO's numbers — worth flagging as a correlation risk if
  this reaches council (other AI-datacenter-connectivity names may be one
  correlated bet, not independent).
- Very high revenue growth rates (114.7% YoY) off a smaller base can
  decelerate quickly; guidance implies growth staying elevated but is
  itself just guidance, not delivered results yet.
- No insider-buying/selling signal found in this pass to weigh either way.
