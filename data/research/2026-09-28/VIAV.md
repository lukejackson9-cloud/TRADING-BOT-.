# VIAV — 2026-09-28

## Catalyst
A real, dated, company-specific catalyst DID land on 2026-09-25 (+9.27%,
$37.23 -> $40.68): Viavi announced it achieved Level 2 Cybersecurity
Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) for key aerospace/defense product
lines, which preserves/unlocks its ability to compete for U.S. DoD
contracts. This is layered on top of an ongoing, already-known story —
growth in Viavi's 1.6T/3.2T data-center optical test portfolio riding the
AI/optical-bandwidth buildout — plus a separate ~$500M public offering
used to retire $450M of debt (date of that offering not pinned down
precisely in search results, treated as background, not the 9/25 driver).

## Sentiment
Bullish on the surface — real news, not rumor, and several sell-side
firms (Northland among them) have lifted price targets recently citing
improved fundamentals. But two things temper this: (1) the stock is
already up 128.3% YTD, an already-extended move well before this specific
print, and (2) GF Value's model puts fair value at $14.86 vs. the current
$40.68 (173.8% "overvalued" by that specific model) — a signal the
market has already priced in a lot of optimism, whatever one thinks of
that particular valuation framework's usefulness here.

## Risks
- Per lessons.md #1 ("sell the news"/overextension), a real, dated
  catalyst that has ALREADY fired and moved the price — as this one did,
  same-day, on 2026-09-25 — is a weaker entry than the same catalyst
  caught before the reaction. Research is only happening now, 3 days
  after the move (9/28), i.e. well after the reaction, not ahead of it.
- Prior journal history for this exact ticker: 2026-09-09 WATCH low
  confidence ("no dated catalyst confirmed despite strong fundamentals")
  and a 2026-09-15 -12.72% reversal noted in watchlist.txt as a repeat
  ticker — VIAV has a demonstrated pattern of round-tripping its own
  pops. A -12.7% giveback happened before on this name after a similar
  unconfirmed-catalyst pop.
- Stock already extended (+128% YTD); a single certification win,
  however real, is being asked to justify a lot of embedded optimism at
  current levels.
- No single source found gave the exact date of the $500M offering
