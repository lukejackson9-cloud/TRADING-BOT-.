## Risks
- **Extreme, still-unfolding volatility**: a ~35-point intraday range
  ($60.85 to $95.77) on the data day itself is enormous even for a binary
  biotech catalyst — this is a genuinely hard name to size or time an
  entry into today, regardless of the catalyst's legitimacy.
- **Unlike GRAL/TWST, at least one FRESH target ($96, today) still sits
  slightly above the current price** ($89.92) — a meaningfully different
  setup from those two prior "already priced past every target" cases.
  But Goldman's $36 target (not yet confirmed updated) and the sheer
  magnitude of the move both argue for real caution before reading this
  as "still room to run."
- **BLA not yet filed** — Q4 2026 filing is a plan, not a submission; the
  regulatory pathway (FDA review, approval, launch) is still entirely
  ahead, multi-year, well beyond this project's 2-week horizon.
- **Company history includes a large dilutive raise** (Dec 2025, $173M
  net from an 8M-share offering at $23) — a huge stock-price pop
  post-data is a classic setup for another dilutive capital raise to fund
  the BLA filing and pre-launch spend, which the company has done before
  when the stock was up.
- Cash position (~$132M as of the most recent report) gives real runway,
  but a company that just had its stock spike 190% has every incentive to
  raise capital opportunistically now, same pattern already seen this
  month with VKTX's post-pop dilutive offering (2026-09-24).
- Next earnings/updates not on a fixed near-term date found; the real
  event (BLA filing) is Q4 2026, outside this project's short-term window.
