## Catalyst
Real, dated, quantified, and company-specific: on 2026-10-01, Enerflex
announced it was awarded a contract to design, engineer, fabricate, and
assemble approximately **450 MW of behind-the-meter natural gas-fired power
generation** for a North American data-center developer, with deliveries
scheduled 2027-2028. Shares jumped ~7.4% premarket and extended to close
+11.63%. This is corroborated across multiple independent sources (SEC 6-K
filing, Seeking Alpha, Investing.com, Benzinga, MarketChameleon) with
consistent figures — not a vague or unconfirmed move.

## Sentiment
Bullish on the news itself, but with real reasons for caution once the
stock's own run is accounted for:
- **EFXT is already up ~280% over the trailing year** and was trading near
  its 52-week high ($29.15) going into this print — this is not a name
  getting a fresh re-rating off a depressed base, it's a continuation of an
  already-extreme run.
- GuruFocus flags the post-pop price (~$25.44) as ~212% above its GF Value
