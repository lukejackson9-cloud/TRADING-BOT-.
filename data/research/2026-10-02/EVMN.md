## Catalyst
Real and dated: on 2026-10-01, Evommune presented late-breaking Phase 2a
proof-of-concept data for EVO301 (a long-acting injectable IL-18 fusion
protein) in a 70-patient moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis trial at the
EADV 2026 Congress in Vienna. The trial met its primary endpoint — a 55%
least-squares-mean reduction in EASI score at Week 12 vs. 22% for placebo
(33-point placebo-adjusted improvement), hit the pre-specified Bayesian
success criterion (p<0.01), and reported no treatment-related serious/severe
adverse events. This is a genuinely positive, dated, company-specific
readout, not a fabricated or vague catalyst — multiple independent outlets
(Gurufocus, TipRanks, Evommune's own IR site, an 8-K filing) corroborate the
same numbers.
