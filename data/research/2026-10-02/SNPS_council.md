## Fact-check notes
Independently verified the two most decisive claims from each side:
1. **Bear's ANSYS antitrust risk claim — REFUTED.** The UK CMA's actual
   phase-one decision in this merger was published December 20, **2024**,
   not 2026 — the Ansys acquisition fully closed July 17, 2025, and the
   CMA matter was resolved (undertakings accepted) by March 2025. The
   FTC consent-order divestiture is real but also already completed
   (mid-2025, assets divested to Keysight). There is NO live antitrust
   overhang on this deal today — the bear case presented ~1.5-2-year-old
   resolved regulatory history as if it were a pending 2026 risk. This
   materially weakens the bear case's strongest-sounding new finding.
2. **Bull's "held the gain on 10-02" claim — CONFIRMED, and understated
   if anything.** SNPS traded in an intraday range of $483.51-$500.70 on
   10-02, actually pushing to a NEW intraday high above the 10-01 close
   before easing back to the $484-490 band — the opposite of an
   immediate blow-off reversal. The cited 24/7 Wall St headline is real
   and accurately quoted.
Net effect: the fact-check removes the bear case's one clear "new tail
risk" finding, while confirming the bull case's day-2 continuation claim
is accurate (and arguably stronger than stated). However, this does NOT
resolve the bear case's remaining, unrefuted points: the live technical
extension signature (independently measured by the specialist, not just
argued by the bear), the stretched relative valuation, the vague OpenAI
deal terms, and insider net-selling.

already choppy going in. Day-2 continuation is a real, favorable data

override a live overextension signature this clean. Does not clear the
bar for a proposal today.
