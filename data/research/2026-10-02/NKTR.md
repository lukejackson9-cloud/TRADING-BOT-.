## Catalyst
Real and dated. On 2026-10-01, Nektar presented new long-term Phase 2b
data for rezpegaldesleukin (its lead Treg-targeting biologic) in alopecia
areata and atopic dermatitis at the European Academy of Dermatology and
Venereology (EADV) Congress in Vienna. The data showed a decline in
sustained treatment response after a six-month off-treatment period,
raising real questions about durability of effect ahead of the drug's
upcoming Phase 3 trial. The stock plunged ~21-22% same-day on the
release, matching the task's reported -22.39% ($60.545 -> $46.99) move
closely. This is a genuine, company-specific, dated clinical readout —
not a rumor, not a stale recycled story.

Notably, two sell-side analysts pushed back hard on the market's
reaction: Jefferies reiterated Buy with a $150 target, arguing the
selloff is unwarranted given the overall alopecia areata opportunity, and
HC Wainwright reiterated Buy at $185 on 2026-10-02. This is a real
disagreement between the market's immediate reaction and at least two
analysts' read of the same data — logged honestly, not resolved either
way here.
