## Catalyst
Real, dated, and multi-dimensional — more than a single Investor Day
re-rating. At its Investor Day on 2026-09-30, Synopsys:
1. Raised FY2027 guidance above estimates: revenue of $11.10B-$11.20B
   (vs. ~$10.81B consensus) and EPS of $19.04-$19.12 (vs. ~$17.81
   consensus) — a real, company-issued, quantified guidance raise, not
   an analyst estimate.
2. Set a new long-term target: ~15% compound annual revenue growth
   FY2026-FY2030 and ~50% adjusted operating margin by FY2030.
3. Announced two named, dollar-quantified AI partnerships: a
   revenue-sharing deal with OpenAI to develop an AI model for the chip
   business, and a multi-year AWS agreement worth more than $1B tied to
   custom AI silicon and cloud infrastructure tooling.
4. Announced a $1B share buyback.
Shares jumped ~13% on 2026-10-01 to $490.54 (task brief: +12.78%,
$434.94 -> $490.54, consistent). Following the move, HSBC upgraded SNPS
from Hold to Buy, raising its price target from $490 to $700 — notably,
this was issued AFTER the pop and implies meaningful further upside from
the new price, not just a catch-up rating change.

## Sentiment
