This is a clean, broad-based, company-reported beat-and-raise — not a
rumor, not a single-analyst action, not a sector-wide move.

## Sentiment
Genuinely bullish on the fundamentals — a real, broad-based beat with
margin growth, not a one-off item. However, the magnitude of the move
(a record single-day gain for a ~$200B+ mega-cap) is itself a major
entry-timing red flag. This closely mirrors JBL's 2026-10-01 verdict
already logged in this same screening batch ("real beat-and-raise but
classic sell-the-news, stretched valuation -- PASS") — a genuine
company-reported beat that triggers an extreme, record one-day pop is
exactly the pattern lessons.md #1 and the project's existing JBL
precedent treat with skepticism, not optimism, for a short-horizon long
entry the session after the move. There is no disclosed near-term
follow-up catalyst before the next quarterly report (months out).

## Risks
- Record single-day gain for a company this size is an extreme move —
  mean-reversion / "priced in" risk is high entering the day after.
- No identified forward catalyst inside this project's ~2-week horizon;
  next earnings isn't due for roughly another quarter.
- Mega-cap, low-beta consulting names don't typically exhibit the kind
  of multi-week momentum follow-through that smaller-cap movers in this
  project's history have shown (positive or negative) — the single
  biggest repricing has very plausibly already happened in one session.
- Directly comparable, same-batch precedent (JBL, 2026-10-01: real
  beat-and-raise, PASS'd specifically for sell-the-news/stretched
  valuation) argues for consistent treatment here.
