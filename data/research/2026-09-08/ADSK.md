# ADSK — 2026-09-08

## Catalyst
Autodesk's -8.3% move to $217.90 was NOT driven by an Autodesk-specific
event. The trigger was Adobe's CEO-transition announcement (Anil
Chakravarthy replacing Shantanu Narayen), which reopened sector-wide
questions about which seat-based-licensing software companies are most
exposed to generative-AI disruption of their pricing model. The selloff
was sector-wide across design/creative software, and Autodesk absorbed the
largest single-day hit of the group. This is a sympathy/re-rating move
off someone else's news, not a company-specific negative catalyst.
Notably, Autodesk's own Q2 FY2027 results (reported ~8/27) actually beat
estimates on both revenue and EPS, and the company raised full-year
FY2027 guidance (revenue to $8.295-8.345B, non-GAAP EPS to $12.52-12.60) —
the fundamental print was good, not bad. The stock has now fallen for 6
straight sessions (~19% cumulative, ~$11B in market cap) since that
report, which is a valuation/multiple-compression story (sector AI-
disruption fear) layered on top of otherwise solid results, not a
deteriorating business.

## Sentiment
Bearish on price action, but the case for bearishness is about narrative/
multiple compression (AI disruption fear for seat-based SaaS), not
worsening fundamentals — the actual guidance raise argues the other way.
TradingView's aggregated TA consensus is STRONG_SELL (2 buy / 18 sell / 6
neutral) — that's a clear, strongly one-sided technical read, consistent
with the 6-day slide and momentum firmly to the downside. This is a rare
case where the technical and fundamental reads genuinely diverge (good
guidance vs. deteriorating chart/sector sentiment), which argues for
caution on both a "cheap now" long thesis and a "keep shorting the
momentum" thesis — T212 is long-only here regardless.

## Risks
- This is a falling-knife pattern (6 straight down days) — trying to call
  a bottom on a sector-sentiment-driven decline (not a company-specific
  fundamental break) is speculative regardless of direction.
- Genuine, not-yet-resolved risk: if the AI-disruption-to-seat-licensing
  narrative has real legs (not just an Adobe-news overreaction), Autodesk
  guidance could prove too optimistic in future quarters even though this
  quarter beat.
- No trade edge here either way given T212 is long-only (no shorting) and
  the technical trend is straight down — a long entry into a STRONG_SELL
  technical consensus and an unresolved sector narrative is not an
  attractive setup.
- Recent MaintainX acquisition adds integration/execution risk on top of
  the guidance.
