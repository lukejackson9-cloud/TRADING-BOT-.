# TARS — 2026-09-08

## Catalyst
TARS (Tarsus Pharmaceuticals) rose to $90.78 (+9.1% on the 2026-09-03→
09-04 session, per Massive.com — not re-derived here). Two dated,
company-specific events converge on 2026-09-04:
1. **Tarsus closed its acquisition of Alkeus Pharmaceuticals** — a
   privately held retinal-disease biotech — adding worldwide rights to
   ALK-001, a Phase 3-ready oral candidate for Stargardt's disease (an
   inherited, currently untreatable blinding disease). The deal was
   originally announced 2026-08-06 (~$450M upfront cash+stock, up to
   $800M total with milestones, funded partly via a $125M private
   placement); 09-04 is the **closing**, not the announcement.
2. **Jefferies upgraded TARS to Strong-Buy and raised its price target to
   $105 from $88** in a note dated 2026-09-04, explicitly citing (a)
   increased FY2026 Xdemvy guidance and strong Q3 prescription-trend data,
   and (b) the Alkeus deal, estimating ALK-001 could reach ~$1B in peak
   sales.

Both are real, dated, and land on the same day as the price move — a
demand-side catalyst (a credible sell-side upgrade with a raised target,
tied to improved core-business guidance) landing alongside a completed,
pipeline-expanding acquisition. This is a stronger and more concrete
combination than "price went up."

## Sentiment
Genuinely bullish but not uncontested. TradingView technical-consensus

Set against that: this stock carries a live, unresolved bear case. Short-
seller Culper Research published a report (dated around July 2026, so
several weeks stale relative to today's move, but never publicly refuted
in anything found here) alleging Tarsus ran patient copay-assistance
donations through the Healthwell Foundation in a way that violates the
federal Anti-Kickback Statute, and that 2026 profit estimates are
overstated because Wall Street is modeling costs as largely fixed when
Culper's read of Q1 2026 filings (S&A at 94% of revenue) suggests
otherwise — Culper's own math implies a possible full-year EBIT of
-$130M if that ratio holds. This is a real, dated, sourced allegation with
potential regulatory/legal tail risk to the company's core (and only
approved) revenue driver, XDEMVY — it hasn't gone away just because good
news landed on 09-04.

## Risks
- **Live, unrebutted short-seller allegation** of an illegal Medicare
  copay kickback scheme tied to XDEMVY, Tarsus's sole approved product and
  main revenue source — a real legal/regulatory tail risk, not resolved
  by this week's good news.
- **Dilution/financing overhang**: up to $800M total deal consideration
  plus a $125M private placement raised concurrently — analysts
  explicitly flagged "near-term execution and dilution concerns" even
  while liking the strategic logic.
- ALK-001 is Phase 3, not yet approved — the ~$1B peak-sales estimate
  driving today's target hike is a projection on an unapproved asset, with
  full clinical and regulatory risk still ahead.
- Single-product commercial concentration (XDEMVY) until/unless ALK-001
  reaches market — company-specific risk is high even excluding the
  Culper allegations.
- Stock has already run (~23% in the week around this note per one
  source) — some of today's positive Jefferies note may already be
  chasing a partly-fired move rather than leading it.
