fired, at a fresh high — this project's own strict short-term-momentum
mandate exists specifically to avoid chasing exhausted moves, per
council.md's design and this session's own TA backtest evidence on
gap-chasing. This isn't a case of the story being fake; it's a case of
the risk/reward at this specific entry point, for this specific
days-to-2-week mandate, not clearing the bar. Per council.md's explicit
rule, a case that isn't "clearly weaker" than the bull thesis still goes
to WATCH, not CANDIDATE — and here the bear case is not merely "not
weaker," it's corroborated by the bull agent's own independent findings.
Confidence: medium-high
