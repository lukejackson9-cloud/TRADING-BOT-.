# TYRA — 2026-09-08

## Catalyst
TYRA (Tyra Biosciences) rose to $28.65 (+15.2% on the 2026-09-03→09-04
session, per Massive.com — not re-derived here). The dated news behind the
move: on 2026-09-04 the company announced it will host a conference call
on **2026-09-09 at 8:00am ET** to reveal initial Phase 2 (SURF302) results
for oral dabogratinib in intermediate-risk non-muscle-invasive bladder
cancer (IR NMIBC) — safety from 40+ patients, efficacy from 20+ patients
at both QD dose levels. TYRA is also presenting at the Cantor Global
Healthcare Conference the same day (1:00pm ET, 09-09).

Important distinction: the thing that actually happened on 09-04 was the
**scheduling announcement**, not the trial data itself. The real binary
readout (does dabogratinib actually work, is it safe) is still unresolved
and lands tomorrow, 2026-09-09 — one day after this note. This is
economically the same shape as this project's "EARNINGS {date} —
pre-catalyst watch" rule (research.md step 5): the market is pricing in
anticipation of a binary event, not a resolved outcome. An analyst may
already have a $45-50 price target (Piper Sandler, others; H.C. Wainwright
reiterated Buy), but those targets predate tomorrow's actual data and are
themselves conditional on it.

## Sentiment

"buy the rumor, sell the news" regardless of which direction the actual
data goes — including reversing on data that is merely in-line rather
than a blowout.

## Risks
- **Binary clinical-data risk lands tomorrow (09-09), completely
  unresolved as of this note.** Buying into this move now is buying into
  the day before a coin-flip-shaped event, not a documented positive
  outcome.
- Small sample sizes disclosed in advance (safety n>40, efficacy n>20) —
  even a "positive" initial readout could be statistically thin and
  subject to a skeptical Street reaction.
- Clinical-stage biotech with no approved product (per Q2 2026 results,
  a $45.6M net loss) — cash runway and dilution risk sit behind any near-
  term data outcome regardless of direction.
- TradingView's own technical consensus is bearish despite the price pop,
  a disagreement worth flagging.
