## Moderator decision
Verdict: WATCH
Reasoning: This is a genuinely closer call than most of this project's
council reviews — the bull case holds up well even after fact-checking
(the second catalyst is real, not a stale claim), and both sides
independently landed at medium confidence rather than one collapsing
under its own research the way TARS's bull case did. But for this
project's specific short-term (days-to-2-week) mandate, three things
tip this to WATCH rather than CANDIDATE: (1) the reaction has already
fired and shown a real (if smaller-than-claimed) same-day fade from its
peak — entering now means buying after the move, not ahead of it,
consistent with lessons.md #1's evidenced pattern; (2) there is no
scheduled near-term catalyst to sustain momentum over the next 1-2
weeks specifically — the next real datapoint (Phase 3 PHrontier) is
years out; (3) the insider-selling pattern is concrete, sourced, and
spans multiple executives and months, which is a genuine (not
manufactured) signal that the people with the best information were not
buying into the run-up. None of these individually would be
disqualifying, but together they mean the bull case does not CLEARLY
survive the bear case for this specific trade, this specific window —
per council.md's rule, that goes to WATCH, not CANDIDATE. The underlying
science and company narrative look legitimately strong; the entry point
and timing for a short-term trade do not.
Confidence: medium
