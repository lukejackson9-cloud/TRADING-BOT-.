# ROIV — 2026-09-09

## Catalyst
Real, dated, company-specific, and fired today. Roivant's subsidiary
Pulmovant announced positive topline Phase 2 PHocus data for mosliciguat
in pulmonary hypertension associated with interstitial lung disease
(PH-ILD): the study met its primary endpoint with a 56.3%
placebo-adjusted reduction in pulmonary vascular resistance, plus
secondary endpoints (improved six-minute walk distance, a meaningful
decline in the NT-proBNP cardiac-strain biomarker). Roivant simultaneously
announced that the follow-on Phase 3 PHrontier study has already
initiated enrollment — i.e. the company moved straight to the next stage
rather than pausing. This adds to a real, close-in-time positive-momentum
backdrop: Priovant (another Roivant subsidiary) received FDA approval for
LISRAYA (brepocitinib) for dermatomyositis in late August, with commercial
launch on track for end of September 2026. Stock reportedly gapped up as
much as +22% pre-market before settling to today's confirmed +18.8% move
to $41.48. Multiple analysts raised targets the same day (TD Cowen to $55
from $50, Guggenheim to $46 from $42).

## Risks
- The reaction already happened intraday (+18.8%, with an even larger
  pre-market gap) — per lessons.md #1, entering after a big
  catalyst-driven gap is historically a worse entry than catching the
  catalyst before it fires. This is the single biggest reason for caution
  here despite the data quality.
- Phase 2 primary-endpoint success does not guarantee Phase 3 success —
  mosliciguat's PH-ILD program is still years from any approval/revenue
  event; this is real progress, not a resolved outcome.
- Roivant is a multi-asset holding-company model (Pulmovant, Priovant,
  and other "vants") — strength in one subsidiary's readout doesn't
  de-risk the others, and the stock's valuation reflects the sum of many
  separate binary bets.
- No competing PH-ILD therapy data was surfaced by this search pass to
  check for a same-class overhang (unlike the ALNY/AMGN pattern seen
  elsewhere in this batch) — flagged as an open question for council to
  press on, not a confirmed risk.
- Small Phase 2 study — typical of this stage, but sample size wasn't
  surfaced by search and should be checked before this reaches any
  proposal stage.
