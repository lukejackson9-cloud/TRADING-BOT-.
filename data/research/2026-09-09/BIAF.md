## Step 0: prior history check
BIAF already has a documented history in this project. On 2026-09-02,

## Catalyst
Two real, dated, company-specific announcements landed right around
today:
1. **2026-09-09**: Nasdaq confirmed bioAffinity regained compliance with
   both the Minimum Bid Price Rule and the Rights/Warrants Rule,
   cancelling a previously scheduled delisting hearing — removes a real
   listing overhang (confirmed via the company's own 8-K and covered in
   a GlobeNewswire biotech roundup the same day).
2. **2026-09-08** (per Benzinga): the company announced expanding its
   CyPath Lung diagnostic test's approved use to post-curative
   surveillance (monitoring lung cancer survivors for recurrence),
   broadening its addressable market beyond initial risk assessment —
   layered on top of the already-known 2026-09-03 nationwide AvMEDICAL
   federal-distribution deal (VA/federal healthcare access), which is
   not fresh today but is part of the same ongoing commercial-growth
   narrative.

However, **the price action is not a clean reaction to good news** —
per Benzinga, BIAF spiked intraday to +54.78% (~$10.20, session high
reported at $11.90) on this news combined with explicitly noted
"low-float trading dynamics," then fully reversed and closed the
session at -41.5% ($8.93), a swing of roughly 96 percentage points
top-to-bottom in one session. This follows a 1-for-15 reverse stock
split executed 2026-08-20/24 for Nasdaq compliance, which cut shares
outstanding to roughly 592,000–982,000 (sources vary slightly) —
an extremely thin float that Benzinga explicitly says "amplifies daily
price volatility."

## Sentiment
Mixed-to-bearish by the close despite genuinely positive news: the
market's final verdict today was a sharp sell-off, not a hold of the
spike, even though the underlying announcements (compliance regained,
expanded test indication) are real positives. TradingView's technical
consensus is BUY (11 buy / 5 sell / 10 neutral) — notably in tension
with the confirmed -41.5% close, which is exactly the kind of
disagreement research.md flags as a prompt to look harder rather than
a tiebreaker; here the closing price action is the more decisive signal
since it's a directly confirmed outcome, not an aggregated indicator
score.

## Risks
- **This is precisely the pattern already flagged for this ticker on
  09-02** ("thin-float pop, verify catalyst carefully") now playing out
  in an even more extreme form — a real catalyst produced a huge
  intraday pump that fully round-tripped into a steep loss by the
  close, consistent with lessons.md #1 (buying into a fired catalyst
  pump is a worse entry) and the classic thin-float pump/dump shape
  from lessons.md #6.
- Share count in the ~600K–1M range post reverse-split means any
  position sizing here would face severe liquidity/slippage risk
  regardless of the news being genuine.
- Company is still deeply loss-making (Q2 2026 EPS -$0.64, improved
  from -$5.07 but still burning cash) with a very recent history of
  dilutive raises (private placement priced at $0.4727/share pre-split
  terms on 2026-08-16) — the compliance win removes a delisting risk
  but doesn't remove financing/dilution risk going forward.
- A same-day full reversal of a >50% intraday gain into a >40% loss is
  itself a red flag for whether any entry today could have been held
  profitably in either direction.
