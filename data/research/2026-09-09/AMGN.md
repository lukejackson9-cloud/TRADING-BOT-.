# AMGN — 2026-09-09

## Catalyst
Negative sympathy/read-through move, not company-specific bad news of its
own. Novartis announced (Sept 4) that pelacarsen — a competing
lipoprotein(a)-lowering drug developed with Ionis — failed its pivotal
Phase 3 trial on cardiovascular outcomes (death/MI/stroke) despite
successfully lowering Lp(a) levels. Because Amgen's own olpasiran targets
the same Lp(a) mechanism and is in Phase 3 (OCEAN(a)-Outcomes, readout
timing not surfaced by search), the market marked AMGN down in sympathy —
punishing the whole drug class for one competitor's failure. AMGN fell
~9.8-10.1% to $393.17-$394.56, its worst single-day move since October
2000 per one source. BMO Capital Markets downgraded AMGN the same day,
adding to the pressure. Separately, the UK's MHRA suspended new-patient
use of Amgen's Tavneos over data-reliability concerns — a real but
smaller, pre-existing overhang, not today's primary driver.

Step 0 check: no prior trade_ledger.md entry for AMGN. This maps closely
to the ALNY case in trade_ledger.md (a rival's trial data raising an
unresolved same-class mechanism question) — there, ALNY had its OWN
positive data undercut by a rival's read; here AMGN has no fresh positive
data of its own at all, just exposure to a rival's failure. That's a
weaker setup than ALNY's, not a stronger one.

## Sentiment
Net bearish on the specific move, though the underlying business was
strong immediately before it — Amgen was near a 52-week high after a Q2
beat and positive Phase 3 VESALIUS-CV data for Repatha (a different,
already-approved drug, unaffected by this news) as of late August.
TradingView's technical consensus (get_rating() cross-check) reads SELL
(4 buy / 12 sell / 10 neutral), consistent with the price action rather
than contradicting it — no divergence to flag here.

## Risks
- Olpasiran's own Phase 3 outcome is genuinely unknown — this is an
  unresolved binary risk being priced today on a proxy (a competitor's
  failure), not a confirmed fact about Amgen's own drug.
- No dated near-term event resolves this uncertainty inside the project's
  ~2-week trading window (no olpasiran readout date surfaced by search).
- This is a decline, not a breakout — there is no long-entry bull case to
  evaluate today; any thesis here would be a "many-Lp(a)-class-failures
  are already priced in, oversold bounce" call, which is speculative and
  not supported by any dated catalyst.
- Sell-side already moved (BMO downgrade) same-day — the reaction to the
  news is not fresh information a research pass can add to.
