# HWM — 2026-09-09

## Catalyst
No positive catalyst — this is a continued negative move. Howmet's stock
has been sliding for several sessions since Elon Musk announced (~Sept
4-5) that SpaceX will cast industrial gas-turbine blades and vanes
in-house at a Texas foundry, which markets read as a threat to a key
growth franchise (precision aerospace/industrial-turbine components) that
has supported HWM's premium valuation. The stock fell ~7.5% on the
initial announcement, another ~5.9% the next session, and is down another
-10.7% today to $231.53 — a multi-day slide, not a single fresh
dated event. CEO John C. Plant is scheduled to present at the 2026
Jefferies Global Industrials Conference tomorrow, which some coverage
attributes part of today's drop to (pre-event de-risking/reduced exposure
ahead of remarks that may or may not address the SpaceX narrative
directly) — that is unresolved event risk, not a catalyst in itself.

## Sentiment
Mixed and actively contested. The negative catalyst (SpaceX competitive
threat) is real and company-specific. But multiple sell-side desks have
pushed back hard the same week: Deutsche Bank called the competitive risk
overstated, and Bernstein reiterated Outperform and raised its target to
$328 (average analyst target reported at $316.22 vs. today's $231.53 —
an unusually large implied gap). TradingView's aggregated technical
consensus (get_rating() cross-check) reads SELL (3 buy / 15 sell / 8
neutral) — the technical picture agrees with the price action, not with
the bulls' valuation case. That disagreement between analyst
price-target commentary and both the price action and the technical
consensus is itself a reason for caution, not a reason to trust the
higher number.

## Risks
- This is a long-only strategy; there's no mechanical edge in "buying a
  falling knife" here — the project's own mean_reversion backtest came
  back negative (-0.17%/trade over 2yr, -0.46%/trade over 6yr, see
  CLAUDE.md's TA section) and was not promoted.
- The core risk (does SpaceX's in-house casting materially dent Howmet's
  addressable market) is genuinely unresolved — analysts are guessing,
  not reporting a settled fact.
- Additional event risk tomorrow (Jefferies conference) could extend the
  move in either direction before any of today's uncertainty resolves.
- Per lessons.md #5, a cluster of sell-side price-target defenses is
  weaker evidence than the company's own confirmed numbers — no new
  company-specific positive data has actually landed yet.
