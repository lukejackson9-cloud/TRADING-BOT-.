## Catalyst
Two different things are blended into today's +11.7% move to $99.83, and
they need to be separated. The durable, company-specific fundamental
story is several weeks stale: CoreWeave's Q2 earnings (reported early
August) showed revenue of $2.58B (+112% YoY), beat expectations, and
management raised FY26 revenue guidance to $12.4-13.2B with a backlog
around $104B — that print itself already drove an 18-20% rally weeks ago,
carrying the stock from the mid-$80s toward $100. Layered on top,
Truist raised its price target to $165 (from $155) in late August, and
Nvidia reportedly increased its stake in CoreWeave (timing unclear from
search). Today's specific pop, however, is attributed by multiple sources
to a new partnership announced between Nebius and Palantir, which sent
"neocloud"/AI-infrastructure stocks up broadly — that is a sector-wide
catalyst, not a CRWV-specific one.
