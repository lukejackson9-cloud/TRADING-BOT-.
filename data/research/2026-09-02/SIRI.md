# SIRI — 2026-09-02

## Catalyst
Deutsche Bank upgraded Sirius XM to Buy from Hold on 2026-09-02, target
$45. This follows real, dated fundamental turnaround signals from Q2 2026
earnings: SIRI returned to positive net subscriber additions (self-pay net
adds of 22,000 — strongest Q2 in four years) with the lowest self-pay churn
in company history (~1.4%). The company raised full-year 2026 guidance:
revenue to ~$8.525B, adjusted EBITDA to ~$2.625B, free cash flow to
~$1.375B.

## Sentiment
Cautiously bullish, grounded in real numbers rather than just narrative —
this is a turnaround-in-progress backed by reported subscriber and churn
data, not a story stock. Still a genuine debate: one recent SIRI move was
actually a *5.8% drop* on the earnings/guidance report itself (per one
source headline), suggesting the market's initial reaction to the
"turnaround" numbers wasn't uniformly positive before this fresh upgrade.

## Risks
- $9.7B debt load — meaningful leverage for a subscription business
  facing secular pressure.
- Structural risk is real and not resolved by one good quarter: listening
  is shifting toward on-demand streaming and changing in-car systems;
  overall subscriber count is still declining even as self-pay churn
  improves.
- The stock dropped on its own earnings/guidance report before today's
  upgrade — worth understanding why before assuming the market will simply
  agree with Deutsche Bank now.

## Verdict
CANDIDATE — a fresh, dated upgrade backed by real reported turnaround
metrics (not just sentiment), on a name with real, documented structural
risk. Sent to council to weigh the turnaround signals against the
structural pressure and the market's own mixed initial reaction to the
underlying numbers.

## Confidence
medium — the subscriber/churn data is genuinely good, but the debt load
and the stock's own drop on its earnings report are real counterweights.
