# CDE — 2026-09-02

## Catalyst
Coeur Mining (gold/silver producer) is up alongside a broad sector-wide rally in precious metals miners, not a company-specific event. Gold rose to ~$4,377/oz (+1.12% on the day) and silver to ~$64.37/oz (+0.48%) on September 2, 2026, extending a run where gold is up ~7.9% and silver ~11.6% over the past month. Gold mining stocks broadly are having their "hottest five-day run since 2008," driven by shifting Fed-rate expectations (a weak August ADP jobs report has markets pricing a ~66% chance of a September Fed cut/hike repricing, up from ~40% a week prior) pushing real yields down and metals up. There was also an earlier (Aug 19) Treasury buyback-plan catalyst that pushed CDE and Hecla up ~13% together. Company-specific news is limited to a Q2 2026 earnings miss on EPS ($0.12 vs $0.33 est.) despite record quarterly revenue (~$1.1B), and an ongoing record $158M exploration program (Palmarejo, Las Chispas) flagged back on July 21. No new company-specific news item dated to today (Sept 2) was found — today's move looks like sector beta to the metals rally, not an idiosyncratic catalyst.

## Sentiment
Bullish on the surface (analyst consensus "Buy," average price target $23.32, ~12.6% above the ~$21.18 last price), but the sentiment is a read-through from the precious-metals macro trade, not fresh company-specific optimism. Sell-side sentiment on CDE itself is mixed given the recent earnings miss.

## Risks
- This is a commodity-beta move: CDE's price is highly correlated to spot gold/silver, and a reversal in Fed-cut expectations or a Treasury-yield bounce could unwind the miners' rally as fast as it built (explicitly flagged in sourcing re: the Aug 19 spike).
- Q2 2026 EPS missed estimates by a wide margin (63.6% miss) despite record revenue — a disconnect worth scrutiny.
- Small/mid-cap miner, thinly diversified geographically (Mexico/US/Canada operations), so single-asset operational risk (e.g., at Las Chispas or Palmarejo) is elevated.
- Move is largely "everyone's up" (Hecla also +13% on the same day in mid-August) rather than CDE-specific outperformance, meaning little differentiated edge in picking CDE over sector peers.
