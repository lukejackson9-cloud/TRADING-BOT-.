# IHG — 2026-09-02

## Catalyst
UBS upgraded InterContinental Hotels Group to Buy from Neutral on
2026-09-02, lifting the target from $157.65 to $188 (~19% raise).

## Sentiment
Contradictory across sources, which is itself the finding here. UBS's
fresh $188 target is notably higher than other recent fair-value estimates
found for the same stock: one source's DDM-derived target sits at $134.06,
another cites analyst fair value estimates around $155-159 with technicals
described as "signaling bearish momentum with limited near-term upside."
A single fresh upgrade sitting well above the broader consensus range,
against a bearish technical backdrop, is not a clean signal.

## Risks
- Valuation already described as "full" by at least one source even before
  UBS's higher target.
- Principal company risks flagged: geopolitical instability (Middle East
  exposure relevant given this week's broader Iran/oil macro theme),
  supply chain, and operational resilience.
- The spread between UBS's $188 and other sources' $134-159 fair value
  estimates is wide enough to warrant treating this as one outlier call,
  not confirmed consensus.
