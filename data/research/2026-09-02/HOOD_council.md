conviction: **moderate** — concrete and sourced, not manufactured, but no

uncertain.

Key sources: Yahoo Finance (analyst estimates + YTD performance),
Investing.com (crypto volume decline), Barchart/CFTC/CNN (litigation),
StockAnalysis.com (valuation), GuruFocus/InsiderTrades.com (insider sales).

## Fact-check notes
Independently re-verified the bear case's single most load-bearing and
most surprising claim — that the prediction-markets/sports-contracts
growth segment (the specific thing Piper Sandler's football-season bull
catalyst depends on) is under live legal threat — via a separate WebSearch
pass, not just trusting the subagent's citation:
- **Confirmed, and if anything understated by the bear agent**: the CFTC
  pressured Robinhood into halting its sports event contracts within 48
  hours of launch (partnership with Kalshi). Multiple states are actively
  litigating against prediction-market sports contracts. The 9th Circuit
  Court of Appeals specifically **rejected Robinhood's own request for
  injunctive relief** against the Nevada Gaming Control Board. A separate
  federal appeals court ruled against prediction markets broadly on
  2026-08-28 (CNBC), explicitly setting up a likely Supreme Court fight.
- The bear agent named Kentucky/Washington/Wisconsin/New York specifically;
  independent search surfaced Illinois, New Jersey, Nevada, and Connecticut
  as the states with the most concrete court activity instead. The exact
  state list doesn't fully match, but the core claim — active, escalating,
  multi-state litigation with at least one adverse ruling against Robinhood
  specifically already on the books — is solid and, if anything, more
  serious than the bear case framed it (an actual court loss, not just a
  pending lawsuit).
- Did not independently re-verify the exact insider-selling dollar figures
  or the 62% crypto-volume-decline figure — these came from named,
  specific sources (GuruFocus, Investing.com) and weren't the single
  most surprising/load-bearing claim, though they're consistent with and
  reinforce the verified regulatory finding rather than contradicting it.

## Moderator decision
**Verdict: WATCH** (downgraded from research.md's CANDIDATE)

Reasoning: This is a case where the bear case doesn't just "not lose" —
its most load-bearing claim survived independent fact-checking *and* turned
out to understate the risk (an actual adverse 9th Circuit ruling against
Robinhood, not just pending litigation). The bull thesis's most
forward-looking, differentiated catalyst — Piper Sandler's football-season
prediction-market revenue estimate — sits directly on top of the product
line that's under live legal attack, including one specific fact pattern
(a 48-hour-old sports contract launch that the CFTC forced offline) that
should give real pause about how durable that growth segment is on a
2-week horizon. Combined with the stock being down 23% YTD despite
increasingly bullish analyst coverage (a real, documented disconnect
between sell-side sentiment and price this year), stretched valuation, and
CFO/CTO both being net sellers in the last 90 days, the honest read is that
this doesn't clear the bar for a buy right now — not because the analyst
calls are wrong about the business, but because the specific near-term
catalyst driving the "buy now" case is more legally fragile than the bull
research surfaced on its own. Worth continued watching, especially if
there's a favorable ruling or the litigation risk clarifies.

Confidence: medium-high on the downgrade itself (the regulatory finding is
well-corroborated and specifically undercuts the bull thesis's freshest
catalyst); medium on exactly where the line should sit, since the core
business (Q2 revenue +32% YoY) is genuinely strong.
