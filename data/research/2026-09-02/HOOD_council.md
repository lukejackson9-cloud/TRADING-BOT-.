# HOOD — Council Review — 2026-09-02

## Bull case
- Real cluster of bullish sell-side action across 3 trading days (Bernstein, Morgan Stanley, Piper Sandler citing NFL/NCAA football-season prediction-market revenue, Scotiabank initiation). Stock still well below its 52-week high, so this reads as a staggered recovery rally, not a blow-off top.
- Self-rated: moderate — real fundamental support, but valuation already rich (11.4x tangible book vs ~3.3x industry) and the stock already ran 21%+ in August.

## Bear case
- Bullishness hasn't translated to price all year — HOOD is down ~23.4% YTD despite increasingly bullish coverage. Crypto trading volume (a major revenue driver) fell 62% YoY to a near-2-year low. Beta of 2.32.
- **The exact growth segment bulls are citing (prediction markets/sports contracts) is under live, escalating regulatory attack** — CFTC pressure, multi-state litigation, adverse court rulings already on the books.
- Insiders (CFO, CTO) net sellers over 90 days (~$197M sold). Self-rated: moderate.

## Fact-check
- Independently confirmed and found the bear case **understated** the risk: the CFTC forced Robinhood to halt its sports event contracts within 48 hours of launch, and the 9th Circuit specifically rejected Robinhood's own request for injunctive relief against the Nevada Gaming Control Board — an actual adverse ruling, not just pending litigation.

## Moderator decision
Verdict: WATCH (downgraded from CANDIDATE)
Reasoning: The bull thesis's most differentiated near-term catalyst (football-season prediction-market revenue) sits directly on top of a product line under live legal attack, including a specific adverse court ruling against Robinhood. Combined with a 23% YTD decline despite bullish coverage and CFO/CTO insider selling, this doesn't clear the bar — not because the analyst calls are wrong about the business, but because the catalyst driving "buy now" is more legally fragile than the bull research surfaced.
Confidence: medium-high on the downgrade; the underlying business (Q2 revenue +32% YoY) is genuinely strong.
