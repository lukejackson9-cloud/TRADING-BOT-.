Ongoing theme, not a fresh single-day trigger: Caterpillar has a record
backlog (~$51-72B depending on source/period), partly AI/data-center power
demand driven, plus a new FieldAI robotics partnership. Part of the
broader documented 2026 sector-rotation theme (industrials/energy/consumer
defensive outperforming as tech cools).

## Sentiment
Fundamentally solid but this reads as a longer-term thematic story, not a
short-term momentum trigger. The stock is already up 32.6% YTD — most of
this theme has already played out in the price this year, and no
single-day fresh news item (an earnings beat, an analyst upgrade dated
this week, a specific contract win) was found to justify entering in the
next 1-2 weeks specifically.

- One source explicitly notes "fundamentals remaining solid but no longer
  accelerating the way the stock price has" plus a pending CFO transition
