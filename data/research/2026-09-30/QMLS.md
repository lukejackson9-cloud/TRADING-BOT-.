## Company identity check
QumulusAI, Inc. (NASDAQ: QMLS) — a cloud infrastructure company that
rents out NVIDIA-GPU-powered compute capacity for AI inference/training
workloads. Confirmed via CNBC/Robinhood/MarketBeat; a small,

3,088 by Q2, Q2 revenue $6.7M). No collision risk found.

## Catalyst
Real and dated: QumulusAI announced $240.9 million in new three-year AI-
inference customer contracts for NVIDIA Blackwell B300 GPU capacity on
2026-09-29 (covering 2,000+ GPUs, expanding two existing customer
relationships first established in July 2026, bringing combined
commitments from those two customers past $344 million). This is
genuinely positive, company-specific, dated news — but the stock still
fell 8.80% ($7.16 → $6.53) same-day. Reporting is explicit about why:
QMLS had already run up ~15% in premarket trading in anticipation of the
announcement, and once the official news hit, the stock reversed and
fell for the rest of the session — a same-day "buy the rumor, sell the
news" round-trip, not a reaction to bad news.

## Sentiment
Underlying business momentum looks real (Q2 2026 revenue +118% YoY, gross
margin expanding to 66.6%, GPU fleet more than tripling quarter over
quarter, $282.5M in signed contracts even before today's new deal) — but
today's price action is a clean, sourced example of lessons.md #1's
pattern: the reaction to a real catalyst had already fired (in
premarket) before the news was even officially confirmed, so buying
after the print means buying after the move, not ahead of it.

## Risks
- **Textbook sell-the-news pattern, well-sourced same-day** (not an
  inference — multiple outlets independently described the premarket
  run-up followed by the reversal on the official announcement).
- **Customer concentration**: the new contracts expand relationships with
  just two named customers; a small number of large contracts is a real
  concentration risk for a company this early in its scale-up.
- **Recently public / thin trading history**: no multi-year track record
  to judge normal volatility range against, and speculative-microcap
  dynamics (large premarket swings, fast reversals) were on display
  today.
- **No dated near-term catalyst found beyond today's news** — nothing
  scheduled in the next 1-2 weeks that would give a fresh reason for the
  stock to re-rate higher from here.
