## Company identity check — ticker collision investigated and resolved
FMP's screener listed XRPN at +21.99% ($10.575→$12.90), while separately
tagging XRPN as "Armada Acquisition Corp. II" at a different price
($16.40). Investigated directly: there is **no actual collision between
two different companies** — both quotes refer to the same underlying
security. Armada Acquisition Corp. II (formerly ticker "AACI," a SPAC)
changed its ticker to **XRPN** effective 2025-10-30, specifically to
reflect a pending $1B+ business-combination deal with **Evernorth
Holdings, Inc.**, a newly formed company built to hold and manage XRP as
a treasury asset (~473M XRP, backed by Ripple, SBI, Antara, Kraken and
others as investors). If/when the merger closes, the combined company
trades on Nasdaq under this same ticker, XRPN. So "XRPN the SPAC
(Armada II)" and "XRPN the pending Evernorth combination" are the same
ticker at two different stages of one transaction, not two different
companies — FMP's $16.40 figure looks like a stale or differently-timed
snapshot of the same security rather than a genuine collision (see
lessons.md #2: WebSearch/data sources disagreeing on exact price for the
same ticker is a recurring, resolvable issue, not evidence of two
different entities here).

## Catalyst
Real and dated, but explicitly **unresolved** at time of writing: Armada
Acquisition Corp. II shareholders were scheduled to vote on 2026-09-30
(today) on completing the Evernorth business combination. The 09-29
move (+21.99% in the mover data; one source put it at +25.14% from
$10.58→$13.24, consistent with lessons.md #2's usual price-precision
caveat) reflects anticipation of that vote plus a same-day ~3.6% rise in
XRP itself (to ~$1.55) amid strong U.S. spot-XRP-ETF inflows. No search
performed today found a confirmed vote result — the meeting appears to
be same-day or very recent relative to this research pass, with the
actual outcome not yet reported.

## Sentiment
Speculative and crypto-correlated rather than fundamentals-driven. This
isn't a normal operating company with earnings or revenue — it's a SPAC
whose entire near-term value proposition is "will this merger close and
will XRP's price hold up." Sentiment on the underlying idea (an
XRP-treasury vehicle analogous to MicroStrategy-for-XRP) is bullish
among crypto-focused commentary, but that's a bet on XRP's price and
deal completion, not a company fundamental.

## Risks
- **Unresolved binary event, directly analogous to research.md's
  earnings-pre-catalyst rule**: the actual vote/merger-completion
  outcome was not confirmed as of this research pass. Per the same
  principle that caps a pre-earnings ticker at WATCH, an unresolved
  vote outcome should not be treated as a settled catalyst either — the
  real event (does the deal close) hasn't happened yet.
- **Reaction already priced in ahead of the vote (lessons.md #1)**: the
  stock already ran up in anticipation; if the vote passes as expected,
  a "sell the news" dynamic is a real risk on top of the deal-completion
  risk itself.
- **This will not behave like a normal equity if the deal closes**: a
  post-merger Evernorth/XRPN would essentially track XRP's price and
  treasury NAV, not a business's earnings — a fundamentally different
  and higher-volatility risk profile than this project's other
  screened names, closer to a leveraged crypto proxy than a momentum
  equity trade.
- Standard SPAC/business-combination risks apply regardless of the vote
  outcome (redemption risk shrinking the post-deal float/cash, general
  crypto-market volatility flowing straight through to the stock).
- Price data across sources for this ticker disagreed by a meaningful
  margin (lessons.md #2) — treat exact percentages/levels here as
  directionally right, not precise.
