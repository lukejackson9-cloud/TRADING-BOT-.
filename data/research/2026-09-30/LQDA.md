## Catalyst
Real, dated, and severely negative: a U.S. District Court in Delaware
ruled TODAY (2026-09-30) that Liquidia infringed claims 1 and 14 of
United Therapeutics' patent (US 11,826,327) covering inhaled treprostinil
for pulmonary hypertension with interstitial lung disease (PH-ILD) --
the indication behind roughly HALF of YUTREPIA's (Liquidia's flagship
product) addressable market. Stock collapsed intraday, updated through
the session (headlines tracked -25% -> -48% -> -52% -> final -57.19%
close). Liquidia says it will submit an FDA label supplement to remove
the PH-ILD indication and plans to pursue appellate options; United
Therapeutics has separately asked the court for injunctive relief that
could go beyond a label change, with both parties reportedly given about
a week to submit proposed remedies -- meaning further negative resolution
risk is still pending, not yet fully priced.
