  60-day extension). Sportradar has disclosed material Latin
  America/Caribbean revenue exposure (~10% of total revenue per its

  four-day-old news working through the stock, not a brand-new headline
  today specifically.

  Neutral with a $16 target on that news. The stock is down ~44-46% YTD;
