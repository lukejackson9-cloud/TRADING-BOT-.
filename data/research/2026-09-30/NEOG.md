## Catalyst
Real, dated, and clearly negative: the FDA announced on 2026-09-29 that
it issued warning letters to Neogen Corporation and Sciarra Aeromed Inc.
following an investigation that linked Neogen Vet HYCOAT (hyaluronate
sodium sterile solution), a veterinary wound-care product marketed as
sterile, to fungal contamination. FDA testing identified multiple fungal
species; the contamination is linked to serious joint infections in
nearly 100 horses and at least 20 horse deaths — described as the
highest number of horse fatalities ever linked to a quality issue in an
FDA-regulated veterinary product. Neogen had voluntarily recalled several
lots in January 2026 after earlier reports (beginning late 2025), but
today's warning letter is a fresh, escalated regulatory action, not a
recycled story. Neogen Vet must submit a corrective-action response
within 15 working days or face further enforcement.
