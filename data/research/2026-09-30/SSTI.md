## Company identity check
Confirmed via Benzinga/GuruFocus/marketbeat: SoundThinking, Inc. (NASDAQ:
SSTI), the public-safety technology company behind ShotSpotter (acoustic
gunshot detection), CrimeTracer, CaseBuilder and ResourceRouter. No
identity ambiguity or ticker collision found.

## Catalyst
Real, dated, and about as unambiguous as a catalyst gets: on 2026-09-28,
SoundThinking signed a definitive merger agreement to be acquired by
Transom Capital Group. Terms: $8.00/share cash upfront plus one
non-transferable Contingent Value Right (CVR) worth up to an additional
$3.00/share tied to 2027 ShotSpotter/SafePointe revenue hitting $73.5M-
$87M, for total potential consideration up to $11.00/share. The $8.00
cash component represented a 46% premium to the 09-28 close. Shares
jumped ~50-52% on 09-29 to close around $8.21 — already trading slightly
ABOVE the $8.00 cash floor, meaning the market is already assigning some
value to the CVR.

## Sentiment
The market reaction is straightforwardly bullish on the deal itself (the
stock re-rated immediately toward deal value), but there isn't a
meaningful "should this run further" debate the way there is for a
typical earnings-pop CANDIDATE — a signed cash-plus-CVR buyout mostly
caps near-term upside at the deal terms, absent a topping bid. The
"Hold" pre-deal consensus and the standalone Q3/Q4 2026 revenue plan
($99-100M, 8-9% adjusted EBITDA margin) are now largely superseded by
the acquisition price as the reference point.

## Risks
- **Reaction has already fully fired (lessons.md #1)**: this is the
  cleanest version of that pattern seen yet — the entire catalyst (a
  signed, priced merger agreement) is public and the stock already
  jumped ~50% to reflect it. There is no "ahead of the news" version of
  this trade left to take.
- **Poor fit for this project's trading style regardless of catalyst
  quality**: Strategy's exits are a 5-trading-day time stop / -4%
  stop / +8% target on a momentum setup. A signed cash-and-CVR
  acquisition isn't a momentum trade — the remaining "upside" is a
  deal-completion/CVR-value question that typically resolves over
  months, not the 1-2 week horizon this project trades on.
- **Merger-arb risk, not momentum risk**: normal deal risks apply
  (regulatory/shareholder approval, financing, MAC clauses) that have
  nothing to do with ShotSpotter's actual news flow. The "law firms
  investigating whether the buyout fairly values the company" headline
  is boilerplate that accompanies nearly every announced M&A deal and is
  weak evidence of real deal risk on its own — but it's also not a
  reason to expect a bump; treating it as either bullish or bearish
  signal would be reading too much into routine plaintiff-firm noise.
- CVR structure means a large chunk of the theoretical $11.00 total is
  contingent on 2027 revenue thresholds — long-dated and uncertain,
  not something a short-term trade thesis should lean on.
