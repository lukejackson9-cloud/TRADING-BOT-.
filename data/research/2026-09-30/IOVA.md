## Step 0: prior context

company-issued beat/raise with broad, above-price analyst target

## Company identity check
Confirmed via GuruFocus/Benzinga/stockanalysis.com: Iovance
Biotherapeutics, Inc. (NASDAQ: IOVA), a commercial-stage biotech behind
Amtagvi (lifileucel) and Proleukin. No identity ambiguity.

## Catalyst
Real, dated, and company-issued: on 2026-09-29 Iovance filed an 8-K
raising full-year 2026 total revenue guidance to $410-420M from a prior
$350-370M — above the ~$403M analyst consensus and implying ~60% YoY
growth — attributed to sustained/accelerating commercial demand for
Amtagvi and Proleukin. Stock surged ~30-32% to a two-year high of
$15.30. Multiple sell-side firms moved targets meaningfully above the
current price same-day: H.C. Wainwright $9→$20, Wells Fargo $14→$18
(Overweight), Barclays and Goldman Sachs both at $15 (roughly in line
with, not below, the post-pop price).

## Sentiment
Strongly bullish and broadly confirmed: this is a company raising its
own guidance on real demand data, not a rumor or a single analyst call,
and several fresh targets sit clearly above today's price (Wainwright's
$20, Wells Fargo's $18) — a genuinely constructive analyst-support
picture, similar in shape to CCL's 09-29 review. Barclays/Goldman's $15
targets are closer to the post-pop price and reflect less remaining
room, which tempers the picture somewhat.

## Risks
- **Reaction already fired same-day (lessons.md #1)**: a +30%+ single-
  session move on the guidance raise itself means entry now is buying
  after the move, not ahead of it — directly analogous to the CCL
  09-29 review, which was downgraded to WATCH on this exact basis
  despite an even cleaner analyst-target picture.
- Commercial-stage cell-therapy names are historically volatile and
  prone to giving back a chunk of a guidance-raise pop on any
  subsequent read-through or execution concern (manufacturing capacity,
  slot utilization, competitive cell-therapy entrants) — none of which
  were checked in-depth here and none negate the catalyst, but they're
  the standard risk class for this kind of name.
- Two of the four cited targets (Barclays, Goldman at $15) are close to
  where the stock already trades, meaning not all fresh sell-side
  opinion sees much room left — a more mixed picture than the
  headline "analysts chase" framing suggests.
- Biotech/commercial-stage names carry real execution risk between a
  guidance raise and actually delivering it; a raise is not itself
  delivered revenue.
