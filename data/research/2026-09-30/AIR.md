## Catalyst
Two real, dated, company-specific events on consecutive days, pulling in
opposite directions:
- **Positive**: AAR reported fiscal Q1 2027 results after market close
  on 2026-09-28 — a real beat, adjusted EPS $1.49 vs. $1.29 est., revenue
  $918.0M vs. $881.1M est. (+24% YoY), plus upbeat Q2 guidance above
  consensus.
- **Negative (the actual driver of today's move)**: alongside earnings,
  AAR announced a $1.8B deal to acquire a 65% stake in privately held MRO
  Holdings at a 10.7x EBITDA multiple (with an option on the remaining
  35% within six years). Financing is ~$2.1B in new debt, $780M of new
  equity issued at $135/share, and a $230M PIPE — a real, dated,
  leverage- and dilution-heavy financing structure, not a rumor.
- The deal materially grows AAR's MRO footprint (7→12 facilities,
  7M→19M service hours, ~1,200→3,000 aircraft serviced/year) with a
  claimed $75M of synergies, but the financing package is what markets
  reacted to negatively, not the quarter itself.

## Sentiment
Bearish despite the earnings beat: the sell-off reads as a reaction to
increased leverage, new share issuance, and integration/execution risk
from a large, debt-and-equity-funded acquisition, not to the underlying
quarterly performance, which was genuinely strong.

## Risks
- Not a BUY setup today — this is a downside mover, documented for
  completeness per the task, not as a reversal candidate.
- Real dilution: $780M of new equity at $135/share is a concrete, sizable
  new share count, independent of how the deal ultimately performs.
- Leverage risk: ~$2.1B in new debt for a company of AAR's size is a
  material balance-sheet shift; integration risk on a 10.7x-EBITDA
  private-company acquisition is unproven until execution is shown.
- The earnings beat itself, on its own, would ordinarily be a positive
  signal — worth noting for context if the market later separates "good
  quarter" from "expensive/leveraged deal" once the acquisition's
  financing is digested, but that hasn't happened yet as of today's
  close.
