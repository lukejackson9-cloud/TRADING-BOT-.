## Identity check
VEEA (Veea Inc., Nasdaq) is a real operating company — an edge-computing/
IoT and connectivity hardware business, not an ETF wrapper, SPAC unit, or
closed-end fund. However, several structural red flags stack up:
- Underwent a 1-for-20 reverse stock split effective 2026-08-28, forced by
  a Nasdaq minimum-bid-price compliance notice (needed to regain
  compliance by 2026-09-28). This is a distress signal, not a growth
  action.
- RTTNews reported the stock actually fell 7% right after the reverse
  split took effect.
- The +149.34% move on 2026-09-15 (close $5.71, ~202M shares volume) is
  driven by a announced **term sheet** (not a signed, definitive deal) to
  combine with NovaGen Group, a "personalized regenerative medicine and
  longevity" company — a business completely unrelated to Veea's existing
  edge-computing operations. A cornerstone investor (GeoNova Capital) has
  signed a term sheet for a $10M investment in the combined entity.
This combination of (a) a forced reverse split for bid-price compliance,
immediately followed by (b) a huge percentage pop on (c) a non-binding
term sheet to pivot into an entirely unrelated industry, with (d) massive
volume relative to a post-split micro-float, is structurally very similar
to the BIAF precedent flagged in screen.md (data/research/2026-09-02/) —
thin-float, reverse-split-adjacent noise rather than a fundamentals-driven
move in Veea's actual business.

## Catalyst
A signed term sheet (not yet a definitive agreement) to combine with
NovaGen Group and launch "NovaGen Health Networks," plus a $10M
cornerstone-investor term sheet from GeoNova Capital. Dated and real as a
press event, but non-binding, pivots the company into an unrelated
business line, and arrives right after a compliance-driven reverse split.

## Sentiment
Speculative/frothy — huge volume and price move suggest retail momentum
chasing a "merger" headline rather than durable re-rating of the

## Risks
- Term sheet is non-binding; "targeting execution of a definitive
  business combination agreement within coming weeks" — could fall
  through entirely.
- Post-reverse-split micro-float means extreme volatility and difficulty
  exiting cleanly.
- Business pivot into regenerative medicine is unrelated to Veea's core
  competency — execution and integration risk are unknowable at this
  stage.
- Classic pump-then-fade risk pattern (see BIAF, trade_ledger.md 09-02:
  "round-tripped $9.75→$17.14→~$9.00").
