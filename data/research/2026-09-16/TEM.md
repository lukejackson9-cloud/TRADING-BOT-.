# TEM — 2026-09-16

## Catalyst
Tempus AI (NASDAQ: TEM), a real operating healthcare-AI/precision-medicine
company, closed +10.67% on 2026-09-15 at $68.85. Multiple corroborating
sources point to a genuine, dated, company-specific catalyst: a Piper
Sandler upgrade to Overweight with a $76 price target (citing the
Personalis acquisition and positive clinical data), layered on top of
recently announced ARPA-H federal funding (~$9.5M) for AI-driven cardiology
research and FDA clearances for cardiovascular diagnostic tools. CEO Eric
Lefkofsky also spoke at a conference about liquid-biopsy pricing upside.
This is part of a longer rally (~70% over the prior 30 days per one
source), which raises an overextension concern (see lessons.md #1 — buying
after a big move that's already run is a weaker entry).

## Sentiment
Bullish. Sell-side upgrade plus multiple positive operational/regulatory
data points reinforcing each other, not a single isolated headline.

## Risks

  an existing extended move (lessons.md #1: "sell the news" / overextension
  risk applies even to a genuine catalyst once the reaction has partly
  fired already, mid-August saw a +20% move on a separate Merck-Moderna
  readout).
- Small-cap-ish, high-multiple AI/healthcare name — likely volatile both
  ways.
- One analyst upgrade + federal grant news is real, but a single upgrade is
  a checkable fact, not proof of a durable trend (lessons.md #5 standard:
  weight it lower than the company's own reported numbers).
- No prior mentions of TEM found in trade_ledger.md or lessons.md — first
