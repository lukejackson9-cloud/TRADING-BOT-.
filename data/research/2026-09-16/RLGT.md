# RLGT — 2026-09-16

## Step 0: prior context
No prior mention of RLGT in trade_ledger.md or lessons.md. Relevant
general pattern: lessons.md #1 — buying right after a catalyst-driven pop
tends to be a worse entry than a better one ("sell the news" /
overextension), evidenced quantitatively and discretionarily multiple
times, applies directly here since the move is a same-week earnings
reaction.

## Identity check
RLGT is Radiant Logistics, Inc. (NYSE American), a real operating
non-asset-based freight/logistics and transportation-management company.
Real common stock, not a wrapper, SPAC unit, or shell — but it is a
small-cap, comparatively thinly-covered name (fewer analysts/less
liquidity than large-caps on this list), worth flagging per screen.md's
liquidity-preference guidance.

## Catalyst
Real, dated, and company-specific: Radiant reported Q4 FY2026 results and
held its earnings call on 2026-09-14. Revenue rose 18.5% YoY to $261.4M,
net income jumped 53.1% to $7.5M, Adjusted EBITDA rose 31.6% with margin
expansion, and the company closed the fiscal year debt-free with an
expanded $200M senior credit facility (extended to 2031). The stock
surged 17.8-19.49% on the reaction (close $9.81 on 09-15). Management
also pointed to the Navegate platform and a new independent-agent program
as forward growth drivers, and characterized the freight cycle as
starting to turn.

## Sentiment
Genuinely bullish and fundamentals-backed — an earnings beat with
margin expansion, debt-free balance sheet, and constructive forward
commentary on the freight cycle, not a rumor or sector-wide sympathy
move. At ~11.7x forward earnings per one report, the stock isn't already
priced for perfection either.

## Risks
- Per lessons.md #1, the price has already moved substantially on the
  earnings reaction (this research is being written the day after the
  call/pop) — the strongest part of this catalyst's reaction has likely
  already fired, which historically makes this a weaker entry, not a
  better one.
- Small-cap, lower-liquidity name — harder to size and exit cleanly than
  a large-cap.
- Freight/logistics is cyclical and sensitive to broader macro demand;
  "freight cycle turning" is management's own characterization, not yet
  confirmed by a second data point.
- Only one earnings print's worth of confirmation so far; no forward
  paper-trade or repeat-pattern history in this project for RLGT
  specifically.
