## Catalyst
No fresh, dated catalyst specific to today. SMCI's last earnings print was
its FY2026 Q4 report on 2026-08-11 (revenue $11.12B, +93% YoY but missing
the $11.56B consensus by ~4%; GAAP gross margin recovered to 17.5% from
9.5% a year earlier). Since then the stock has been drifting on general
AI-infrastructure enthusiasm — record FY2026 backlog (CEO cited $60B+ in
new orders), FY2027 revenue guidance of $65-72B, and continued Blackwell
GB300 shipments (~$13B in back orders) — but none of that is new news as
of today. Next earnings isn't until 2026-11-03, well outside this system's
~2-week window. Today's +4.5% looks like sector-wide AI-hardware momentum
rather than a company-specific, dated event.

## Sentiment

  reversing per the company's own guidance.
- Revenue miss vs. consensus in the most recent quarter despite the strong
  YoY growth headline.
- No new information today — trading on this alone risks chasing a move
