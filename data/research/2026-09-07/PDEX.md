# PDEX — 2026-09-07

## Catalyst
Pro-Dex released its fiscal 2026 Q4 and full-year results (quarter ended
June 30, 2026) on 2026-09-03: net sales of $20.4M (+17% YoY, driven mainly
by its largest customer's next-generation orthopedic handpiece volumes)
and gross profit of $7.1M, up 103% YoY. Full-year sales grew 16%, and the
CEO cited the completed APM acquisition as a contributor. This is a real,
dated, substantive catalyst.

However, the reaction is muddled: one source (referencing what looks like
the same report) says EPS beat estimates ($0.87 vs $0.66, or elsewhere
$1.20 vs $0.62 — the two figures don't agree and may be conflating this
report with a prior quarter), and reports that shares actually traded
*down* $0.83 to $61.75 on the day of the release — i.e. an initial
negative reaction to a beat. Today's confirmed price of $73.41 (+18.9%) is
well above that post-earnings level, several trading days later, with no
distinct news item dated 09-05 through 09-07 found to explain a fresh
same-day move. This reads like a delayed, partial re-rating catching up to
a genuinely strong quarter rather than a fresh catalyst today.

## Sentiment
Fundamentally positive (real margin and revenue growth, contract-driven),
but the muddled/inconsistent reporting on the immediate market reaction
and the multi-day lag between the report and today's specific pop make it
hard to say sentiment is cleanly bullish right now vs. already-past
momentum.

## Risks
- Small-cap, thin-coverage medical device name — a large single-customer
  revenue concentration (the "largest customer's" handpiece line is
  explicitly called out as the main driver) is a real dependency risk.
- Conflicting EPS figures across sources raise real doubt about whether
  all the numbers found actually belong to the same reporting period —
  this warrants a fresh, careful read of the actual 8-K/press release
  before treating the "beat" as settled.
- No confirmed catalyst specific to today, only a several-day-old earnings
  report with a reportedly negative initial reaction.
