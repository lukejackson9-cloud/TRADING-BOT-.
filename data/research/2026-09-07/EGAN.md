# EGAN — 2026-09-07

## Catalyst
Real and dated. eGain (customer-engagement/AI knowledge-hub software,
small-cap ~$200M market cap) reported fiscal Q4 and full-year 2026 results
after market close on 2026-09-03. Q4 revenue was $22.2M, down 4% YoY;
full-year FY2026 revenue was $91.1M. EPS beat estimates, but the market
reaction was driven by FY2027 guidance: total revenue guided to
$84.5-86.0M (a ~7% YoY decline at the midpoint), adjusted EBITDA guided
down sharply to just $0.7-1.4M for the full year, and the company expects
to swing to a GAAP net loss of $2.0-3.0M — a sharp reversal from recent
profitability. The core issue: legacy-customer revenue is expected to
fall ~40% and legacy ARR ~60% in FY2027, and AI-customer revenue growth
(+20% YoY to $55.1M in FY2026, guided +8-10% for FY2027) isn't offsetting
that decline fast enough. Multiple sources report shares fell 22-28% in
the initial reaction (pre-market/after-hours), consistent with today's
confirmed -17.9% intraday move being the continuation/settling of that
post-earnings reaction.

## Sentiment
Bearish. This is a straightforward guide-down on a structural business
transition (legacy revenue collapsing faster than the AI business can
replace it), not sentiment or macro noise. Three analysts reportedly still
carry a "Buy" rating with a $12.83 price target (implying large upside
from current levels), but that appears to predate or not yet reflect the
FY2027 guidance cut — it should be treated with skepticism until a
post-guidance analyst update is confirmed.

## Risks
- Confirmed structural decline in the legacy business (-60% ARR guided)
  is a real, company-specific fundamental risk, not just a one-quarter
  miss.
- Guided swing to a GAAP net loss in FY2027 after a "record cash flow"
  FY2026, per one Q4 earnings-call summary — profitability trajectory is
  reversing, not just decelerating.
- Small-cap, thin-coverage stock (~$200M cap, 3 analysts) — headline
  price targets may be stale and not adjusted yet for the new guidance.
- No evidence of a stabilizing catalyst (e.g., a large new AI contract)
  announced alongside or after the guidance cut to offset the bad news.
