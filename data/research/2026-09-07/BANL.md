volatility pause on/around 2026-09-04; the stock closed that day at $9.01,

driver is the large share sale (likely an insider/institutional block
sale or secondary offering) that halted trading, not any operational or
earnings news.

fundamentals-driven move. No earnings, guidance, or business-catalyst
