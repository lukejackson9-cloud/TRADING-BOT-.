## Catalyst
Ginkgo Datapoints (a Ginkgo Bioworks business line) announced a
partnership with Eli Lilly's TuneLab AI/ML drug-discovery network on
09-16, providing discovery-data generation services (small-molecule
ADME and antibody developability testing) to TuneLab member companies.
Stock reacted +11.05% on 09-17 (close $7.84). This is a real, dated,
company-specific announcement — not a rumor — though note a peer (Twist
Bioscience) also rose on a separate, related TuneLab agreement the same
day, so part of the reaction may reflect a shared "AI-drug-discovery
data providers" theme rather than being purely Ginkgo-specific. No
dollar figures for the deal's economic size were disclosed in any
source found.

## Sentiment
Mixed. The deal itself is a genuine positive data point for Ginkgo's
pivot toward AI-data/Datapoints revenue, but it lands against a
deteriorating fundamental backdrop: Q2 2026 revenue fell 48% YoY with a
wider net loss ($57M), the company is burning cash, and a recent analyst
action was a downgrade (TD Cowen, Buy→Hold) with price targets being
trimmed (~12% cut, implied fair value ~$14 — still above the current
$7.84, but from an analyst who just downgraded, not upgraded).
