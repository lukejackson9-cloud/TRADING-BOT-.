## Identity check
Aethlon Medical, Inc. (Nasdaq: AEMD) is a real, currently-operating
biotech (Hemopurifier medical-device platform), not a shell — but its
identity is in the process of changing entirely. On 2026-09-17 Aethlon
announced an all-stock reverse-merger with private biotech North
Immunology: the combined company will be renamed North Immunology,
Inc., trade under a NEW ticker (NRTX), be run by North's management,
and existing Aethlon shareholders will retain only ~4.75% of the
combined entity (North's shareholders/PIPE investors get ~95.25%).
Aethlon also executed a 1-for-5 reverse split in late July 2026, cutting
shares outstanding to roughly 650,000 pre-merger — an extremely thin
float. This is a classic reverse-merger/shell-conversion structure:
buying AEMD common stock today is effectively buying a claim on ~4.75%
of a still-private, pre-clinical biotech (North's lead asset NOR-101 is
an early-stage bispecific antibody for atopic dermatitis, not yet in
the clinic), wrapped in a legacy ticker that will stop existing once the
deal closes (expected Q1 2027) plus a CVR tied to Aethlon's old
Hemopurifier business.

## Catalyst
Real and dated: the merger announcement itself, 2026-09-17, is what
drove the reported +374% (some sources cite up to +471% intraday). This
is a genuine, company-specific, dated event — not a rumor.

## Sentiment
Speculative and skeptical despite the size of the move. H.C. Wainwright
reiterated a Neutral rating with only a $1.50 price target immediately
after the news — a striking disconnect from a stock trading near $6.78,
and gurufocus explicitly flagged "price-to-sales signals market
skepticism." The mechanics here (thin float post-split, ~95% dilution
of existing holders, ticker/company identity about to disappear
entirely) point to a speculative re-rating/gamma-driven pop on deal
news, not a fundamentals re-rating of Aethlon's actual (legacy,
struggling) business.

## Risks
- **Existing AEMD shareholders are being diluted to ~4.75% of the
  combined company** — the stock's current price does not represent
  "owning more of a strong business," it represents a shrinking claim
  on a new, unproven, pre-clinical drug candidate.
- Extremely thin float (~650K shares pre-merger post reverse-split) —
  exactly the mechanical setup (see BIAF, AEHL, VEEA, FCUV in
  lessons.md) that produces violent, unstable price action unrelated to
  fundamental value.
- Deal isn't closed (expected Q1 2027) — closing risk, PIPE financing
  risk, and shareholder-vote risk all remain between now and then.
- Reaction has already fired in full (up to +471% intraday already
  reported) — per lessons.md #1, buying after a catalyst has already
  been fully priced is a weaker entry, not a better one.
- Analyst target ($1.50) is a fraction of the current price, a strong
  signal the move has outrun anything resembling fundamental valuation.
