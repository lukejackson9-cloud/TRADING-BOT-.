## Bear case (from independent agent)
Summary: The approved indication's addressable market is genuinely tiny
— Sanfilippo type A is one of the rarest subtypes (US incidence ~0.26
per 100,000 births; independently corroborated via Orphanet/NORD data
in this review, though the bear's specific historical "153 diagnosed
patients" figure wasn't independently reproduced, the order-of-magnitude
rarity was confirmed) — a trickle-revenue product that can't plausibly
replace the profitability the Angelman failure cost the company.
Balance sheet is genuinely stressed: ~$436M cash against ~$97M/quarter
burn (~4.5 quarters runway), a stockholders' deficit that widened to

liabilities. This is an early-signal-to-Phase-3-failure pattern
(Angelman) that should raise real skepticism about platform-level risk
elsewhere in the pipeline, not just that one asset. Reimbursement risk
is real at a $3.95M price point, largely a Medicaid-covered pediatric
population. Sell-side has not reversed bearish (Goldman cut to $19 on
09-04, Evercore downgraded to in-line) as of available data.
Key sources: ir.ultragenyx.com, ojrd.biomedcentral.com (MPS
epidemiology), fool.com (Q2 2026 earnings transcript), STAT
News/FierceBiotech/Endpoints News, BioSpace/EMJ Reviews, GuruFocus/
