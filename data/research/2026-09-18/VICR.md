## Ticker identity
Vicor Corporation (NASDAQ: VICR) is a real, established operating
company — a power-module semiconductor manufacturer. Ordinary common
stock.

## Catalyst
On 2026-09-17 Vicor stock jumped ~18.5-18.8% on a cluster of real, dated
announcements: (1) acquisition of two large New Hampshire sites
(Merrimack + Hooksett, ~1M sq ft combined) to build ChiP Fab-2 and
Fab-3, nearly tripling domestic manufacturing capacity as the existing
Andover Fab-1 runs near full utilization; (2) a non-exclusive license of
Vicor's Vertical Power Delivery (VPD) technology to an unnamed "major AI
OEM," which secures royalty income even on modules a third party
supplies; (3) continuation of momentum from a new $150M share buyback
authorization announced 2026-09-11. Backlog reached $380M in Q2, up 26%
sequentially and 145% year-over-year — a real, quantified demand signal
behind the AI power-delivery growth story, not just sentiment.

## Sentiment
Bullish, well-corroborated across multiple independent outlets
(Benzinga, StocksToTrade, Timothy Sykes, Pomegra) all converging on the
same two announcements (fab expansion + VPD license) plus the earlier
buyback news.

## Risks
- **Unnamed counterparty**: the VPD license is to an unspecified "major
  AI OEM" — unlike GNRC's named Amazon deal, this can't be independently
  verified or sized, which is a real gap in an otherwise strong-sounding
  catalyst.
- **Already-extended run**: this is the second dated catalyst inside a
  week (buyback 09-11, fab+license 09-17), and one source separately
  described "a 200% stock surge" building over a longer window — this
  looks like a continuing, already-running momentum story rather than a
  fresh, first-reaction move, closer to the TEM overextension pattern
  than to a clean one-off (lessons.md #1).
- Capacity expansion (new fabs) is a multi-year buildout, not an
  immediate revenue event — the near-term stock move is pricing in
  future capacity before it exists.
- Power-semiconductor/AI-infrastructure names have shown sector-wide
  correlated moves elsewhere this project has tracked (lessons.md #3) —
  worth checking whether peers (e.g. other power-module or analog names)
  moved together the same day before treating this as purely
  company-specific; not confirmed either way in this pass.
