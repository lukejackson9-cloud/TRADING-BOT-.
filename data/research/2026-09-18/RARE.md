## Identity check
Real operating company (Ultragenyx Pharmaceutical Inc.), Nasdaq-listed

## Catalyst
FDA approved Ultragenyx's gene therapy Fayuvi (rebisufligene
etisparvovec) for pediatric Sanfilippo syndrome type A (MPS IIIA) —
the first-ever FDA-approved treatment for this fatal neurodegenerative
disease and Ultragenyx's second approved gene therapy overall. This is
a real, dated (approval reported 09-17, closing the session +12.58% at
$14.50), company-specific catalyst, not a rumor or an analyst reaction
to someone else's news. Multiple sell-side price-target hikes followed
same-day/same-week directly citing the approval: Canaccord $37→$39,
Cantor Fitzgerald $33→$39, Citi $31→$32, and a Wells Fargo note framing
the approval as unlocking a "long revenue tailwind."

## Sentiment
Bullish. Real regulatory milestone plus a cluster of analyst target
increases all citing the same event (not independent stories) — a
stronger form of confirmation than a single analyst action alone.

## Risks
- Sanfilippo syndrome type A is an ultra-rare pediatric indication —
  small addressable patient population, so near-term revenue impact is
  likely modest versus the size of the stock move; commercial ramp,
  pricing, and reimbursement for a one-time gene therapy are unproven.
- Company has a recent, well-documented history of binary trial failure
  in a different program (ASPIRE, Angelman syndrome, -44% on 09-03) —
  shows real platform-level clinical risk even though it doesn't
  directly touch this approved asset.
- GuruFocus's "68% undervalued" GF Value read should be treated with
  skepticism (per lessons.md #2, single-source valuation models can be
  noisy) rather than taken as confirmation on its own.
- Some source disagreement on exact FDA decision date (one report cites
  a scheduled 09-19 PDUFA date, others report the approval as already
  announced 09-17) — the reaction is clearly already priced into the
  09-17 close either way, so any residual "decision day" catalyst is
  resolved, not still pending.
