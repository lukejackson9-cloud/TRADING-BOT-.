## Catalyst

to Overweight, raising its price target from $4.00 to $8.00, citing the

possible approval by December 4, 2026. The +9.93% move on 09-18 is
continued momentum from that 09-16 upgrade, not a new dated development
of its own — no fresh 09-17/09-18-specific news was found beyond
continued trading commentary. The stock is already up ~47% over the
trailing 30 days per one source, meaning a meaningful part of the
reaction to the Barclays call has already played out.
