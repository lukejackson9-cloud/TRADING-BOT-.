five-year "Innovation Exemption" — a temporary regulatory pathway

gained a fresh, dated confirmation on 09-18: Citi initiated a Buy
rating on SECZ that same day. The stock rallied from the mid-$7s through
an intraday spike to ~$11.07 before closing 09-18 at $10.86 (+21.61%),
and continued higher into 09-19 per later reporting (~+22% further).
This is a genuine, company-specific (not sector-generic — SECZ is
repeatedly named individually, not just "tokenization stocks in
general") regulatory catalyst with a real, dated, incremental
confirmation two sessions later, not a one-day pop on a stale story.
