## Catalyst
Step 0 check: no prior mention of FAC in trade_ledger.md, lessons.md, or
watchlist.txt — first time this ticker has come up in this project.

Identity confirmed: FAC = Factorial Energy, Inc., a real Nasdaq-listed
operating company developing solid-state battery technology (FEST/
Solstice platforms), backed by In-Q-Tel and automakers (Mercedes-Benz,
Stellantis, Hyundai, Kia). Recently public (a warrant class, FACWW,

Real, dated, company-specific catalyst found: on 2026-09-17, Factorial
announced a partnership with Mitsui Kinzoku (a major Japanese materials
company) to scale manufacturing of its all-solid-state battery platform
using Mitsui Kinzoku's sulfide-based solid-electrolyte expertise. Stock
surged 14.4% that day on the news. The ticker's 09-18 print in this
batch (+10.62%, close $6.25) is a SECOND session of gains after the
initial pop, not the announcement day itself. Per lessons.md #1
("buying right after a big catalyst-driven gap tends to be a worse
entry"), this is a real catalyst but the reaction had already largely
fired the day before — by 09-18 the market had a full session to price
it in, and the stock was still climbing further into the move rather
than pulling back.

## Risks
- Two-session-old catalyst by the 09-18 print — classic "sell the news"
  setup per lessons.md #1; an entry today would be chasing an
  already-extended move, not catching it early.
- Recently public, small-cap, pre-commercial-scale solid-state battery
  company — partnerships/MOUs are a step toward revenue, not revenue
  itself; execution risk on scaling is real and multi-year.
- Warrant overhang (FACWW) typical of recent de-SPAC-style listings can
  add dilution/volatility risk.
