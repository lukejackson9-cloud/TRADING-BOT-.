## Company identity check
Confirmed: Surgery Partners, Inc., Nasdaq: SGRY, operator of surgical

## Catalyst
No fresh, dated, SGRY-specific news was found for the 2026-09-22 session
itself ($13.66 -> $15.80, +15.7%). The real underlying catalyst is the
Idaho Falls divestiture: Surgery Partners completed the sale of its
ownership interests in Mountain View Hospital and Idaho Falls Community
Hospital to Intermountain Health (~$797M gross / ~$587M net proceeds),
announced/completed on **2026-09-17** — five days before this move, not
today. That announcement already moved the stock at the time (accompanied
by updated FY2026 guidance reflecting the loss of Idaho Falls revenue,
a ~30bps leverage improvement, and reduced Medicaid payor exposure).
JPMorgan raised its price target to $15 (from $14) on 2026-09-14, also
before this move, and an analyst reiterated Buy with a $19 PT citing the
divestiture — again riding the same already-known transaction. The most
"today"-dated item found is a batch of 13F institutional-holdings
disclosures (Jupiter Topco, Pentwater Capital, BlackRock reported as Q2
buyers) — but 13F filings disclose QUARTER-OLD positions on a lag; a
filing hitting the news wire today is not the same as fresh buying
happening today, and doesn't constitute a new company-specific catalyst.
No SGRY-specific news dated 2026-09-22 was found beyond continued
market digestion of the 09-17 transaction.

## Sentiment
Bullish on the underlying story (balance-sheet deleveraging, reduced
Medicaid exposure, sharper surgical-facility focus, sell-side price
target increases) but this is the same "already fired" catalyst
continuing to be re-priced across multiple sessions rather than new
information arriving today. Fits the same pattern flagged in lessons.md
#1: buying several days after the actual catalyst (09-17) risks entering
an already-extended move rather than an early one, especially with the
stock climbing from a post-announcement dip (~$13.34 on 09-17) up through
$15.80 five days on.

## Risks
- Stale-catalyst / sell-the-news pattern (lessons.md #1): the real news
  is 5 trading days old; today's push has no new information behind it
  that could be verified.
- Rising interest expense and anesthesia-related cost pressure flagged
  as ongoing headwinds by multiple sources even amid the divestiture
  optimism.
- Stock is still down ~38.7% over the trailing year and trading well
  below its 52-week high ($23.44) — the "recovery" narrative is real but
  not yet validated by a sustained trend.
- 13F-driven "buying" headlines reference Q2 2026 positions, disclosed
  with a lag — not confirmation of current, ongoing institutional demand
  today.
