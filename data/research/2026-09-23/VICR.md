## Risks
- **Overextension (lessons.md #1)**: this is now the third consecutive
  dated catalyst event in the same story driving the stock up further
  from an already-extended base (+17.66% on 09-18, another +19.85% on
  09-22) — buying here means buying near the top of a multi-week run, not
  at the original catalyst.
- The AI-OEM licensee remains unnamed even after the guidance raise —
  the market is pricing in a partner whose identity, deal size, and
  durability are still not independently confirmed.
- Valuation has detached from any conventional fair-value estimate
  (GuruFocus: +293% above GF Value) even before this latest leg up.
- Heavy insider selling ($410.6M/12mo) sits uncomfortably next to a stock
  making new highs on unconfirmed-counterparty guidance.
