# JAGX — 2026-09-23

## Catalyst
Real but minor, heavily distorted by a corporate action. Jaguar Health
completed a 1-for-15 reverse stock split effective 09-17 (down to ~520,088
shares outstanding — an extremely thin float). On top of that, the FDA
waived 2027 user fees for two of its products (Mytesi and Canalevia-CA1),
easing near-term cash pressure — a real, dated, company-specific
regulatory item, but a fee waiver is a minor administrative relief, not a
approval/label/efficacy event.

## Sentiment
Extremely volatile, not genuinely bullish. The stock's headline "+1,190.6%
close" and "as much as +1585.4% intraday" moves on 09-22 are mechanically
inflated by the reverse split's drastically reduced share count on top of
the news, not a clean read of investor conviction. Confirms the read:
by 09-23 (today), the stock is already down -74.14% intraday per FMP's
live losers list — a round-trip crash that is the single clearest
sell-the-news/thin-float-noise pattern seen in this project to date.

## Risks
- **Reverse-split distortion**: percentage-change figures across the
  split date are not economically meaningful — the "+1190%" number
  reflects share-count mechanics as much as any news.
  Thin float post-split (~520,088 shares) means normal dollar volume
  produces extreme percentage swings in either direction.
- **Already crashed**: -74% intraday today confirms this was a blow-off,
  not a sustained re-rating — a textbook case for lessons.md going
  forward (this project has documented FTFT and other reverse-split churn
  before; JAGX is a cleaner, more extreme version of the same pattern).
- The actual catalyst (fee waivers) is minor relief, not the growth/
  approval story the price action implied.
