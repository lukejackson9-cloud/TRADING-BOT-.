# PATH — 2026-09-04

## Catalyst
UiPath reported Q2 FY2027 results on 2026-09-03 (after close). Headline numbers
beat: revenue of $410.3M topped the ~$397.6-397.8M consensus (up 13% YoY), and the
company posted its fourth consecutive quarter of GAAP profitability (operating
income $31.6M, adjusted EPS $0.15). The selloff was driven by forward guidance and
underlying growth-quality metrics: Q3 FY2027 revenue guidance of $440M-$445M implies
only ~8% YoY growth, roughly half the pace just delivered, and full-year revenue
guidance of $1.79B was seen as unimpressive. More importantly, Annualized Recurring
Revenue grew just 12% YoY to $1.938B, and net new ARR of $37M was a sequential
step-down from $49M in Q1 FY2027 — a leading indicator investors watch closely for
future subscription momentum. The stock fell ~14-17% intraday, notable because PATH
had rallied ~29% over the prior month heading into the print, so this also unwinds
recent momentum/expectations built into the stock.

## Sentiment
Bearish/skeptical, tilting more negative post-print. Canaccord downgraded PATH to
Hold from Buy (price target actually raised slightly to $17 from $15, but rating
cut), citing the stock's recent rally plus a "competitive environment that's only
getting more fluid" — i.e., a thesis-level concern about UiPath's competitive
position in AI-driven automation, not just one soft guide. A Seeking Alpha piece
explicitly argues the "SaaS recovery is overdone" and downgrades the rating. Average
analyst price target (~$13.53) is reported as running below even the post-drop
price (~$15-16), implying the sell-side, on balance, does not see this dip as a
buying opportunity in the near term despite a nominal 67% Buy-rating share (which
appears to reflect stale/pre-print ratings not yet updated with new price targets).

## Risks
- Deceleration is showing up in ARR and net-new-ARR — the core forward-looking
  subscription metrics — not just a conservative one-quarter guide. That is a
  harder story to dismiss as a market overreaction.
- Emerging competitive-positioning concern (Canaccord) in AI-native automation
  tooling is a thesis risk, not a one-time event; no fresh, findable catalyst
  suggests this reverses quickly.
- Stock had already rallied 29% in the month before earnings, so part of today's
  drop is simply giving back a expectations-driven run-up rather than a fresh
  overreaction to be faded.
- CFO transition (Deputy CFO Hitesh Ramani elevated to CFO) adds a small
  leadership-transition variable during a growth-scrutiny period.
- Average sell-side price target below current price suggests limited near-term
  analyst-side support for a bounce thesis.
