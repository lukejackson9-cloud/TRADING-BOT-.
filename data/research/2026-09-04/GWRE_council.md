# GWRE — Council Review — 2026-09-04

## Bull case (from independent agent)
Summary: Q4 FY26 beat both lines (revenue $411.1M vs ~$402.7M est., EPS
$0.99 vs ~$0.93 est.), attrition remains extremely low, and over half of
FY27's guided net-new ARR is already under signed contract with defined
ramp dates — an unusually de-risked guide, not a guess. Sell-side mostly
raised targets even as the stock fell (Wells Fargo to $195, Oppenheimer to
$210, Citi to $181), consensus ~$210 sits ~22% above the post-drop price.
RSI(14) at 21 is squarely oversold. A directly analogous precedent exists:
GWRE crashed on an near-identical "beat but ARR-guide-disappoints" pattern
in June 2026 and fully recovered within ~13 weeks.
Self-assessment: weak — "a speculative technical trade dressed in
fundamental language, not a strong fresh long thesis." The bull agent
itself surfaced the decisive counter-fact: this is the SAME playbook
repeating for a second consecutive quarter (ARR growth 19%→18% both times,
double-digit crash both times), which argues the market has learned to
punish this specific pattern rather than that today's drop is a one-off
overreaction. Zero insider buying across 19 sale transactions in the
trailing 12 months, still-rich valuation (P/S 12.3x vs ~11x 10-yr median,
forward P/E ~38x) even after the drop, and the June recovery took ~3
months, not two weeks.

## Bear case (from independent agent)
Summary: The FY27 guide-down isn't just conservatism — Guidewire's own CFO
said the outlook assumes attrition normalizes off the record-low level
achieved in FY26, a company-sourced admission that part of this year's
"beat" leaned on a churn tailwind management doesn't expect to repeat.
Even after the ~20% drop, GWRE trades at ~41.5x forward non-GAAP earnings
and ~98-109x trailing — a credentialed independent analyst (Seeking Alpha)
explicitly rates it Hold post-drop, saying it "lacks a margin of safety
for new positions given decelerating growth guidance." GWRE fell alongside
Asana and UiPath the same week amid a broader high-multiple software
repricing (IGV down ~22% from highs) — buying into that trend on a 1-2
week horizon bets on timing a sector bottom, not just GWRE's own print.
Insider activity is a mild negative (steady CEO 10b5-1 selling, zero
buying), though disclosed as routine, not a smoking gun.
Self-assessment: moderately strong — "a real caution case for a 1-2 week
long, not a slam-dunk short thesis." Explicitly found no evidence of a
structural business breakdown, competitive loss, or accounting issue.
Key sources: [CFO attrition-normalization admission — Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4943106-guidewire-why-a-15-percent-post-earnings-selloff-still-doesnt-make-it-a-buy), [Sector-wide software repricing — Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4640337-asana-guidewire-and-uipath-lead-enterprise-software-stocks-down-following-earnings), [Valuation — stockanalysis.com](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/gwre/statistics/)

## Moderator decision

and surfaced the decisive fact that kills it: this is the second time in
