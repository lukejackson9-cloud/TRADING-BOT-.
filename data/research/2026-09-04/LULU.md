# LULU — 2026-09-04

## Catalyst
Lululemon reported Q2 FY2026 earnings on 2026-09-03 (after close). Headline EPS beat
($2.92 vs. $2.51 est.) but revenue missed ($2.415B–$2.416B vs. ~$2.461B est.), and
comparable sales fell 9% (10% in constant currency), with leggings sales down 20%.
Management slashed full-year guidance sharply: revenue to $10.35B–$10.50B (a 5–7%
decline) from prior guidance of $11.00B–$11.15B (flat to down 1%), and EPS guidance
to $9.48–$9.73 from $10.95–$11.15. The shortfall was concentrated in China Mainland,
and interim co-CEO Meghan Frank flagged "negative commentary" on social media as a
headwind during the quarter. Stock fell ~17–18% on the day, hitting an 8-year low,
days ahead of a new CEO starting.

## Sentiment
Bearish, and moving further bearish post-print. At least five sell-side desks cut
price targets same-day: JPMorgan ($154→$95), Morgan Stanley ($93→$83, Underweight),
Wells Fargo ($105→$95), Bernstein ($145→$115, downgraded to Market Perform), Stifel
(to $100). Consensus rating has shifted to "Hold" (21 analysts). No credible bull
case or "overreaction" argument surfaced in search results — commentary frames this
as a second consecutive disappointing quarter and a structural deceleration, not a
one-off miss.

## Risks
- This looks like a genuine fundamental deterioration (comp sales down for multiple
  quarters, guidance cut twice in a row), not a market overreaction to otherwise
  solid results — the classic setup where "buying the dip" is a falling-knife trap.
  This bot is long-only, so a purely negative catalyst with no bounce thesis is not
  tradeable regardless of magnitude.
- New CEO transition adds leadership uncertainty layered on top of the operational
  miss.
- China Mainland exposure and unclear resolution of the "negative social media
  commentary" issue (brand/reputational, not just macro).
- Stock is down 52.7% YTD — a name in a well-established downtrend, not a stable
  base being tested.
- Some price targets (e.g., Wells Fargo $95, JPM $95) are now roughly at or below
  the post-drop price ($99.67), meaning even "reduced" targets don't imply much
  near-term upside.
