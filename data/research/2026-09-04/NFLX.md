# NFLX — 2026-09-04

## Catalyst
Sources are inconsistent on the exact magnitude (one source shows -3.70%
intraday move quoted mid-session at ~$82.67, vs. the screen's -5.4% for the
full session — treating the screen's close-of-session figure as
authoritative here since it's the more recent read). Drivers found:
continued fallout from Q2 guidance that undercut Wall Street's revenue
estimate, forward Q3 revenue guidance again coming in slightly short of
consensus, technical weakness (trading below both 20-day and 200-day
moving averages), and some profit-taking after a recent bounce off lows.
The stock is also still digesting the scrapped Warner Bros Discovery bid
from earlier this year. Broader market was soft today (S&P -0.38%,
Nasdaq -0.29%) but NFLX's decline is well beyond that, so this is
stock-specific, not just beta.

## Sentiment
Mixed-to-bearish near-term, but with a notable disconnect: Wall Street
still carries a "Buy" consensus (51 analysts) with a $93.66 mean target,
about 13% above the last quoted price — i.e. sell-side hasn't capitulated
even as the stock has fallen ~33% over the past year. That's a real bull
data point, but stale guidance-miss narratives repeating quarter over
quarter is a genuine pattern, not noise.

## Risks
- Growth deceleration is now a recurring theme (multiple quarters of
  light guidance), which argues against treating today as an isolated
  overreaction.
- Mega-cap, highly liquid — a -5%+ day is a large move for this name and
  reflects a real re-rating of growth expectations, not illiquidity/thin
  float distortion.
- No specific near-term (days) catalyst identified that would reverse
  today's move — analyst price targets are a 12-month view, not a
  short-term signal.
- Conflicting figures in press between -3.7% and -5.4% for the same day
  make it hard to be fully confident in the exact scale of the move from
  open-web sources alone.
