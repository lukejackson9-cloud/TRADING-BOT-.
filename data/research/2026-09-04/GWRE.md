# GWRE — 2026-09-04

## Catalyst
Guidewire reported fiscal Q4/FY2026 results (quarter ended July 31, 2026) after the
close on 2026-09-03. The print itself was strong: revenue of $411.1M beat the
$402.7M estimate (up 15% YoY), adjusted EPS of $0.99 beat the $0.93 consensus,
Annual Recurring Revenue reached $1.242B (+19% YoY), Cloud ARR grew 35% and now
represents 84% of total ARR, and gross ARR attrition hit a record low (<1.5%
overall, <1% for core systems customers). The stock nonetheless sold off ~15% after
hours and continued falling into today's session (down ~20-22% intraday, as low as
$157.05) because fiscal Q1 FY2027 revenue guidance ($372M–$378M) came in about 3.3%
below consensus at the midpoint — a guide-down on an otherwise beat-and-raise-shaped
quarter. A stronger-than-expected U.S. jobs report the same morning added a
macro/rate-driven headwind for high-multiple software names generally, which
compounded the move.

## Sentiment
Mixed-to-cautiously-bullish among sell-side, bearish in immediate price action.
Price targets were cut across the board (DA Davidson to $222, RBC to $215, Raymond
James to $180, Stifel to $200) but the search results indicate most analysts kept
Buy/Outperform ratings rather than downgrading the name outright — i.e., this reads
as a valuation reset on a single conservative quarterly guide, not a broad thesis
change. Coverage explicitly frames this as a "beat and guide-down" and asks whether
the ~15-20% drop is an overreaction; some technical commentary noted dip-buying
interest building in the low $100s intraday. Countervailing bearish view (Seeking
Alpha) argues the stock was priced at ~41.5x forward non-GAAP earnings (120x+
trailing) even before the drop, so a "quality compounder" argument doesn't
necessarily mean the post-drop price is cheap.

## Risks
- Valuation was extreme heading into the print (120x+ trailing P/E), so even after
  a ~20% cut the stock may still not be statistically cheap — multiple compression
  in richly-valued software names can continue well past the first down day,
  especially with a rates-driven macro overhang.
- The guidance miss is a real deceleration signal on the metric (ARR/forward
  revenue) markets care most about for a subscription business, not just headline
  noise.
- Thin datapoint: only one earnings reaction to judge "bounce" behavior against;
  no confirmed reversal/basing pattern yet, only "hinted at" dip-buying.
- Rate-sensitivity risk is macro, not company-specific — a further hawkish
  Fed repricing could keep pressuring the stock regardless of Guidewire's own
  execution.
