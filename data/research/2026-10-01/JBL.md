## Identity check
Confirmed: JBL = Jabil Inc. (NYSE), the electronics manufacturing
services (EMS) company. No collision risk found.

## Catalyst
Real and dated: Jabil reported Q4 FY2026 results and FY2027 guidance on
2026-09-30. Results were genuinely strong — revenue up ~28% YoY to $10.6B
(vs. $9.7B consensus), beating on both revenue and adjusted EPS, with an
optimistic FY2027 outlook. Despite this, the stock fell ~10% the same day
(screener: -10.03%, $318.84 -> $286.86).

## Sentiment
Bearish price action on genuinely good fundamental news — a classic
"sell the news" reaction. Multiple sources frame this as profit-taking
after a large run-up into the print rather than any flaw in the actual
results; one Seeking Alpha piece explicitly calls the plunge "a gift for
value investors," i.e. a view that the drop is sentiment/valuation-driven
rather than fundamentals-driven.

## Risks
- Valuation: P/E (TTM) of 35.8x vs. a 5-year median of 14.4x — the stock
  was priced for perfection heading into the print, which is consistent
  with a reaction that fades a beat-and-raise.
- Heavy insider selling over the trailing 12 months (~$79.8M sold vs.
  ~$0.1M bought) — management has not been accumulating into strength.
- This project is long-only with no shorting capability (Hard Risk
  Rules) — a negative-reaction mover like this has no actionable long
  setup regardless of how good the underlying quarter actually was,
  unless a case can be made for a dip-buy, which the valuation and
  insider-selling data argue against here.
