## Identity check
CarMax, Inc. (NYSE: KMX) — a real used-vehicle retailer (also runs
CarMax Auto Finance, CAF). Confirmed real, not a collision.

## Catalyst
Real and dated: CarMax reported Q2 FY2027 results on 2026-09-29
(after/around market hours), with the stock reacting on 2026-09-30,
closing at $55.11 (-6.96% from $59.23). Headline numbers were a clear
beat — EPS $1.16 vs. $0.73 consensus (+59%), revenue $7.88B vs. ~$7.09B
consensus (+19.5% YoY), combined retail/wholesale unit sales +14.7%,
comp sales +13.0%. This is the same "good headline, soft underneath"
shape as HPE/GWRE/ASO in the trade ledger: the stock opened sharply
higher ($59.14, intraday high $59.65) before reversing hard once the
market digested the details:
- Used retail gross profit per unit (GPU) fell to $2,105, down $111 YoY.
- Wholesale GPU fell to $858, down $135 YoY (wholesale margin dollars
  held flat only via higher unit volume).
- Management still guides full-year retail GPU DOWN year-over-year in
  H2 (an improvement on the prior guide, but still a decline, not a
  reversal).
- CAF income (+32% to $136M) was flattered by a one-time $17M gain on a
  non-prime securitization sale plus a $29M loan-loss-provision
  reduction — not purely organic strength.

## Sentiment
Mixed-to-bearish for the immediate reaction, despite constructive
longer-term analyst commentary (Truist raised its target to $60/Hold;
RBC and Baird reportedly see upside; management flagged a Nov. 3
strategic update and a resumed buyback). The market's same-day verdict
was clearly negative — this is a sell-the-beat day on margin-quality
concerns, not a dip to be bought on the headline numbers alone.

## Risks
- This is a decline, not a breakout — no long thesis fits a down day
  driven by real, structural per-unit margin erosion.
- The margin erosion (GPU down YoY on both retail and wholesale) is a
  genuine, company-disclosed trend, not a one-off — management itself
  guides it continuing into H2, just at a smaller magnitude.
- CAF's income improvement leans on non-recurring items (securitization
  gain, loss-provision release), overstating the quality of that
  segment's underlying run-rate.
- Next scheduled catalyst (Nov. 3 strategic update) is outside this
  project's ~2-week trading horizon.
