## Catalyst
Real and dated, but the reaction is the opposite of what the data itself
would suggest: Q32 Bio presented extended 36-week results (plus initial
16-week off-drug observation) from Part B of its SIGNAL-AA Phase 2a trial
of bempikibart in severe alopecia areata, in a late-breaker at the EADV
(European Academy of Dermatology and Venereology) Congress 2026 on
2026-09-30. Data itself reads as genuinely positive: 33 patients with
severe/very severe alopecia areata, mean 35.3% reduction in SALT
(Severity of Alopecia Tool) score at 36 weeks, 40% achieving a SALT-20
response (≥20% scalp hair regrowth), and — notably — durable or even
deepening response during the 16-week off-drug follow-up, which
differentiates it from JAK inhibitors (which typically require
continuous dosing). Oppenheimer reiterated its rating citing "durable
trial data."

## Sentiment
This is a clean sell-the-news case, not an ambiguous one. The stock
initially spiked pre-market on the release, then fully reversed intraday
to close down 13.2% (Nasdaq halted it for volatility at the open),
despite the Nasdaq Biotechnology Index rising ~0.5% the same session —
i.e. this is QTTB-specific disappointment, not sector drag. The honest
read: the market had priced in an even stronger readout (the stock had
already run up into this data, including triple-digit analyst-initiation
coverage — Clear Street, Jefferies, Morgan Stanley Overweight — in the
prior weeks), and "good but not great enough" data triggered a classic
expectations-reset selloff.

## Risks
Small clinical-stage biotech, single-indication readout dependency,
thin trial size (n=33 in the specific cohort cited). The catalyst here is
real and company-specific, but it already fired and the market's verdict
on it (a sharp same-day reversal) was negative — this is the "catalyst
already resolved, badly" case, not an unresolved opportunity. No edge in
chasing either direction post-reversal in a 2-week window.
