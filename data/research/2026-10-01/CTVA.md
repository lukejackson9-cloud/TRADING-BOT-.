## Data-source discrepancy resolved
Massive.com showed CTVA basically flat (-0.28%, $77.87 -> $77.65 for
09-29/09-30), while FMP's live losers list showed -83.81% to $12.57.
Investigated: this is a spin-off basis artifact, not a crash. Per
Corteva's own SEC 8-K filings, Corteva spun off its seed business as a
new independent company, Vylor (NYSE: VYLR), effective 2026-10-01 --
each CTVA shareholder of record (as of 2026-09-24) received 1 VYLR share
per CTVA share held, with no "when-issued"/"ex-distribution" trading
beforehand, so the entire value transfer happened in one day with no
gradual lead-in. $77.65 (pre-spin combined-entity close) vs. ~$12.57-
$12.75 (post-spin, crop-protection-only residual price) is exactly this
effect, not a literal stock split and not a real decline. FMP's $12.57 is
the correct current price for the real (now smaller) post-spin company.
