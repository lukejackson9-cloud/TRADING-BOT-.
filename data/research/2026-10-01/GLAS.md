## Catalyst — sector-wide negative, not GLAS-specific (correlation flag)
On 2026-09-30 (the exact session covered by this move), DEA Chief
Administrative Law Judge Derek Julius suspended hearings on the federal
marijuana rescheduling (Schedule III) proceeding, after opponents moved
to admit a 2026-09-23 GAO report flagging "gaps" in the record. This hit
the whole cannabis sector same-day/after-hours: Trulieve (TRLV, also in
today's batch) fell ~14-15% on the news and the AdvisorShares Pure US
Cannabis ETF (MSOS) fell as much as ~8% after hours. Schedule III
reclassification is the single biggest overhang/catalyst for the entire
multi-state-operator cannabis sector because it would eliminate the
Section 280E tax penalty — pausing it is bad news for every cannabis
name simultaneously, not a GLAS-specific event.

Direct confirmation of GLAS's own move tying to this exact news wasn't
