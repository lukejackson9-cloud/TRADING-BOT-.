## Catalyst
Real and dated, two compounding negative drivers, both sector-level or
already-fired:
1. **DEA rescheduling pause (primary driver)**: on 2026-09-30, DEA Chief
   Administrative Law Judge Derek Julius suspended hearings on the
   federal marijuana rescheduling (Schedule III) proceeding, after
   opponents moved to admit a 2026-09-23 GAO report flagging "gaps" in
   the record. Schedule III reclassification — which would eliminate the
   Section 280E tax penalty, the single biggest overhang on
   profitability for every U.S. cannabis multi-state operator — is now
   delayed indefinitely (DEA has until 2026-10-13 to say whether it will
   even reopen the hearing record). This hit the whole sector same-day:
   TRLV fell ~14%, and the AdvisorShares Pure US Cannabis ETF (MSOS) fell
   as much as ~8% after-hours. This is sector-wide, not TRLV-specific —
   see GLAS.md for the same-day correlated move in a different cannabis
   name.
2. **Sell-the-news on Monday's (09-28/09-29) own deal**: a secondary
   driver was a "sell-the-news" reaction to TRLV's own exclusive master
   licensing agreement with Connected Cannabis (Alien Labs/Connected
   brands, Florida+Texas), announced a day or two earlier — the market
   had already given credit for that real, company-specific positive
   news before today's DEA-driven reversal erased it and then some.

## Sentiment
