## Catalyst
Real, dated, negative, and multi-part — a cluster of same-week factors
resolving bearish on 2026-09-30:
1. **"Sell the news" on the AI credit facility**: Hut 8 recently
   announced a $1.07B senior secured revolving credit facility for AI
   data-center expansion; the market is unwinding the initial enthusiasm
   around it rather than extending it.
2. **Insider Form 144 filing, dated night of 2026-09-29/filed 09-30**:
   officer/director Michael Ho disclosed a planned sale of 1.5M shares
   (~$139M). **Important nuance, verified**: CEO Asher Genoot publicly
   clarified same-day that this is NOT an open-market sale — the shares
   are collateral under a prepaid forward contract tied to a borrowing
   arrangement (Springtide Creek Ltd., an entity Ho owns), and that Ho
   "has not sold any shares since we started the company." This softens,
   but does not eliminate, the signaling risk — it was still disclosed
   as a planned sale under Rule 144 and the market reacted to the
   filing before the clarification circulated.
3. **Unresolved Q2 2026 earnings overhang**: Hut 8 posted a $1.27/share
   loss vs. a $0.55 consensus loss, with revenue of $74.9M missing the
   $79.4M estimate — a real, still-digesting profitability concern.
4. Technical confirmation: trading below both its 20-day ($93.90) and
   50-day ($93.38) moving averages.

## Sentiment
