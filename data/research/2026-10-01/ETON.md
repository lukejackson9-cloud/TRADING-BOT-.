## Catalyst
Real, but already fired several sessions ago — today's move is the
unwind, not the event. Eton's stock had already spiked ~39% over a
two-day period in late September on genuine fundamental news: record Q2
2026 revenue of $37.6M (+99% YoY) and a raised FY2026 revenue guidance to
over $145M, plus commercial momentum from relaunching IMPAVIDO (rare
leishmaniasis treatment, U.S. availability announced 2026-09-28) and an
FDA Fast Track designation for AMGLIDIA (endocrinology). Today's -13.01%
move ($59.19 → $51.49) is explicitly characterized by coverage as
"valuation concerns and profit-taking following a massive run-up," not a
new negative catalyst — no earnings miss, no safety signal, no guidance
cut found.

## Sentiment
Still constructive on fundamentals even after today's pullback: H.C.
Wainwright raised its price target to $70 (from $65, Buy rating) and
average analyst target sits at $71 (~19% implied upside from the
post-pullback price) — i.e. sell-side hasn't reversed its view, this
reads as a technical/valuation pullback after a parabolic run, not a
thesis change.

## Risks
Classic overextension risk: the prior +39% two-day spike plus today's
reversal is the exact shape lessons.md #1 describes (a real catalyst
whose price reaction has already largely played out before a research
pass catches it). Chasing either the original pop or today's dip inside
a 2-week window has no demonstrated edge per this project's own
backtesting and discretionary history. Small/micro-cap commercial-stage
pharma also carries ordinary single-product concentration risk
(IMPAVIDO/AMGLIDIA-dependent revenue).
