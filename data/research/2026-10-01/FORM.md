## Identity check
Confirmed: FORM = FormFactor, Inc. (NASDAQ), maker of semiconductor
probe cards and test equipment. No collision risk found.

## Catalyst
Real and dated: Deutsche Bank (analyst George Brown) initiated coverage
on FORM on 2026-09-30 with a Buy rating and $200 price target, citing
FormFactor's new position as a second qualified source of probe cards for
Nvidia GPUs manufactured at TSMC (an incremental ~$0.5B TAM, comparable
to FormFactor's entire 2026 logic probe-card revenue base), plus a
doubling HBM-testing TAM by 2028. Stock closed +9.59% to $149.31, near
its 52-week high of $160.27. FORM also co-hosted the CEO Investor Summit
during SEMICON West the same week, adding visibility.

## Sentiment
Bullish, and the CNBC "biggest midday movers" list groups FORM alongside
ROG/UTHR/MRNA — a genuinely mixed set of sectors, not a single sector
story, reinforcing that this is company-specific today rather than a
repeat of the 09-08 AI-infra cluster.

## Risks
- Already extended: the stock is within ~7% of its 52-week high after
  this pop — buying right after a large gap is, per lessons.md #1, often
  a worse entry than it looks, even on genuinely good news.
- Single-analyst-driven (lessons.md #5 caution) — one firm's initiation,
  however well-reasoned, is not yet confirmed by other coverage or by a
  company disclosure/contract announcement of its own.
- CEO Mike Slessor sold ~$1.83M of shares around this move (he retains
  ~466,694 shares, so this is a partial sale, not a full exit, but it is
  insider selling into strength, not buying).
