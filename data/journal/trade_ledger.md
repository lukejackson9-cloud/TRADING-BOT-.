# Trade Ledger

One row per ticker-decision, chronological. This is the primary record
skills/journal.md reads and updates — every research.md verdict and
council.md review lands here, plus the actual outcome once it's known.
Purpose: a single place to prompt against and reflect on before forming a
fresh opinion on a new ticker, not a scattered pile of per-ticker files.

**How to read the Outcome column**: PENDING means the verdict was given
but not enough time/data exists yet to know if it was right — most
entries below are PENDING as of 2026-09-09, since the real outcome-check
pass is scheduled for 2026-09-11 (see HANDOFF.md/CLAUDE.md). RESOLVED
means an actual price/event outcome has been checked against a real data
source (Massive.com OHLCV preferred over WebSearch summaries — see
lessons.md's #2). Never mark something RESOLVED without a real check.

**Full detail for every CANDIDATE/council-reviewed ticker** (the trades
that actually mattered) is below in narrative form. **Every other
researched ticker** (WATCH/PASS from research.md alone, never reached
council) is in the compact appendix table at the bottom — still logged,
per the user's "every single trade" ask, just without the extra narrative
depth since these never became real trade candidates.
